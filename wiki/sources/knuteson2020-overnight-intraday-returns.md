# Knuteson 2020 — Strikingly Suspicious Overnight and Intraday Returns

Part of a series (2010.01727, 2107.12516, 2201.00223) documenting the overnight-positive / intraday-negative return split across world indices over decades.

**Use the data, not the theory.** The return decomposition is replicated by mainstream work ([[glasserman2025-overnight-news]], [[boyarchenko-larsen-whelan-overnight-drift]], Cooper-Cliff-Gulen 2008, Lou-Polk-Skouras 2019). Knuteson's manipulation explanation is heterodox; the mainstream candidates are dealer inventory compensation, news timing, and retail order-timing behavior. For the [[Overnight-Drift]] concept and the PRD, the decomposition's robustness across explanations is precisely what makes it the strongest structural effect on the menu — it does not depend on any one theory being right.
