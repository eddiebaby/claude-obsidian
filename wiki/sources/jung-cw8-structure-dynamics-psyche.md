## Overview

CW 8 collects the essays where Jung lays out his **dynamic models of the psyche** — how psychic energy works, how the conscious/unconscious system regulates itself, how dreams function, and how meaning can connect events acausally (synchronicity). Where [[jung-cw5-symbols-transformation|CW 5]] supplies the *symbol corpus* and the mythological method, CW 8 supplies the **theory of why any of it works**: the energic standpoint that makes a symbol a real psychological force rather than a decoration.

For the dream-symbol project, this is the **theoretical backbone**. It grounds three things the symbol pages assert but do not justify: (1) why symbols attract and transform libido, (2) why dreams compensate the conscious attitude, and (3) why the dream has a dramatic structure. All three are stated here in Jung's own words with stable paragraph numbers.

## Central Arguments

1. **The psyche is an energic system** (¶1–59). Psychic energy ("libido") is general life-energy, not Freud's sexuality — "a complete X, a pure hypothesis, a model or counter" (¶57). It is read two ways: the *causal* view asks "Why does it happen?", the *final* view "To what purpose?" (¶530). The hinge claim: **"What to the causal view is *fact* to the final view is *symbol*"** (¶45).

2. **Symbols transform energy** (¶46–47). "Psychic development cannot be accomplished by intention and will alone; it needs the attraction of the symbol, whose value quantum exceeds that of the cause." The symbol is the device by which dammed-up libido is canalized into new (cultural, spiritual, developmental) form.

3. **The psyche self-regulates by tension of opposites** (¶48–68). Equivalence + entropy: "The greater the tension between the pairs of opposites, the greater will be the energy that comes from them" (¶49–50). Progression = adaptation advancing; regression = libido damming and flowing backward, which activates the unconscious — its "slime" carries "germs of new life" (¶63). "Regression leads to the necessity of adapting to the inner world of the psyche" (¶66).

4. **Dreams compensate the conscious attitude** (¶545–546). "When I attempted to express this behaviour in a formula, the concept of *compensation* seemed to me the only adequate one." A dream-*series* reveals a directed plan — "the individuation process" (¶550).

5. **Dreams have a dramatic structure** (¶561–565): exposition → development → culmination/peripeteia → lysis. This is the **primary source** for the [[Dream Dramatic Structure]] page.

6. **Meaning can connect events acausally** (¶816–968). Synchronicity = "meaningful coincidence of two or more events, where something other than the probability of chance is involved" (¶969); the *tertium comparationis* is the archetype as "the introspectively recognizable form of *a priori* psychic orderedness" (¶965).
