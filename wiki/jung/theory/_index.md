# Jung's Theory of the Psyche — CW 8

Navigation: [[index]] | [[concepts/_index]] | [[jung/symbols/_index|Symbols (CW 5)]]

**Source**: [[jung-cw8-structure-dynamics-psyche]] (CW Vol. 8) — *The Structure and Dynamics of the Psyche*

The theoretical backbone for the dream-symbol project. Where the [[jung/symbols/_index|CW 5 symbol corpus]] supplies *what* the symbols are, CW 8 supplies *why they work*. Three documents:

1. **[[CW8 Theoretical Foundations]]** — the machinery: psychic energy/libido, causal vs. final standpoint, entropy & tension of opposites, progression/regression, symbol formation, the complex, archetype/instinct, the Self & individuation, synchronicity.
2. **[[CW8 Dream Methodology]]** — the procedure in Jung's words: compensation, the interpretive rules, "taking up the context," the four-phase dramatic structure, the dream-series and "big" dreams.
3. **[[CW8 Integration Framework]]** — the wiring: how CW 8 theory grounds the CW 5 symbols and von Franz's craft; thematic symbol bundles; the second-half-of-life shift; one unified loop.

The operational form of all three is the runnable procedure [[Working with Dream Symbols]].
