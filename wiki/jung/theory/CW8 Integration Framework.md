# CW 8 — Integration Framework

> [!abstract] Document 3 of 3
> The **wiring diagram**. Three sources, one method. [[jung-cw8-structure-dynamics-psyche|CW 8]] supplies the *theory* (why symbols work), [[jung-cw5-symbols-transformation|CW 5]] supplies the *corpus* (what the symbols are), and [[vonfranz-dreams-1998|von Franz]] supplies the *clinical craft* (how it plays out in real cases). This document shows how they lock together and feed the runnable procedure in [[Working with Dream Symbols]].

---

## 1. The division of labor

| Source | Supplies | Role in the method |
|---|---|---|
| **CW 8** (theory) | The energic model: libido, compensation, symbol formation, dramatic structure, synchronicity | *Why* the procedure works — the engine spec |
| **CW 5** (corpus) | ~75 transformation symbols with cross-cultural amplifications; the hero-myth pattern | *What* gets consulted at the archetypal step — the lookup |
| **von Franz** (craft) | Case-based dream dynamics; the two-column association method; the felt sense of "little" vs "big" | *How* it goes in practice — the bedside technique |

No single source is sufficient. CW 5 without CW 8 is a symbol dictionary (the failure mode the method exists to prevent). CW 8 without CW 5 is theory with nothing to interpret. von Franz without either is craft without a backbone.

---

## 2. How CW 8 theory grounds the CW 5 symbols

Each thing the symbol pages *assert*, CW 8 *justifies*:

| CW 5 symbol page asserts… | CW 8 grounds it via… |
|---|---|
| A symbol carries energy / "charge" | Libido is general life-energy ([[CW8 Theoretical Foundations]] §1); the symbol's "value quantum exceeds that of the cause" (¶47) |
| Symbols are bridges, not disguises | "What to the causal view is *fact* to the final view is *symbol*" (¶45) — the final standpoint *is* the symbolic one |
| Every symbol is double (e.g. [[Dragon]] guardian/devourer) | Energy comes from the **tension of opposites** (¶49–50); the polarity is the power source, not a contradiction |
| The hero "regresses to the mother" to be reborn | Progression/regression (¶60–66): regression activates the unconscious's "germs of new life" (¶63) and forces adaptation to the inner world |
| Transformation symbols (fire, sun, sacrifice) | Symbol formation (¶47): dammed libido canalized by analogy into developmental form |
| Symbols recur across cultures | Archetype as "the introspectively recognizable form of *a priori* psychic orderedness" (¶965) |

**The bridge case:** the ¶555 dream — *a snake guarding a golden bowl in an underground vault* — is CW 8 explicitly reaching into the CW 5 corpus. Jung says the personal context fails to explain the dream's emotionality, so "we have to go back to mythology, where the combination of snake or dragon with treasure and cave represents an ordeal in the life of the hero" (¶555). That single move *is* the integration: CW 8's method invoking CW 5's symbols ([[Serpent]]/[[Dragon]] + [[Treasure]] + [[Cave]]) under a strict gate.

## 3. How CW 8 theory explains von Franz's dream dynamics

| von Franz technique | CW 8 grounding |
|---|---|
| Two-column method: dream text left, associations right | "Taking up the context" (¶542) — meaning fixed by the dreamer's own associations, deciphered "as a difficult text" |
| Read the dream as a little drama with a turning point | The four-phase dramatic structure (¶561–565); the lysis carries the compensation |
| Distinguish trivial from fate-laden dreams | "Little" vs. "big" dreams (¶554); big dreams at life's "critical phases" (¶555) |
| Watch the whole series, not the single dream | The series as a directed plan = "the individuation process" (¶550) |
| Stay with the image; resist premature meaning | Start from "I have no idea what this dream means" (¶533); no stereotyped interpretation (¶543) |

von Franz is CW 8's method practiced at the bedside; CW 8 is the theory her cases instantiate.

## 4. The unified model — one loop, three sources feeding it

The procedure lives in [[Working with Dream Symbols]]. Here is which source powers each step:

```
Step 0  Segment the dream (4-act drama)      ← CW 8 ¶561–565
Step 1  Mark charged images                  ← CW 8 §6 complex/affect; von Franz "big" feel
Step 2  Personal associations (first)        ← CW 8 ¶542 "taking up the context"; von Franz 2-column
Step 3  Cultural associations                ← Amplification layer 2
        └─ still charged? ────────────────── gate
Step 4  Archetypal amplification             ← CW 5 corpus (the ¶555 move), licensed by CW 8 §5,§7
Step 5  Compensation / completion            ← CW 8 ¶545–546 (and Roesler's completion caveat)
Step 6  Dream-ego agency across the series   ← CW 8 ¶550 + Structural Dream Analysis
Step 7  Hold open → Active Imagination       ← CW 8 The Transcendent Function (¶131–193)
```

**The gate (Step 3→4) is the whole game.** CW 8 both *licenses* archetypal amplification (the archetype is real, active, energy-bearing — §5, §7) and *restricts* it (no stereotyped interpretation, ¶543; meaning fixed by the dreamer, ¶533). The CW 5 corpus is consulted only when the dreamer's own associations are exhausted and charge remains — exactly the ¶555 condition.

## 6. Second-half-of-life shift

CW 8 (*The Stages of Life*, ¶749–795 — synthesized; with ¶555–557 directly read) anchors the developmental axis the symbols ride. The famous figure: life as the **sun's course** — morning ascent (ego-building, adaptation to the outer world) vs. afternoon descent (turning inward, meaning, individuation). "At about thirty-six he passes the zenith of life" (¶556). The same symbol shifts valence across this divide: in the first half, the hero *slays* the dragon (builds an ego, separates from the mother); in the second, he must *descend* and integrate what he conquered. The "big" dreams cluster precisely at the transitions — youth, puberty, the onset of middle age, and "within sight of death" (¶555). For dream work this means: **read the symbol against the dreamer's life-stage**, because the unconscious's compensatory aim reverses across the zenith.

---

## 7. Where synchronicity fits

The acausal principle (§9 of [[CW8 Theoretical Foundations]]) is the outer boundary of the model: it licenses taking *waking coincidences around a dream* as part of the same archetypal constellation (the [[CW8 Theoretical Foundations|golden scarab]] case), while warning that such coincidences are *contingent*, not causal "signs." Practically: note them, weight them lightly, never let them override the dreamer's associations.
