# Moon (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The moon represents the feminine principle, emotional receptivity, and cyclical rhythms. Associated with the night, the psyche, and the unconscious. Mother principle in night form.

## Key Meanings

- Feminine principle
- Emotional receptivity
- Cycles and rhythms
- Night consciousness
- Psyche and dreams
- Tides and flow

## Mythological Context
