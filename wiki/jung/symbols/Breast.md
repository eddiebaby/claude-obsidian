# Breast (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The breast is the mother symbol expressing nurture, comfort, and life-giving. Represents maternal love, sensuality, and the source of sustenance. Both comforting and potentially devouring.

## Key Meanings

- Mother and nurturing
- Life-giving nourishment
- Comfort and safety
- Feminine/maternal principle
- Sensuality and desire
- Agape (divine love)

## Symbolic Associations

- "Mama" — primal mother
- Breast-beating — ritual mourning
- Drink of immortality from breast
- Arrow-shot breasts — vulnerable heart
- Exposed breasts — vulnerability

## Psychological Meaning

- Dependency and security
- Maternal bonding
- Nourishment from the mother
- Source of life
- Comfort principle

## Dream Context

Breast dreams signal:
- Need for maternal comfort
- Dependency issues
- Sensuality and desire
- Nourishment seeking
- Mother connection
