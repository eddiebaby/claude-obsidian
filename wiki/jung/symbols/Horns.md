# Horns (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Horns represent animal instinct, raw power, and the demonic or devil principle. Two-horned symbolism common. Horns appear on demons, devils, and creatures of instinct. The aggressive phallic power that civilized consciousness has repressed.

## Key Meanings

- Animal instinct
- Raw power
- Demonic force
- Phallic aggression
- Repressed nature
- Devil's mark

## Mythological Context

- Devil with horns
- Satyr horns
- Goat symbolism
- Sacred animal horns
- Aggressive power

## Psychological Meaning

- Instinctual aggression
- Repressed animal nature
- Power not yet integrated
- Shadow aggression
- Phallic unconscious

## Dream Context

Horns dreams signal:
- Animal instinct rising

- Devil/shadow appearing
- Repressed nature emerging
- Power confronted
