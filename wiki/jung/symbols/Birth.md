# Birth (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Birth is the symbol of emergence from unconsciousness into consciousness, from potential into actuality. Represents new psychological development, the birth of consciousness, and creative emergence.

## Key Meanings

- Emergence into consciousness
- Creation and genesis
- New psychological beginning
- Birth of the divine child
- Separation from mother
- Theories of psychological birth

## Types

- **Birth with caul** — protected/fated
- **Anal interest** — pleasure in creating
- **Birth goddess** — Bes, childbirth deities
- **Tear from womb** — traumatic separation
- **Birth cave** — Bethlehem, womb-tomb

## Psychological Meaning

- Consciousness emerging
- New self being born
- Separation and individuation
- Creative potential
- Birth of hero/divine child

## Dream Context

Birth dreams signal:
- New consciousness emerging
- Creative impulse active
- Separation from mother
- Beginning of new phase
- Rebirth/renewal
