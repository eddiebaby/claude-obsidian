# Cerberus (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Cerberus is the three-headed guard dog at the entrance to the underworld. Represents the threshold guardian preventing return from the underworld and the danger of the deep unconscious.

## Key Meanings

- Three-headed watchdog
- Underworld guardian
- Threshold keeper
- Barrier between worlds
- Danger and protection combined
- Honey-cake appeasement

## Mythological Role

- Guards Hades' realm
- Prevents escape from underworld
- Must be overcome or appeased
- Hercules captures as labor
- Psychic obstacle

## Psychological Meaning

- Dangerous threshold guardian
- Unconscious keeping watch
- Barrier to regression
- Multi-faceted threat
- Strength of underworld forces

## Dream Context

Cerberus dreams signal:
- Underworld threshold approaching
- Dangerous passage ahead
- Guardian force appearing
- Need to appease danger
- Death/transformation boundary
