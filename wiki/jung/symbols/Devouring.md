# Devouring (Symbol/Motif)

## Core Interpretation

The devouring motif is common to sun-myths and rebirth cycles. The hero is swallowed by the whale-dragon, enters the belly/underworld, and is regurgitated reborn. The devouring is not merely destructive but is the necessary entry into transformation.

The whale-dragon represents the maternal womb-like container that swallows and regenerates. Frobenius traces this motif across cultures as fundamental to hero and rebirth mythology.

## Key Meanings

- Hero's descent/entry into transformation
- Maternal womb-swallowing
- Death as necessary passage
- Engulfing by the unconscious
- Regeneration through being consumed

## Mythological Examples

- Whale-dragon swallowing the hero
- Jonah in the whale
- Hero's night-sea journey
- Lamia devouring children
- Mother's mouth/womb as engulfing

## Dream Context

Devouring dreams signal:
- Regression fear
- Being overwhelmed by maternal pull
- Entry into transformation
- Loss of boundaries/ego dissolution
