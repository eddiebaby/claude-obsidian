# Soma (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Soma is the drink of immortality in Hindu mythology. It represents psychic nourishment and spiritual intoxication. Soma-Agni (fire-water) combines the dual nature of fire as both destructive and creative.

The soma is not merely physical drink but the essence extracted through sacrifice - the divine food that grants immortality.

## Key Meanings

- Drink of immortality
- Divine nourishment
- Fire-water combination
- Extracted through sacrifice
- Spiritual ecstasy

## Hindu/Vedic Context

- Soma in sacrifice ritual
- Amrita (nectar of immortality)
- Connection to Agni and fire
- Divine intoxication

## Psychological Meaning

- Nourishment from the unconscious
- Spiritual sustenance
- Transformative drink
