# Hesperides (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The Hesperides are the distant western garden where golden apples of immortality grow. Represents the goal of the hero's quest—treasure guarded in the far distance. The precious fruit that grants eternal life. What is sought but difficult to attain.

## Key Meanings

- Golden apples
- Distant treasure
- Guarded goal
- Immortality promise
- Hero's quest
- Western garden

## Mythological Context

- Greek garden of gods
- Golden apples of immortality
- Heracles' eleventh labor
- Hera's treasure
- Western paradise

## Psychological Meaning

- Ultimate goal of transformation
- Treasure hard to reach
- Immortality wish
- Hero's achievement
- Self realization

## Dream Context

Hesperides dreams signal:
- Quest emerging
- Treasure sought
- Goal identified
- Hero journey starting
- Immortality wished
