# Coniunctio (Alchemical Union)

## Jung's Core Interpretation

*Coniunctio* is the alchemical marriage — the union of opposites that produces the Philosopher's Stone. It is the central event of the opus, the goal toward which the entire process moves. Psychologically it is the integration of the unconscious with consciousness: the union of the ego with its shadow, anima/animus, and ultimately with the Self.

The *coniunctio* is not a merger that dissolves both partners. It is a union that preserves both — like a chemical compound that is more than the sum of its elements.

---

## The Axiom of Maria Prophetissa

The foundational alchemical axiom, running "like a *leitmotiv* throughout almost the whole of the lifetime of alchemy, extending over more than seventeen centuries" (CW 12, ¶26):

> "One becomes two, two becomes three, and out of the third comes the one as the fourth."

Maria Prophetissa (the Jewess, sister of Moses) — the most ancient female alchemical authority — stated this as the fundamental law of transformation. Jung traces it:

- **1 → 2**: The original unity splits (consciousness from unconscious, Sol from Luna, ego from shadow)
- **2 → 3**: The tension of opposites generates a third thing — the *coniunctio* process itself
- **Out of the third → 1 as the 4th**: The reconciling symbol emerges, the *lapis*, the Self — but it is not the original unity restored. It is a *new* synthesis containing what was previously opposed

The fourth = the shadow's contribution to wholeness, the excluded principle that makes the synthesis real rather than merely mental.

---

## The Alchemical Marriage

The *coniunctio* is most concretely imaged as the wedding of Sol (the Sun King) and Luna (the Moon Queen). The central illustration of the *Rosarium philosophorum* (1550) is a sequence of ten images showing this union step by step:

1. Confrontation of King and Queen
2. Their descent into the bath (*balneum philosophorum*)
3. The sexual union
4. Death (*nigredo* of the corpse)
5. Washing of the corpse
6. The soul ascending from the corpse
7. Illumination
8. Return of the soul
9. Resurrection
10. The final *coniunctio* producing the crowned hermaphrodite

This is also the pattern of individuation: encounter → descent → dissolution → renewal → integration.

## The Mystic Vessel (Figure 23, CW 12)

Figure 23 in CW 12 depicts "the mystic vessel where the two natures unite (*sol* and *luna*, *caduceus*) to produce the *filius hermaphroditus*" — Hermes Psychopompos flanked by the six gods of the planets.

The vessel (*vas hermeticum* = the alchemical retort) is the contained psychological space in which the *coniunctio* can occur:
- The analytic relationship as the vessel
- A sustained engagement with the unconscious
- The temenos (sacred enclosure) of active imagination

What is produced: the *filius hermaphroditus* = the divine androgyne = the symbol of psychic wholeness that contains both masculine and feminine principles.

## The Fountain: *Fons Mercurialis*

Figure 25 in CW 12: "The fountain of life as *fons mercurialis*" — from the *Rosarium philosophorum* (1550). Sol and Luna appear above the fountain; stars (mineral quintessence) surround it.

The fountain of life is the *water* that flows from the *coniunctio*. Dream 15 in the series:

"The dreamer's mother is pouring water from one basin into another."

Jung interprets: the mother (collective unconscious) pours the water of life to the sister (anima). The transferring of the water signals that the mother-imago has been replaced by the anima — a decisive step in the individuation process. The *coniunctio* with the anima liberates the living water.

Life-waters after the *coniunctio* are called: *aqua nostra*, *mercurius vivus*, *argentum vivum*, *vinum ardens*, *aqua vitae*, *succus lunariae* — "a living being not devoid of substance" (¶94).

## The *Matrimonium Alchymicum*

The ancient alchemical treatise attributed to Maria Prophetissa: "Marry gum with gum in true marriage." (¶209)

The "gum" is a secret name for the transforming substance — called "gum arabic" on account of its adhesive quality. Khunrath declares the "red gum" = the "resin of the wise" = a synonym for the transforming substance. One commentator: it is the *vis animans* (life force), likened to the "glue of the world" (*glutinum mundi*) — "the medium between mind and body and the union of both."

The *coniunctio* produces the glue that holds psyche and soma together. This is why Jung later connected it to his concept of synchronicity — the *acausal connecting principle* that binds psychic and physical events.

## The Sun and Moon Bath (Figure 27, CW 12)

"Life-renewing influence of the conjoined sun and moon on the bath" — from Milan, Biblioteca Ambrosiana.

Three figures bathe in the enclosure beneath the conjoined Sol and Luna. The bath (*balneum*) is where dissolution occurs in the service of *coniunctio*: the old structure must dissolve before the new can form. The lunar and solar influence on the bath represents the anima and the spirit jointly presiding over this dissolution.

## Dream Context in the Series

The *coniunctio* theme runs through the dream series as the movement toward wholeness. Dream 22 (¶200):

"On the margin of the note there is a drawing: a wheel or wreath with eight spokes."

The eight-spoked wheel = the mandala = the *coniunctio* in its circular form. The message on the note: "Salvation does not come from refusing to take part or from running away. Nor does it come from just drifting. Salvation comes from complete surrender, with one's eyes always turned to the centre."

The *coniunctio* requires not flight and not passive drift, but *active engagement* — "eyes always on the centre" — which is the psychological definition of individuation.

## Psychological Meaning

The *coniunctio* is the psychological event of:

1. **Ego-Self union** — not the ego becoming the Self, but the ego coming into a living relationship with the Self; consciousness acknowledging what transcends it

2. **Integration of the contrasexual** — in a man, the anima (projected onto women, now recognized as inner) becomes an inner function of relatedness and creativity; in a woman, the animus becomes inner logos and discernment

3. **Resolution of the main complex** — what had been most strongly repressed (the core of the shadow, the mother- or father-complex) is finally metabolized, not escaped

4. **The paradoxical state** — containing good and evil, light and dark, male and female without splitting off either; "thesis and antithesis and at the same time synthesis" (¶22)

5. **The production of the lapis** — the concrete psychological outcome: a person who has become themselves, fully, including the parts that collective opinion deems inferior
