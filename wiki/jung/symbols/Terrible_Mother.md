# Terrible Mother (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The Terrible Mother is the shadow aspect of the Mother Archetype - the devouring, suffocating, all-consuming maternal principle. She represents what must be overcome for individuation: the incest taboo, the danger of regression, the pull back to unconscious fusion.

Jung explicitly names her as the source of all evil fantasies and the basis of psychological disturbance. She appears in myths as the witch, the Lamia, the Sphinx, the Terrible Goddess.

## Key Meanings

- Devouring maternal principle
- Incest taboo
- Regression and infantilism
- Psychological stagnation
- The dark mother who would keep the child unconscious

## Manifestations

- Lamia (child-eating demon)
- Lilith (nightmare, devouring mother)
- Sphinx (enigmatic, devouring)
- Whale-dragon mother
- Kali (terrible and nurturing)

## Dream Context

Terrible Mother dreams signal:
- Unresolved dependency
- Fear of regression
- Incestuous longings (unconscious)
- Need for separation and individuation
- Devouring anxiety
