# Sacrifice (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Sacrifice is both a literal ritual and a psychological principle. The sacrificial fire (Agni) transforms the offering into divine substance. Jung sees sacrifice as fundamental to psychological transformation - something must be surrendered for rebirth to occur.

The sacrifice is not merely loss but transformation - what is burned becomes spirit, becomes light.

## Key Meanings

- Offering to the divine
- Transformation through burning
- Surrender of ego
- Death-rebirth principle
- Sacred offering

## Ritual Dimensions

- Fire sacrifice (Agni)
- Soma drink (immortality)
- Participatory sacrifice (being consumed/transformed)

## Psychological Meaning

- Ego-death
- Surrender to the Self
- Transformation requiring loss

## Dream Context

Sacrifice dreams signal:
- Necessary surrender
- Transformation requiring surrender
- Offering to something greater
- Death of old identity
