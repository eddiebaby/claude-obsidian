# Dog (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The dog is a psychopomp - a guide between worlds, especially the guide to the underworld and rebirth. In Egyptian mythology, Anubis (dog-headed) assists in reconstruction and regeneration. The dog helps gather the scattered pieces and restore wholeness.

The dog is associated with death but also with fruitfulness and renewal. It is the guardian and the helper.

## Key Meanings

- Psychopomp/guide to underworld
- Death as entry into rebirth
- Gathering and reconstruction
- Faithfulness and guardianship
- Connection to the mother (through Hecate, maternal Anubis)

## Mythological Examples

- Anubis (Egyptian jackal-god)
- Hecate (dog-headed underworld goddess)
- Cerberus (guardian dog)
- Mithras sacrifice and the dog

## Dream Context

Dog dreams often signal:
- Guide appearing for life transition
- Need for loyalty or devotion
- Reconstruction after breakdown
- Death-rebirth threshold
