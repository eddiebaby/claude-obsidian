# Bull (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The bull is a symbol of brute force, generative power, and the father principle. Represents instinctual masculine strength, fertility, and sacrificial power. Both creative and destructive.

## Key Meanings

- Father and paternal power
- Animal vitality and instinct
- Fertility and procreation
- Sacrifice and conquest
- Brute force and courage
- Dragon-slayer (hero conquers instinct)

## Mythological Context

- Bull-sacrifice in rituals
- Dionysian orgies with bull
- Dragon and bull combination
- Father-symbol
- Hidden feminine (in Attis-Cybele)
- Seed potential

## Psychological Meaning

- Instinctual masculine power
- Brute force needing integration
- Generative capacity
- Father authority
- Sacrifice required

## Dream Context

Bull dreams signal:
- Masculine power rising
- Instinctual impulses
- Father complex
- Need for sacrifice
- Sexual/aggressive energy
