# Fire (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Fire is libido in its raw, transformative aspect. It is both destructive and creative. Jung extensively analyzes fire through the Hindu god Agni (the sacrificial flame), who is simultaneously the mediator between gods and men, the subject and object of sacrifice.

Fire represents psychic energy that must be carefully tended. Improperly handled, it leads to regression (incest, infantilism). Properly integrated, it powers individuation.

## Key Meanings

- Libido/psychic energy
- Purification and transformation
- Divine mediator (Agni)
- Passion and destruction
- The sacrificial principle

## Sanskrit Connections

- **Agni**: divine fire, mediator
- **Tejas**: psychological intensity, sharpness, fire-principle
- **Soma**: drink of immortality connected to fire

## Dream Context

Fire dreams signal:
- Rising libido or passion
- Need for purification or transformation
- Destructive forces requiring integration
- Sacred or spiritual energy
