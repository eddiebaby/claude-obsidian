# Rose (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The rose is the beloved and eternal feminine beauty. Symbolizes the womb of nature, renewal, and spiritual love. The flower that opens and closes in eternal cycles.

## Key Meanings

- Beloved symbol
- Eternal feminine
- Beauty and grace
- Womb of nature
- Eternal renewal
- Spiritual love
