# Vine (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The vine is sacred nourishment producing wine and grapes. Represents Dionysian ecstasy and the fruits of the earth. The plant that transforms into divine drink.

## Key Meanings

- Sacred plant
- Wine production
- Nourishment source
- Dionysian principle
- Transformation to divine
- Grapes and intoxication

## Mythological Context

- Dionysus/Bacchus vine
- Sacred vine cult
- Wine ceremonies
- Fertility symbol
- Ecstatic transformation

## Psychological Meaning

- Nourishment from earth
- Transformative drink
- Ecstatic intoxication
- Divine fermentation
- Sacred fruit

## Dream Context

Vine dreams signal:
- Nourishment flowing
- Ecstasy approaching
- Sacred drink available
- Transformation active
- Dionysian forces
