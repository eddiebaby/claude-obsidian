# Baldur (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Baldur is the Norse god of light and beauty whose death initiates the end of the world. Represents the vulnerability of consciousness and light to dark forces. The bright principle that can be destroyed.

## Key Meanings

- Light and beauty
- Vulnerability to darkness
- Death of the bright principle
- Inevitability of destruction
- Connection to Loki (shadow)

## Norse Mythology Context

- Killed by mistletoe (hidden weakness)
- Death precipitates Ragnarok
- Loki arranges betrayal
- Represents end of harmony

## Psychological Meaning

- Consciousness can be destroyed
- Beauty is fragile
- Shadow (Loki) opposes light
- Cycle of destruction/renewal

## Dream Context

Baldur dreams signal:
- Vulnerability of consciousness
- Hidden weakness being exploited
- Loss of innocence
- Approaching transformation through death
