# Hygeia (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Hygeia is the goddess of health and wholeness. Represents the integrated, healed state. Health as harmony of all parts. The goal of psychological treatment—restoration to wholeness and natural functioning.

## Key Meanings

- Health and wholeness
- Integration achieved
- Natural functioning
- Goddess of healing
- Balance restored
- Vitality returned

## Mythological Context

- Greek goddess of health
- Aesculapius's daughter
- Divine healing power
- Health maintenance
- Prevention principle

## Psychological Meaning

- Integration of psyche
- Healing achieved
- Natural functioning
- Wholeness embodied
- Vitality restored

## Dream Context

Hygeia dreams signal:
- Healing progressing
- Wholeness approaching
- Health returning
- Integration working
- Balance emerging
