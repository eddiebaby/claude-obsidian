# Furnace (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The furnace is the alchemical vessel - the retort or athanor - where transformation occurs. The fiery furnace represents the womb-like container where old substances dissolve and new ones are born.

The three men in the fiery furnace (Christian imagery) represent the trinitarian soul being forged in the fire of transformation.

## Key Meanings

- Alchemical transformation vessel
- Womb-like container
- Fire/heat as transformative
- Purification through burning
- Crucible of change

## Alchemical Symbolism

- Athanor (the furnace)
- Retort and melting pot
- Wedding chamber/marriage
- Container of the work

## Dream Context

Furnace dreams signal:
- Alchemical transformation underway
- Being heated/purified
- Crucible experience
- Dissolution and reformation
