# King (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The king represents ruling consciousness, paternal authority, and established order. The wounded king (Fisher King) represents consciousness damaged and needing healing through the Grail quest.

## Key Meanings

- Ruling principle
- Paternal authority
- Established order
- Consciousness directing
- Divine right
- Wounds needing healing

## Psychological Meaning

- Ego as king
- Conscious rule
- Father principle
- Ordering force
- Authority wounded

## Dream Context

King dreams signal:
- Authority present
- Rule consciousness
- Father principle
- Order needed
- Wound/healing theme
