# Circle (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The circle is the divine order and cosmic wholeness. Circle of gods arranged in mandala form represents the self and totality. Perfection of form without beginning or end. The circumference as boundary of consciousness containing all archetypal powers.

## Key Meanings

- Divine order
- Wholeness complete
- Cosmic totality
- Perfect form
- Self as circle
- Sacred boundary

## Mythological Context

- Circle of gods (Balinese)
- Mandala form
- Zodiac circles
- Cosmic ordering
- Sacred geometry

## Psychological Meaning

- Self actualization
- Integration achieved
- Totality expressed
- Psychic wholeness
- Boundary contained
