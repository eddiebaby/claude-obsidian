# Flood (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The flood is overwhelming water representing dissolution and rebirth. The glorious flood carries away old consciousness and brings renewal. Connection to the maternal embrace and dissolution.

## Key Meanings

- Overwhelming water
- Dissolution of old self
- Destruction and rebirth
- Maternal embrace
- Chaos before creation
- Purifying deluge

## Mythological Context

- Biblical flood
- Cyclical destruction/rebirth
- Chaos water
- Primordial ocean
- Hindu dissolution

## Psychological Meaning

- Ego-death immersion
- Overwhelm by unconscious
- Return to source
- Dissolution for renewal
- Transformation through drowning

## Dream Context

Flood dreams signal:
- Overwhelming emotion
- Ego-dissolution imminent
- Rebirth through drowning
- Maternal return
- Transformation coming
