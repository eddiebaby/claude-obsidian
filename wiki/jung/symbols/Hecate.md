# Hecate (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Hecate is the triple-bodied underworld goddess, dog-headed and wielding serpents. Psychopomp who brings news and accompanies Demeter searching for the stolen daughter. Triple form represents the three aspects of transformation: past, present, future or birth, life, death.

## Key Meanings

- Underworld guardian
- Triple-form goddess
- Psychopomp (guide between worlds)
- Dog-headed power
- Regeneration keeper
- Boundary guardian

## Mythological Context

- Triple-bodied Hecate (Roman)
- Dog sacrifices to Hecate
- Serpent-wielding power
- Demeter's companion
- Birth and marriage goddess
- Crossroads guardian

## Psychological Meaning

- Shadow integration (triple form)
- Underworld guide
- Transformation facilitator
- Boundary crosser
- Regenerative power
