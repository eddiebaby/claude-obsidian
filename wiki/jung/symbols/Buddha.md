# Buddha (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Buddha represents the enlightened consciousness, the awakened self. Birth myths emphasize separation from family and renunciation. Symbolizes the achievement of non-self and transcendent awareness.

## Key Meanings

- Enlightenment and awakening
- Transcendent consciousness
- Separation from family ties
- Renunciation of worldly life
- The Self archetype
- Non-self realization

## Birth Myths

- Miraculous conception (heavenly)
- Birth of future Buddha prophesied
- Separation from parents
- Unfulfilled desires leading away
- Renunciation as path

## Psychological Meaning

- Highest consciousness possible
- Transcendence of ego
- Awakening to truth
- Separation from mother
- Individuation completion

## Dream Context

Buddha dreams signal:
- Spiritual awakening underway
- Need for renunciation
- Enlightenment seeking
- Transcendent awareness emerging
- Non-attachment path
