# Serpent (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The serpent is the alchemical symbol of transformation - the shedding of skin represents death and renewal. The mercurial serpent (from alchemy) symbolizes the psychic transformation. The serpent is both wise and dangerous, both healing and poisonous.

Unlike the dragon (which is often fixed in its evil), the serpent is active transformation - the principle of continuous change.

## Key Meanings

- Transformation and shedding
- Alchemical principle (mercurial)
- Wisdom and danger
- Death-rebirth cycle
- Continuous renewal

## Alchemical Significance

- Mercurial serpent as transformation agent
- Ouroboros (tail-eating serpent)
- Kundalini (inner serpent power)

## Dream Context

Serpent dreams signal:
- Transformation underway
- Shedding of old skin/identity
- Alchemical change
- Wisdom emerging from danger
