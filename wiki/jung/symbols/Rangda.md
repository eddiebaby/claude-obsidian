# Rangda (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Rangda is the witch-thief of children from Balinese tradition. Represents the devouring mother shadow and fear of maternal death-bringing power. The opposite of nurturing maternal principle—the mother as destroyer and child-stealer.

## Key Meanings

- Devouring witch
- Child-thief
- Maternal shadow
- Death-bringing mother
- Fearful archetype
- Destroyer principle

## Mythological Context

- Balinese painted wood figure
- Witch tradition
- Child-stealing danger
- Death goddess aspect
- Folk terror figure
