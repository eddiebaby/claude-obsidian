# Hydra (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The Hydra is the Mistress of Beasts—a multiple-headed monster representing chaotic multiplicity and temptation. Each head is a separate desire or impulse. The hero must confront and integrate these disparate animal natures. Regenerative—when one head is cut off, others grow back.

## Key Meanings

- Multiple-headed chaos
- Animal temptations
- Fragmented desires
- Regenerative power
- Beast mistress
- Temptation embodied

## Mythological Context

- Greek hydra with many heads
- Heracles' trial
- Regenerative monster
- Animal multiplicity
- Temptation lord

## Psychological Meaning

- Fragmented instinct
- Multiple competing desires
- Chaotic unconscious
- Shadow multiplicity
- Integration work required

## Dream Context

Hydra dreams signal:
- Temptation multiple
- Desires conflicting
- Chaos threatening
- Hero trial present
