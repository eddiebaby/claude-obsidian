# Chalice (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The chalice is a vessel containing divine substance, representing the receptive maternal principle and the containing of sacred essence. Symbol of the Holy Grail and feminine capacity to hold and preserve.

## Key Meanings

- Container of divine substance
- Maternal receptive principle
- Holy Grail symbol
- Vessel for transformation
- Sacred holding
- Feminine power to contain

## Christian Significance

- Cup at Last Supper
- Christ's blood contained
- Grail quest symbol
- Sacred offering vessel

## Psychological Meaning

- Receptive feminine principle
- Capacity to contain
- Nourishment and blessing
- Holy or sacred space
- Transformation container

## Dream Context

Chalice dreams signal:
- Receptive capacity needed
- Sacred substance being held
- Feminine power emerging
- Spiritual nourishment
- Transformation vessel
