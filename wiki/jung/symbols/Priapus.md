# Priapus (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Priapus, the phallic guardian god with erect phallus and serpent coils. Represents fertility, abundance, and the generative power of nature. Guardian of gardens and orchards. The phallic principle made divine.

## Key Meanings

- Phallic divinity
- Fertility guardian
- Abundance protector
- Sexual generativity
- Nature's generative force
- Guardianship principle

## Mythological Context

- Greco-Roman fertility god
- Garden guardian
- Serpent-phallic combination
- Apotropaic power
- Abundance bestower

## Psychological Meaning

- Generative instinct
- Sexual consciousness
- Creative fertility
- Protective guardianship
- Nature's fecundity
