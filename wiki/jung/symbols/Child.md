# Child (Divine Child) (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The divine child is a symbol of new consciousness being born. It is always vulnerable, always in danger, always needing protection. The child represents the emerging self - tender, new, full of potential.

The child is often wounded or threatened in myth - representing the fragility of emerging consciousness.

## Key Meanings

- New consciousness emerging
- Vulnerability and innocence
- Divine incarnation
- Potential and growth
- Always threatened/wounded

## Mythological Examples

- Christ child
- Hero as child
- Divine infants in danger
- Horus (Horpi-chrud)

## Dream Context

Child dreams signal:
- New development emerging
- Vulnerability needing protection
- Innocent consciousness
- Growth and potential
