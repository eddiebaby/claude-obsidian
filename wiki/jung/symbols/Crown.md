# Crown (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The crown represents eternal glory, achievement, and the highest point of transformation. The soul adorned with crown as bride and queen in the eternal splendour. Victory achieved through sacrifice. The crown is the reward given by God in heaven.

## Key Meanings

- Eternal glory
- Achievement recognized
- Royalty and authority
- Bridal glorification
- Victory reward
- Heavenly adornment

## Mythological Context

- Bride crowned as queen in heaven
- Conqueror adorned
- Crown from heaven's springs
- Eternal crown imagery
- Victory crowning

## Psychological Meaning

- Self actualization
- Integration achieved
- Ego transcendence
- Victory over shadow
- Eternal consciousness

## Dream Context

Crown dreams signal:
- Achievement imminent
- Glory approaching
- Transformation complete
- Bridal consciousness
- Victory celebrated
