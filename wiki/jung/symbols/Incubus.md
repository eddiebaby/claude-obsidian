# Incubus (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The incubus is the male demon that visits sleepers at night, particularly female sleepers. Represents repressed sexual desire made demonic. The nightmare demon—invasive, possessing force from the unconscious. Sexual anxiety embodied as supernatural attack.

## Key Meanings

- Night demon
- Sexual invasion
- Nightmare terror
- Repressed sexuality
- Sleep possession
- Demonic force

## Mythological Context

- Medieval folklore
- Demon lover
- Succubus counterpart
- Sleep visitations
- Sexual nightmares

## Psychological Meaning

- Sexual repression

- Anxiety possession
- Shadow sexuality
- Fear of Other
