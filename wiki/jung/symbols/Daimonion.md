# Daimonion (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The daimonion is the guardian spirit, the inner voice that restrains and warns. It is the divine sign (semeion) that prevents the ego from overreach and directs toward a higher purpose. Not commanding but advisory, it represents the Self's subtle guidance through intuitive restraint and protective caution.

## Key Meanings

- Guardian spirit
- Inner voice
- Restraining force
- Divine sign
- Protective caution
- Fate's messenger

## Mythological Context

- Genius loci (Roman guardian spirit)
- Greek daimon (divine spirit)
- Intermediary between divine and human
- Oracle's inspiration
- Fate's indicator

## Psychological Meaning

- Self's subtle guidance
- Intuitive wisdom
- Protective shadow
- Fate consciousness
- Individual destiny

Daimonion dreams signal:
- Inner guidance present
- Restraint needed
- Fate approaching
- Spirit communication
