# Hand (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The hand represents creative power and the phallic principle. An open hand with large fingers symbolizes generative creative force. Fire is produced from the hand; fire comes from hand. Represents conscious grasping and creative manipulation of reality.

## Key Meanings

- Creative power
- Phallic principle
- Conscious grasping
- Generative force
- Active manipulation
- Finger extension (phallic aspect)

## Mythological Context

- Hand opens with large fingers in fantasy imagery
- Halo around fingers (sacred creative power)
- Hand from unconscious drawing things up
- Agni (fire god) worshipped as golden-winged bird from hand

## Psychological Meaning

- Ego's grasp on reality
- Creative agency
- Phallic consciousness
- Power to manifest
- Conscious will applied

## Dream Context

Hand dreams signal:
- Creative power emerging
- Grasping consciousness
- Phallic force active
- Manipulation of reality
- Agency returning
