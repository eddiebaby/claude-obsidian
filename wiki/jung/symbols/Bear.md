# Bear (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The bear is a symbol of the mother goddess and fierce protection. Associated with Artemis and wild feminine power. Represents both nurturing and dangerous maternal force.

## Key Meanings

- Mother goddess
- Fierce protective power
- Wild nature
- Constellation (Big Bear, Little Bear)
- Earth mother principle

## Mythological Context

- Artemis/Diana with bear
- Artio Celtic bear goddess
- Mystic identity with mother
- Constellation in sky

## Psychological Meaning

- Maternal protective rage
- Connection to wild/instinctual nature
- Earth-grounding
- Primal feminine power

## Dream Context

Bear dreams signal:
- Maternal protection or danger
- Instinctual power rising
- Connection to earth/nature
- Fierce maternal love or devouring
