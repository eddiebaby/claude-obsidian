# Bacchus (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Bacchus is the god of ecstatic transformation through wine and intoxication. Represents the dissolution of ego-boundaries and access to divine or unconscious states. The god who unmasks hidden truth through altered consciousness.

## Key Meanings

- Divine ecstasy and intoxication
- Dissolution of ego boundaries
- Truth-revealing power
- Fertility and regeneration
- Consecration and blessing

## Mythological Context

- Roman equivalent of Dionysus
- God of wine, theater, transformation
- Presides over mystery cults
- Connected to rebirth through dismemberment

## Dream Context

Bacchus dreams signal:
- Loss of control or boundaries
- Access to hidden truths
- Transformative intoxication
- Divine ecstasy or possession
