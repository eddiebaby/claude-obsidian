# Castration (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Castration is a symbol of sacrifice of masculine sexual power and the subjugation of instinct. Represents ritual mutilation, loss of generative capacity, and the renunciation of raw instinctuality.

## Key Meanings

- Sacrifice of sexual power
- Ritual mutilation
- Loss of generative force
- Incest prohibition (castration as motif)
- Snake-inflicted wound
- Self-castration (renunciation)

## Mythological Context

- Attis-Cybele cult castration
- Tree-felling as castration
- Ritual castration in mystery religions
- Snake bite castration
- Self-castration as religious act

## Psychological Meaning

- Sacrifice of instinct
- Integration of sexuality
- Renunciation for higher good
- Loss of raw masculine power
- Subjugation of animal nature

## Dream Context

Castration dreams signal:
- Loss of power feared
- Sexual conflict
- Renunciation needed
- Masculine identity crisis
- Ritual sacrifice underway
