# Tree (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The tree is a maternal symbol: the womb from which life grows, and the coffin in which the dead are buried. It represents the unified space where mother-symbols (earth, wood, water) converge. The tree is simultaneously life and death.

The Cross as Tree of Life - Christ hanging on the tree represents the god-man born from the mother-tree, sacrificed, and reborn. The tree connects underworld, earth, and heaven.

## Key Meanings

- Mother symbol (entwining, birth-giving)
- Womb and tomb (life and death)
- World axis (Yggdrasil, cosmic tree)
- Resurrection and rebirth
- Cross as tree of life

## Mythological Examples

- Yggdrasil (Norse world-tree)
- Osiris resting in tree branches
- Christ on Tree of Life
- Egyptian tree giving birth

## Dream Context

Tree dreams often signal:
- Connection to the mother
- Life/death cycles
- Spiritual grounding or rootedness
- Transformation through entwining
