# Cross (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The cross is the tree of life transformed into the symbol of Christ. It represents the intersection of opposites - horizontal (earthly) and vertical (spiritual). The cross marks the center where all directions meet.

Jung sees the cross not as mere Christian symbol but as an archetypal pattern of wholeness and transformation that precedes Christianity and transcends it.

## Key Meanings

- Intersection of opposites
- Center point (mandala principle)
- Tree of Life transformed
- Psychological wholeness
- Divine and human united

## Historical Depth

- Pre-Christian symbolism
- Tree-Cross connection
- Four directions/four functions
- Mandala of the opposites

## Dream Context

Cross dreams signal:
- Integration of opposites
- Centering and wholeness
- Spiritual crossing
- Sacrifice and transformation
