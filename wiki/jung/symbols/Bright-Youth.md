# Bright Youth (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The bright youth is the psychopomp figure—the shepherd-god who leads the herd of mankind. A variation of the Orpheus, Poimandres, or Krishna archetype. He represents the longing to become a god, the fascination with the highest ideal. Yet he also constellates the shadow—the destructive monster that follows him.

## Key Meanings

- Psychopomp guide
- Shepherd archetype
- Divine messenger
- Radiant ideal
- Collective leader
- Shadow-constellator

## Mythological Context

- Orpheus leading souls

- Krishna guiding the herd
- Divine youth archetype
- Solar hero
