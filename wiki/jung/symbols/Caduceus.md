# Caduceus (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The caduceus is the entwined serpents on a staff, representing the reconciliation of opposites and alchemical transformation. Symbol of Mercury/Hermes and the integration of dual principles.

## Key Meanings

- Two serpents entwined
- Reconciliation of opposites
- Alchemical transformation
- Mercury (mercurial principle)
- Mediation and balance
- Hermes (psychopomp) staff

## Alchemical Significance

- Represents the work of alchemy
- Integration of contraries
- Transformation through union
- Medical symbol (healing)
- Psychic balance

## Psychological Meaning

- Integration of shadow and light
- Reconciliation of opposites
- Psychic balance
- Mediation principle
- Transformation through union

## Dream Context

Caduceus dreams signal:
- Need for integration
- Balancing opposites
- Alchemical transformation
- Healing underway
- Mercury/messenger energy
