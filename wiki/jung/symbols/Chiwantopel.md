# Chiwantopel (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Chiwantopel is the animus-hero figure from Miss Miller's fantasies. An Aztec/Mayan inspired masculine figure representing the soul-mate and the dreamer's highest masculine ideal. Embodies horse-snake symbolism.

## Key Meanings

- Animus figure (soul-mate)
- American Indian hero
- Masculine ideal
- Horse symbolism (animal vitality)
- Snake symbolism (wisdom/danger)
- Death threat/transformation

## Miss Miller Context

- Appears in her fantasies
- Represents the beloved masculine
- Subject of her impossible longing
- Connection to Indian symbolism
- Death through union

## Psychological Meaning

- Animus projection
- Ideal masculine self
- Animal-spiritual combination
- Unattainable soul-mate
- Divine masculine principle

## Dream Context

Chiwantopel dreams signal:
- Animus projection active
- Masculine ideal sought
- Spiritual-animal synthesis
- Impossible love/longing
- Hero-soul-mate appearing
