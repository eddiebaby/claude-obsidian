# Cow (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The cow is the heavenly mother - the great nurturing principle that carries the sun-god Ra on her back. In Egyptian mythology, Hathor and Nuit are cow-goddesses who represent the sky, fecundity, and the maternal womb from which all beings emerge.

The cow represents containment, nourishment, and the primordial mother substance.

## Key Meanings

- Heavenly maternal principle
- Nurture and fecundity
- Sky goddess
- Origin and containment
- Life-giving substance

## Mythological Examples

- Hathor (cow-headed Egyptian goddess)
- Nuit (sky-cow, mother of Ra)
- The Divine Cow carrying gods
- Hindu sacred cow

## Dream Context

Cow dreams signal:
- Connection to primal nurturance
- Abundance and fertility
- Heavenly or spiritual mothering
- Containment and security
