# Egg (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The egg is the primordial symbol of wholeness and the Self. Contains all potential within undifferentiated form. The cosmic egg from which all creation emerges.

## Key Meanings

- Wholeness container
- Primordial potential
- Self-symbol
- Creation origin
- Undifferentiated whole
- Cosmic principle

## Mythological Context

- Cosmic egg in creation myths
- Brahma emerging from egg
- Orphic cosmology
- Alchemy symbol

## Psychological Meaning

- Self in undifferentiated form
- Total potential
- Wholeness before division
- Creation's seed
- Integration symbol

## Dream Context

Egg dreams signal:
- Wholeness emerging
- Potential ripening
- Self developing
- Creation beginning
- New birth coming
