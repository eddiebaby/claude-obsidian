# Swan (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The swan is the bird of Eros and Apollo—divine messenger of beauty and love. It sings most beautifully as it dies, connecting music, transformation, and transcendence. The white swan embodies grace, spiritual aspiration, and the alighting of divine Eros upon human consciousness.

## Key Meanings

- Divine bird (Apollo)
- Eros messenger
- Beauty embodied
- Song at death
- Spiritual grace
- Transformation bird

## Mythological Context

- Apollo's sacred bird
- Eros appearing as swan
- Death-song motif
- Divine manifestation
- Love's messenger

## Psychological Meaning

- Eros coming alive in soul
- Beauty recognition
- Spiritual aspiration
- Death acceptance
- Divine love manifesting
