# Mistletoe (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Mistletoe is the death-dealing shadow brother. Represents the fatal weapon that kills the solar hero. Parasitic plant growing on trees, the invisible threat hidden in the plant world. In Baldur mythology, the one weapon through which the beautiful hero can die.

## Key Meanings

- Death dealer
- Shadow brother
- Fatal weakness
- Parasitic force
- Hidden danger
- Darkness in light

## Mythological Context

- Baldur's death by mistletoe
- Invisible threat
- Plant growing on sacred tree
- Loki's weapon
- One fatal exception

## Psychological Meaning

- Shadow aspect of hero
- Hidden weakness
- Death's inevitability
- Limitation of consciousness
- Vulnerability revealed

## Dream Context

Mistletoe dreams signal:
- Hidden danger present
- Vulnerability exposed
- Shadow approaching
- Limitation confronted
- Death awareness
