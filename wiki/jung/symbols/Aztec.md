# Aztec (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The Aztec represents the primitive masculine aspect—the father principle in its raw, phallic, heroic form. Embodies masculine ideal with solar and eagle symbolism. For the anima-possessed woman, the Aztec symbolizes the masculine inner figure too exalted for embodied love. A personification of the animus.

## Key Meanings

- Primitive father figure
- Phallic masculine power
- Solar hero archetype
- Exotic masculine ideal
- Animus (inner masculine)
- Feathered warrior

## Mythological Context

- Aztec warrior with sun symbolism
- Eagle-feathered crown
- Sun identification (solar hero)
- Primitive American tradition
- Conquest and power
