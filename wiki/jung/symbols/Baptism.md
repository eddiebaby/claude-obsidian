# Baptism (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Baptism is ritual death and rebirth through water or fire. Represents purification, initiation into new consciousness, and dying to the old self. The symbolic crossing from one state to another.

## Key Meanings

- Death and rebirth
- Purification and cleansing
- Initiation into mystery
- Water immersion (return to womb)
- Fire baptism (spiritual purification)
- Entry into Christian community

## Types

- **Water baptism** — return to primordial waters, rebirth
- **Fire baptism** — purification through burning, Holy Spirit
- **Mythological parallels** — dying god resurrections
- **Psychological** — ego-death and transformation

## Dream Context

Baptism dreams signal:
- Need for purification
- Initiation/threshold crossing
- Dying to old identity
- Entry into new consciousness
- Spiritual cleansing
