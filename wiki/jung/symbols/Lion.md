# Lion (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The lion represents solar strength and conscious power. Strength and courage embodied. Associated with divine kingship and the sun's heat. The lioness appears in regenerative contexts mixing with serpent wisdom.

## Key Meanings

- Solar strength
- Conscious power
- Courage embodied
- Royal authority
- Divine force
- Predatory instinct

## Mythological Context

- Lion and serpent mixing (regeneration)
- Lions in royal imagery
- Solar beast
- Dangerous and graceful
- Strength and majesty

## Psychological Meaning

- Ego strength
- Solar consciousness
- Instinctual power
- Assertive force
- Courage activation

## Dream Context

Lion dreams signal:
- Strength emerging
- Courage needed
- Solar energy active
- Power confronted
- Royal consciousness
