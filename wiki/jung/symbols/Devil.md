# Devil (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The devil is the shadow principle embodied, the rejected evil split off from consciousness. Often appears with cloven hoof (animal nature) and rides horses (possession of libido). Represents what consciousness refuses to integrate.

## Key Meanings

- Shadow personified
- Evil principle split off
- Cloven hoof (animal sign)
- Rides horses (possesses libido)
- Temptation and seduction
- Chaos and destruction

## Christian Significance

- Satan as adversary
- Tempter of humanity
- Prince of darkness
- Hell ruler
- Antichrist principle

## Psychological Meaning

- Rejected shadow
- Possession by instinct
- Unintegrated evil
- Chaos threatening order
- What consciousness denies

## Dream Context

Devil dreams signal:
- Shadow encountering
- Possession threat
- Temptation present
- Evil aspect surfacing
- Integration challenge
