# Water (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Water is the maternal element - the primordial womb from which all life emerges. The sea is both nurturing and devouring, both source and grave. Water represents the formless unconscious from which consciousness rises.

The night-sea journey through water is the journey through the unconscious underworld toward rebirth.

## Key Meanings

- Primordial mother substance
- Unconscious and undifferentiated
- Womb and ocean
- Source and grave
- Formlessness

## Mythological Context

- Waters of creation
- Night-sea journey
- Oceanic participation mystique
- Amniotic waters

## Dream Context

Water dreams signal:
- Regression into unconscious
- Entry into mother-world
- Cleansing or purification
- Undifferentiated state
