# Sun (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The sun is the goal of consciousness, the divine principle toward which libido strives. It represents the highest aspiration of the psyche and the ultimate goal of transformation. The moth longs for the sun; the hero seeks solar illumination.

In Egyptian mythology, Ra is the sun-god who journeys through the underworld at night and is reborn each morning, representing the cycle of death and rebirth through which consciousness must pass.

## Key Meanings

- Divine consciousness and light
- The goal of psychic transformation
- Solar hero (Christ, Mithras, Horus)
- Rational illumination vs. emotional relatedness
- Life force and generative power

## Dream Context

Sun dreams typically signal:
- Consciousness seeking expansion
- Spiritual aspiration or enlightenment
- Goal-orientation and ambition
- The Self appearing as light/radiance
