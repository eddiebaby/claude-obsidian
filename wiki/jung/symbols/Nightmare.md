# Nightmare (Mare) (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The nightmare connects etymologically to the "mare" - the female horse. It combines the horse's sexual/libidinal significance with the maternal devouring power. The nightmare "rides" the sleeper - carrying them away at a gallop, representing possession by unconscious forces.

The nightmare is both a sexual and an anxiety phenomenon - the mare as mother-goddess carrying the dreamer away into the underworld.

## Key Meanings

- Maternal possession
- Sexual anxiety dream
- Riding motif (sexual significance)
- Rhythmic trampling/pounding
- Being carried away to underworld

## Etymology

- Mare = female horse
- Nightmare = mare-riding spirit
- "Treading" = sexual movement
- Connected to Fate (Mara, death-goddess)

## Dream Context

Nightmare dreams signal:
- Maternal anxiety or possession
- Sexual/libidinal arousal
- Loss of control to unconscious forces
- The mother-goddess riding/claiming the dreamer
