# Mirror (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The mirror reflects truth and self-knowledge. Represents seeing one's true nature and the danger of narcissistic self-absorption (Narcissus myth). A tool for consciousness seeing itself.

## Key Meanings

- Reflection and truth
- Self-knowledge
- Truth-seeing
- Narcissism danger
- Consciousness aware of itself
- Illusion vs reality

## Psychological Meaning

- Self-reflection

- Ego awareness
- Vanity danger
- Self-knowledge path
