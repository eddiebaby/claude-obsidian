# Bird (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Birds represent the soul, spirit, and transcendence above material reality. Golden-winged birds especially signify divine or spiritual principle. The soul's ability to rise and escape earthly bonds.

## Key Meanings

- Soul and spirit
- Transcendence and freedom
- Divine messenger
- Golden wings = divine light
- Wishful thinking (escape impulse)
- Language of higher consciousness

## Mythological Context

- Soul-birds in ancient religions
- Golden eagles as divine
- Psychopomps guiding souls
- Ascent symbolism

## Psychological Meaning

- Aspirations toward spirit
- Freedom impulse
- Escape from matter
- Soul's yearning
- Spiritual aspiration

## Dream Context

Bird dreams signal:
- Spiritual aspirations
- Freedom seeking
- Soul development
- Higher consciousness emerging
- Transcendence of earthly concerns
