# Blood (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Blood is the symbol of life force, covenant, and sacrifice. Dragon's blood grants power and knowledge but corrupts. Human blood is sacred, binding, transformative.

## Key Meanings

- Life force and vitality
- Covenant and binding
- Sacrificial offering
- Power and knowledge (dragon blood)
- Corruption through power
- Eucharistic transformation

## Sacred Significance

- Blood sacrifice (Agni, ritual)
- Eucharistic blood (Christ)
- Dragon blood (hero power)
- Blood oath (binding covenant)
- Menstrual blood (taboo, power)

## Psychological Meaning

- Vital energy
- Sacred commitment
- Transformation through sacrifice
- Power from below (instinct)
- Life itself made conscious

## Dream Context

Blood dreams signal:
- Vital energy being released
- Sacred covenant or obligation
- Sacrifice required
- Power emerging
- Life force activated
