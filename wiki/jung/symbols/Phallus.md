# Phallus (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The phallus is the creative generative power that draws things up from the unconscious. It is the masculine principle of creation and manifestation. In Hindu mythology, Pamyles is the phallic daemon who is both the drawer-up of contents from the unconscious and the creative mediator.

The phallus is not merely sexual but represents the power to create, to generate, to bring forth the numinous.

## Key Meanings

- Generative/creative power
- Masculine principle
- Drawing-up from unconscious
- Father power
- Creative force

## Mythological Examples

- Pamyles (phallic daemon drawing water/contents from unconscious)
- Priapus (phallic god)
- Lingam (Hindu symbol of creative power)

## Dream Context

Phallus dreams signal:
- Generative power or creative drive
- Masculine assertion
- Drawing up unconscious contents
- Procreative or creative intent
