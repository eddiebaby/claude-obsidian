# Garden (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The garden is a cultivated, safe space representing the mother's protected domain. Paradise or Eden garden symbolizes the unconscious before the fall into consciousness.

## Key Meanings

- Safe maternal space
- Paradise or Eden
- Cultivated unconscious
- Protected realm
- Before consciousness
- Abundance contained

## Psychological Meaning

- Mother's holding space
- Before differentiation
- Safe unconscious
- Paradise lost
- Regressive safety

## Dream Context

Garden dreams signal:
- Mother connection sought
- Safety regression
- Paradise longing
- Cultivation needed
- Peace seeking
