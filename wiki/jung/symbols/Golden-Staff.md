# Golden Staff (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The golden staff is the crystallized inner authority and indestructible, incorruptible power. It represents power-drive transformed into inner firmness. The dreamer no longer stands on collective earth but stands upon their inner basis—an inner earth, inner foundation. What was possession by passion becomes inner authority.

## Key Meanings

- Inner authority
- Crystallized power
- Incorruptible principle
- Inner basis
- Mediation symbol
- Personal foundation

## Mythological Context

- Hermes' caduceus
- King's scepter
- Healer's staff
- Authority emblem
- Sacred instrument

## Psychological Meaning

- Ego surrender to inner Self
- Passion transformed to wisdom
- Personal authority discovered
- Inner stability achieved
- Inflation transcended
