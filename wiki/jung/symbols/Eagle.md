# Eagle (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The eagle is the divine bird representing the highest consciousness, paternal principle, and transcendent vision. Associated with the father-god and solar power. Represents the goal of consciousness.

## Key Meanings

- Divine bird
- Father principle
- Solar power
- Transcendent vision
- Highest consciousness
- Divine messenger

## Mythological Context

- Jupiter's/Zeus' bird
- Eagle as supreme bird
- Father-god symbol
- Solar association
- Warrior courage

## Psychological Meaning

- Highest perspective
- Paternal principle
- Spiritual aspiration
- Divine viewpoint
- Transcendence goal

## Dream Context

Eagle dreams signal:
- Divine perspective emerging
- Father principle present
- Transcendence sought
- High vision achieved
- Spiritual power
