# White Woman (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The white woman represents the anima figure as messenger of death or bridge to the other world. Dressed in bright, pure raiment, she approaches the dreamer with a calling. She embodies the feminine wisdom and divine beauty that draws the masculine principle toward transformation and the unknown.

## Key Meanings

- Anima figure
- Messenger of death
- Bridge to otherworld
- Feminine wisdom
- Divine beauty
- Pure transcendence

## Mythological Context

- Wise women of Greek tradition (Diotima)
- Valkyries calling the hero
- Death-guide in many traditions
- Bride of transformation
- Sacred feminine

## Psychological Meaning

- Soul calling to consciousness
- Anima mediating between worlds
- Feminine principle guiding transformation
- Death awareness emerging
- Spiritual destination

## Dream Context

White woman dreams signal:
- Anima activation
- Death awareness
- Transformation calling
- Feminine wisdom needed
- Otherworldly journey
