# Womb (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The womb is the maternal container and place of origin. Represents return to the mother, regression, but also the creative potential and the place where all life begins.

## Key Meanings

- Maternal container
- Origin point
- Birth place
- Safe enclosure
- Regression site
- Creative potential

## Psychological Meaning

- Mother bond
- Return to source
- Regression wish
- Safety seeking
- Life origin
- Fertility womb

## Dream Context

Womb dreams signal:
- Mother regression
- Return to origin
- Safety seeking
- Birth themes
- Creative potential
