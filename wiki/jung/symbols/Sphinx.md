# Sphinx (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The Sphinx is a semi-theriomorphic representation of the mother-imago - the Terrible Mother in disguise. She guards thresholds and asks riddles. The Sphinx represents the enigmatic, unknowable quality of the maternal unconscious and the mystery that must be solved for passage.

Jung sees the Sphinx as the embodiment of mystery and as a warning of the psychological danger of the maternal pull.

## Key Meanings

- Mother-imago in enigmatic form
- Guardian of thresholds
- Riddle-keeper
- Terrible Mother aspect
- Psychological mystery

## Mythological Context

- Sphinx of Oedipus (asks riddle of identity)
- Egyptian sphinx (guardian, enigmatic)
- Symbol of the unknowable mother

## Dream Context

Sphinx dreams often signal:
- Encounter with mystery
- Riddle to be solved for passage
- Mother-imago recognition
- Threshold or initiation
