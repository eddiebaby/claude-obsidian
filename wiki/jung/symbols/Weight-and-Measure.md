# Weight and Measure (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Weight and measure represent the fundamental principle governing psychic work—the discernment between concrete and symbolic interpretation. The problem of "weight and measure" appears throughout dream analysis: to what extent should a dream image be taken literally vs. symbolically? Feeling (not logic) provides the weighing function that values dream content appropriately.

## Key Meanings

- Psychic discernment
- Proportion principle
- Balance and valuation
- Concrete vs. symbolic
- Feeling-weighted judgment
- Canon standard

## Mythological Context

- Wisdom of Solomon ("measure and weight")
- Alchemical balance
- Divine ordering principle
- Anthropos panton metron ("man is the measure")
- Sapientia dei (God's wisdom)

## Psychological Meaning

- Discrimination function
- Feeling valuation
- Proper proportion
- Integration of opposites
- Psychological weighing
- Canon establishment
