# Light (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Light is consciousness itself emerging from darkness. Represents illumination, knowledge, and divine grace. The rays that penetrate and illuminate the unconscious.

## Key Meanings

- Consciousness and awareness
- Illumination and clarity
- Divine grace and blessing
- Knowledge revealed
- Spiritual light
- Penetrating rays

## Mythological Context

- Divine light principle
- Dawn as rebirth
- Sacred light
- Mystical illumination
- Enlightenment

## Psychological Meaning

- Consciousness emerging
- Knowledge achieved
- Clarity attained
- Divine insight
- Illumination of shadow

## Dream Context

Light dreams signal:
- Consciousness expanding
- Illumination coming
- Mystery revealed
- Spiritual insight
- Knowledge arriving
