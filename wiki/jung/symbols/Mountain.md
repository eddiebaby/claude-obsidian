# Mountain (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The mountain is the high point representing aspiration and achievement. An obstacle to overcome and a place where divine forces dwell. Represents the goal of ascent and spiritual elevation.

## Key Meanings

- High point and aspiration
- Obstacle to overcome
- Divine dwelling place
- Peak achievement
- Spiritual ascent
- Obstacle to separation

## Psychological Meaning

- Goal to reach
- Aspiration embodied
- Hard climb required
- Achievement awaited
- Transcendence path

## Dream Context

Mountain dreams signal:
- Aspiration active
- Climb underway
- Peak near
- Obstacle present
- Ascent path
