# Demon (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

Demons are evil spirits representing possession by unconscious forces and the chaotic side of the psyche. Can be external threats or internal psychological figures that overwhelm consciousness.

## Key Meanings

- Evil principle
- Possession by unconscious
- Chaotic destructive force
- Shadow embodied
- Threat to consciousness
- Underworld inhabitant

## Psychological Meaning

- Shadow possession
- Unconscious forcing its way
- Disowned psychological content
- Breakdown of consciousness
- Regression or possession

## Dream Context

Demon dreams signal:
- Shadow possession fear
- Unconscious breaking through
- Evil force encountered
- Possession or overwhelm
- Integration needed
