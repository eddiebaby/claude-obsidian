# Wooden Rule (Symbol)

## Core Interpretation

The wooden rule (carpenter's rule) represents craftwork and the making of things straight. It is the instrument of order, the measure that brings consciousness. The rule stands for mankind's first attempt to subdue nature—the canonical instrument creating order from chaos. In Monica's dream, standing on the rule means being grounded in order and measure.
