## The Dream

I'm in a desert, getting shown around Phantom Ranch and an impressive desert property. I tell someone it's not far from where my grandfather lived (across from a clock tower), and I found the road from an old garbage dump that was near my grandfather's actual house. It's also Christmas time and everyone is leaving the city in a pattern — today, the people that watch football, etc. I'm driving out of the parking lot with Niky and we see my ex, and she flips us off. After that we go to a hotel room to watch some football and it's packed with guys from my high school. Then Niky texts the realtor who showed us Phantom Ranch and says we stayed, but "at least we weren't drinking with a bunch of guys." I think: why did you send that?

**The road from the garbage dump.** The way into the deep property begins at the refuse heap near the grandfather's actual house: whatever the family had no use for, carted to the edge of the property line — that is where the road starts.
