### Symbols & Amplification

- **Martin throwing tacks in his path** — small sharp things in the walking route, placed by the stepbrother. The day-residue bull is the key: the bull is "brute force, generative power, and the father principle" ([[Bull]], CW 5), lamed by a nail and freed by a guide. On the subjective level, the instinctual masculine is hobbled by small sharp injuries, and Martin is how the dream pictures them.
- **The bus to the refinery** — public transport to night work.
- **Refinery at night, lights and steam, letting the pressure out of the tanks** — industrial transformation of crude material, with the ego doing the most important safety job: controlled release. A refinery is a [[Furnace]] at scale: the vessel where crude becomes fuel. It's canalization in hardware (CW 8 ¶83; [[Libido_Transformation]]).
- **The woman stays with the religious old man who's "getting out of line"** — the anima chooses the senex over the weed. The wise-old-man archetype appears as "priest, teacher" (CW 9i ¶398), here degraded and unruly, and she stays to talk with him.
- **Key in the wallet to the roof lounge, to smoke weed** — a real access credential, used for the old ascent.
