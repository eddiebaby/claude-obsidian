# 2026 — Year Dream Review (year to date)

> **Year to date (partial): 06-25 → 11-01.** 48 of 75 dreams have no associations; none has your verdict. Revisit this page on 2027-01-01.

## The verdict

**2026 was the year the dreams moved the problem from building to belonging. "I can't finish my house" became "whose house is this?"**

The series opened on an unfinished house and a contested inheritance. It spent the summer on capacity (the gun, the water, the anima, the claim) and the fall back on the house it started with: the father's, the stepmother's, the grandparents' sold one. The ego got markedly more capable along the way, from a passive watcher in June to four completed acts in October. What it hasn't yet done is the thing the series asks for most: light the fire, and claim its own work.

## The year's story

It began in Montana with rich, excavated soil and a house Scott couldn't finish, while a shadow-figure finished a bigger one and walked away. A gun got smuggled past metal detectors and won back. For two weeks in July the psyche broke open: the firing boss served him food, a river ran under the courthouse full of fish that wouldn't bite, he signed a job at a farm, and he expelled the third man from the hotel room (the year's only perfect act). Then the dreams became a job description: restore your grandfather's engine, climb with her, here's your road map, here's your claim-check. He did the climbing and stopped at the anchor. All August he was useful on everyone's site but his own. In September the old career came back with a face and the substance came back into his hand. In October the series returned to where it started, the house and the family. He told his father the truth, beat his stepmother and called it unfair, gave his father his silver, found his dead dog, and finally saw a building set right into the rock.

## The initial dream, re-read

Jung held that the first dream of a series often anticipates the whole process. [[2026-06-25-montana-inheritance|06-25 Montana Inheritance]] did. Its day residue was the **stepfamily inheritance**: "doesn't get along with one stepbrother, who's a lawyer; father is getting old; stepmother is sick." Four months later the stepbrother has a name (Martin, 10-01 and 10-26), the stepmother is the most-attacked figure of the fall, and the father gets the son's silver (10-25). The initial dream's two panels were *can't finish my own house* and *the brothers feud over the business while I watch*. October answered the second panel with the end of watching: "I don't like her" (10-01). The first panel is answered only in image so far: glass set into the cliff (10-23).
