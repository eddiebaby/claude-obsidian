## Thread 1 — The Unfinished Structure (study vs. the deal)

The spine of the series, and the declared waking conflict. Montana: *I can't finish my house* — while the shadow finishes a bigger one. Classroom: graduation is easy, but the comfortable part of the psyche doesn't *want* it, because class beats work. Tent: the dream-ego stays in a portable shelter reading the Collected Works while the anima — a *real-estate* dealer, a completer of houses — leaves. The figures who complete and monetize structures are always **others**: Jason Dunn, the posh woman. The completing energy is alive but projected. Taking it back means finishing one thing.

**07-09 development:** the dream-ego issues the work order *itself* — walks out of the fast-food counter world with a rake in hand and thinks, in-dream, *get back to work* ([[2026-07-09-promotion-to-customer]]). First time the completing energy appears as a tool in the ego's own grip rather than in a projection's. The counter-risk is named the same scene: "promotion to customer" — a customer *consumes*; liberation from the counter can slide into pure intake (food, books) instead of production. The rake corrects it: proprietor of your own ground, gathering what has fallen.

**07-11 development — the deal is signed and the move-in happens.** Two structure-firsts in one night: [[2026-07-11-farm-eggs-and-keys]] closes an actual employment *in one conversation* (offer → keys → signed), and [[2026-07-11-mountain-house-cat-snow]] opens with a completed **move-in** — residence taken in a new dwelling, if not one built. The customer-risk inverts cleanly at the farm: he arrives to buy and leaves as *courier* — distribution of existing local value, the consulting business in dream-shorthand. The unfinished-house note still sounds in the triple's first panel: the anima-union happens in the **ex's** house, foreign and stuck ([[2026-07-11-anima-in-exes-house]]) — the soul-life still has no owned address.

## Thread 2 — The Gun: Libido Legalized, Still Unused

The clearest single-image progression in the series ([[Libido_Transformation]]):
1. **06-27** — illegal, smuggled past the persona's metal detectors; triumphantly retrieved; "reclaimed but not integrated." Open question set: *what does the gun become?*
2. **07-02** — the cop-who-is-criminal (corrupt inner authority) is executed; the condemned man's cigarettes pass into the dreamer's hands. The false law that criminalized the energy dies.
3. **07-07** — the gun lies openly on the workplace floor. No detectors, no charge. Legal — and idle, while attention scatters across clipboards.

4. **07-09** — a candidate answer to *what does the gun become*: the **inverter-charger**, found among the dreamer's own locker contents inside the bag of food ([[2026-07-09-promotion-to-customer]]). Not a weapon but a converter — stored DC into usable AC, and the reverse flow to recharge. That is canalization in hardware, and it was the dreamer's property all along, stored inside the old work-world and handed back by the very boss who fired him.

Legitimization was complete by 07-07; 07-09 may have supplied the channeling symbol. Provisional until a future dream shows energy actually *flowing through* it. The standing assignment — [[Active Imagination]] — remains the series' most concrete pending action, now with two images to hold: the gun and the inverter-charger (ask each what it wants to power).

5. **07-10** — a third interface image: the **rod and line**, cast into the river under the courthouse ([[2026-07-10-courthouse-river-fishing]]). The channel is deployed for the first time — line actually in the water — and *no current flows*: fish swarm every cast, nothing bites. The dream stages the pending assignment itself and diagnoses the failure — inherited surface tackle (grandfather's popper, a sight-lure) in water you can't see through, worked from the commentator's position. The flow-through confirmation the watch list wants was shown *failing to start*, with the reason attached.

6. **07-13** — the gun enters **discourse**: collegial gun-talk with the hometown gangster ([[2026-07-13-gangster-safe-cocaine]]) — the ego in conversation with the one figure in the psyche for whom the energy-instrument is an everyday working tool. The thread's question ("what does the gun become?") is now being *discussed with a user* rather than staged in objects.

## Thread 3 — Water in Every State

One substance, five states — a hydrology of the relation to the unconscious ([[Aqua-Permanens]]):
- **Unreachable**: stuck at the first buoy, can't get from the channel to open ocean (06-28; Scott's own note: active-imagination trouble).
- **Overflowing**: 4–5 gallons of milk, uncontained, in the childhood home (06-30) — nourishment present but unheld; a vessel problem.
- **Thrown**: splashing Bart at brunch (07-01) — the unconscious landing on others uninvited.
- **Frozen**: the pond under the fallen tree's roots (07-02) — impossible speed across the hard surface; frictionless contact, dry the whole way.
- **Filling a vessel**: the stone well, slowly (07-03, *provisional*) — the counter-image: containment happening at its own pace.

The task named from every direction: **build the vessel**. Melt, don't skate; hold, don't splash; greet what arrives (the 06-30 foyer). Watch the next water dream for its state — liquid *and contained* would mark a stage change.

**07-08/07-09 development:** 07-08 kept the built-structure-over-water motif (boardwalk, pier — two readings, unresolved). Then 07-09b delivered the series' first full shoreline: **on the sand itself**, no structure, and the seascape is *beautiful* — the relation to the depths has turned aesthetic, not threatening. But no one enters the water, and what comes *out* of it is the cops — skimming the surface on jet skis. The frictionless-gliding mode has been handed to the pursuers while the ego takes the dunes. Liquid-and-contained remains the stage-change marker.

**07-10 development — the marker substantially met.** A sixth state, and the biggest single water-move in the series: a **giant river, liquid, indoors, housed in a built marble hall** ([[2026-07-10-courthouse-river-fishing]]) — liquid *and* contained, in the grandest vessel the psyche could build, sitting directly under the courthouse. And the ego engages the water **directly for the first time**: casting into it, repeatedly, unafraid. The water turns out to be full of **fish** (also a series first) — living contents, eager, swarming every cast, some large. Two qualifiers hold the stage change short of complete: the water is *opaque* (muddy brown, can't see through — prima-materia register), and **no exchange occurs** — nothing bites, nothing passes from water to ego. The relation has moved from unreachable → thrown → frozen → shoreline → *engaged at the surface, no hookup*. Next marker: a bite, or entry into the water.

**07-11 development — the instinct enters the frozen element, joyfully.** Snow returns for the first time since 06-28 — and where that powder was *already tracked* and the ego stuck at the buoy, tonight's snow is fresh and **the cat is in it**, first time, loving it, jumping, exploring, refusing to come indoors ([[2026-07-11-mountain-house-cat-snow]]). The animal soul has no problem with the cold element the ego has circled for weeks — and while it plays there, a **bird lands on its head**: spirit alighting on instinct, a small natural coniunctio, outdoors, unmanaged, while the ego overheats the house from inside. The temperature axis is now explicit at both poles: frozen-and-played-in outside, over-fired vessel inside. Refined marker: the *ego* joining the element the instinct already enjoys.

## Thread 6 — Authority in Transition

Old professor (chimney-smoker mentor) → new TM mentor, poor but deeply respected (06-29) → the lawyer-advocate who forecloses reckoning (06-30) → the storyteller-teacher and the cop-who-is-criminal, shot (07-02) → the old boss working harmlessly at the next desk (07-07). Set that against the 07-07 complaint ("I keep trying to concentrate but I'm distracted"): the series proposed its own remedy a week before naming the symptom.

**07-09, double development.** The Walmart regime composts past de-fanged into *serving*: Sharon, the boss who fired him, hands over the food — and the bag contains his own equipment. Lynn is reduced to two functions, an appeal nobody wants (refused on the spot, individuation named aloud) and a threat priced at **$4** — and the tape measure turned out to be *biographically real*: the actual firing pretext. Scott was reporting ethical violations over the store manager's head; management combed ~8 months of self-checkout footage to find one forgotten $4 item on a ~$300 order and fired the whistleblower on it. The cop-who-is-criminal (07-02) was never abstract — the outer law literally *was* the ethics violator punishing its reporter. The residue isn't a debt; it's the internalized **surveillance court** — the inner auditor that reviews months of footage for one lapse. But the same night the cops physically land for the first time in the series — from the **sea**, on jet skis: the old law is no longer an outer employer; it surfaces from inside, on its own schedule. Shrunk to comedy in one dream, arriving in force in the other — not dead yet.

**07-10 development — under the court, the river.** The very next night the family walks *into* the courthouse seeking redress for a real theft — and the dream reroutes them to the basement, where the building turns out to sit on top of a **giant living river**. The surveillance court is revealed as architecture over the water: the stolen value is recovered by *fishing*, not litigation. And two firsts: the family calls **their own father** (personal positive authority), and — the night after the jet-ski landing — **no cops appear at all**. The law-thread's first police-free dream.

**07-11 development — the first good employer, and she's feminine.** Every employer-figure in the series has been the negative old regime (Sharon, Lynn, the counter). Tonight a known, real, benevolent woman **provides, hires, and entrusts the keys** in one conversation ([[2026-07-11-farm-eggs-and-keys]]) — the positive counter-image to the surveillance court is not a better court but a *farm*: production at biological pace, no footage reviewed. Police-free across all three dreams — two consecutive police-free nights now. One caution: the surveillance *method* survived the court's demise by migrating into the ego itself — the reconnaissance stakeout of the ex's house (07-11a) is the inner auditor's technique applied to his own past.

**07-13 development — the court's counter-pole constellates.** The authority spectrum completes: after the corrupt court and the good farm, the **gangster** ([[2026-07-13-gangster-safe-cocaine]]) — enterprise wholly outside the institutions, armed, self-capitalized, resident in the hometown layer. Third consecutive police-free dream, and this one is *actually criminal*: the surveillance court fails to appear precisely where it would have jurisdiction. Its inner writ is lapsing. The gangster is the shadow-twin of the farm woman — same self-owned-enterprise archetype, renegade costume — and he is met collegially, opening his safe unprompted.

## Thread 8 — Agency: From Can't-Move to Can't-Focus

The [[Structural Dream Analysis]] arc: passive watcher (06-25) → covert action (06-27) → blocked mobility (06-28) → contactful-then-chased (06-29) → escape plus one small grab (06-30) → wall-leap, the peak (07-01) → overshoot then movie-watching abdication (07-02) → decision withheld at the threshold (07-03, provisional) → social ease with attentional failure (07-07). Trend is upward with a characteristic failure at the **middle range — traction**: either too little agency or unusable excess (impossible ice speed, splash landings). The newest development is the important one: the blockage has migrated from the body (buoy, malnourishment, ice) into **attention itself** (clipboards, distraction). The dreams now stage the waking weakness in its own register — in Roesler's terms, the material is ripe.

**07-08 → 07-09 extension:** constraint → self-propelled motion (roller skates), then the series high — verbal confrontation with the negative authority, witty reframe ("thanks for the promotion to customer"), refusal of regression, individuation named in-dream, tool in hand. And the traction gap looks *occupied*: the rake scrapes asphalt, the flight goes through deep dune sand — the ego now takes the friction paths while the **cops** do the frictionless skimming. But a new axis opened the same night: **relational cost**. The escape works and the anima is left standing where the law lands. The failure mode's migration continues: body → attention → **relatedness**.

**07-10 extension — the gap moves to grip.** The ego stance is strong: at the water for the first time, equipped, persistent, unafraid, narrating accurately. But the dream is bracketed by two versions of the same failure — circling without *parking* (street cleaning; the open spots aren't claimable yet) and casting without *hooking* (fish swarm, nothing bites). Contact is fully established; commitment isn't. The migration now reads: body → attention → relatedness → **grip/engagement** — and the in-dream commentary habit ("I remark as much") names the mechanism: observing instead of entering.

**07-11 extension — grip achieved in one register, failed in another.** The triple splits the grip question by domain. Vocational: **executed** — offer heard, keys taken, *signed up*, the series' first completed commitment ([[2026-07-11-farm-eggs-and-keys]]). Relational: still fumbled — the stalled car at the exit from the ex's house, then parked surveillance instead of either the conversation or the departure (07-11a). Inner-work: the ego witnesses receptively (looks up, *sees* the bird land) but manages badly (over-fires the thermostat, tries to recall the playing instinct indoors) (07-11c). The grip capacity exists; it currently engages only where the transaction is simple and the counterpart benevolent. Watch whether the signature-energy spreads to the feeling-domain.
