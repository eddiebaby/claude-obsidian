# Dreamer Context

The standing personal context every dream analysis reads first. These are Scott's own associations, in his words where possible. Add to this page whenever a new figure gets an association, and don't ask again about anything already here.

## Formative biography

- **Mother's death.** Scott's mother died in a **car accident when he was five. He was in the accident.** *(Stated 2026-09-18.)*
- **Grandparents raised him.** After the accident his grandparents moved in to take care of him. He stayed very close to them for the rest of their lives.
  - Analysis implications: the mother archetype is structured by early loss, and the grandparents were the actual parental pair. Read maternal and caretaking figures against this: [[2026-06-25-montana-inheritance|Jesse Adler as mother/sister caretaker]], [[2026-06-30-court-and-overflowing-milk|"Mom doesn't care much" and the overflowing milk]]. Also check **cars** as a possible thread rather than assuming day residue: the dead man in the car (07-02), the stalled car (07-11), the woman asleep in the car as the storm comes in (07-29), and the minor car accident on ice at Big Bear in 07-02's associations.
