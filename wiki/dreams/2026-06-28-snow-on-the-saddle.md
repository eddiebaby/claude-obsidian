# 2026-06-28 Sunday

## The Dream

### Part 1 — the mountain of garbage

I'm climbing a mountain of garbage. I don't remember many details, but the image is clear: a mountain of garbage. *(I think it had something to do with my dream yesterday.)*

### Part 2 — snow on the saddle / the vodka / DTG

I'm here in Hawaii with my friend Ian, who recently visited. We go to the top of the mountain and there's just a little bit of snow. Then a big open landscape — like the Saddle Road between Mauna Loa and Mauna Kea, just one two-lane highway going through — and here we've gotten a lot of snow, maybe 3–4 feet. Not the little sprinkling that was on top of the mountain. There's already a whole set of tracks through the snow, like someone had already run through it — like getting to a ski resort and finding someone already ran through all the fresh powder, through these multiple feet of snow. I show Ian that we do in fact get snow here.

Then I see that someone drank multiple big bottles of the cheap vodka I used to drink — three 1.75 L bottles, maybe 2/3 drunk. I pick them up and put them on top of a shelf. I feel that almost hungover sickness.

Then I see my friend I know from online but have never met in life — I knew it was him, DTG (his nickname). He's walking through an almost turnstile contraption, like getting on a subway. I kick a small empty vodka bottle to him and he kicks it back. We have a few words but I don't remember saying anything important.

### Part 3 — stuck at the buoy / the girlfriend / the ex / the Pepsi

Early morning. I'm trying to get out on the water, into the ocean. I can't make it far — stuck at the first buoy. We're in a channel trying to get out to the open ocean; the beach is on the left, the buoy on the right. *(Maybe a reference to how I'm having trouble with active imagination.)*

I think in the same dream I'm talking to my girlfriend, and she's afraid of me cheating on her — which she has been worried about while she's been away the last week. Later in that dream I do see my ex, which she doesn't like. Nothing happened — I just ran into her at a grocery store (I've been doing gig work, personal shopping). Then later I was helping a Pepsi vendor move 30-packs of Pepsi out of the display. Then I woke.
