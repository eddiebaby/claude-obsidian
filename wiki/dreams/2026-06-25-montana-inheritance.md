# 2026-06-25 Thursday

## The Dream

### Part 1 — Montana / the unfinished house

I'm somewhere in the plains of Montana. It's not the nicest weather, but there's a lot of work being done to the land. I'm working on my part of the property, and Jason Dunn from high school is working on a larger house on the other side of the property. Excavators come through and knock down all the vegetation — there's a lot of raw soil. It's very brown, dark, and rich.

Something happens where I can't finish my house, but Jason Dunn's house is done. Then this girl I used to work with, Jesse Adler, is with Jason. Jason is going to leave the house and donate it — he doesn't want it anymore. Jesse's going to be my new girlfriend or something; we instantly really like each other, and we hadn't shacked up before because I was always in a relationship. Jason's in there basically packing up and giving away all the stuff he doesn't want, particularly some vinyl records. The house is big, with open vaulted ceilings.

We start deciding where we're going to go on vacation later that summer. We're thinking of backpacking and taking the train to a mountain town. I have visions of one in particular where the train pulls right into town and it's just a lovely little walking town. I used to work with Jesse in the photo industry, and I guess she was an anima projection — I was very attracted to her, especially for her intellect and kindness.

*(The cat wakes me up; I fall back asleep into a second dream.)*

### Part 2 — the feuding brothers / glass bar on the water

This dream feels like the TV show Succession, although I've never seen it — but I know it's about a family feuding over who gets a business. In this dream I'm sort of just a passive watcher. One brother wants the company and abruptly comes in thinking he's going to get it, but when he comes to the office he just starts playing video games. One of the other brothers makes a flyer of him playing video games and hands it out to everybody to make him look bad.

I walk down to a building that's on the water. It's very modern, glass on both sides so you can see straight through, and I can see the blue water on the horizon — it reminds me of Lake Tahoe, California. At first I think the building's an office, but when I get in I realize it's some sort of bar. People start coming in and getting drinks. A person with a kid leaves. There's art on the wall, painted directly on the wall — I think it's the Waipiʻo Valley, I'm trying to remember, but I remember a landscape on the wall with the name written there. The brothers are in there squabbling over the flyer we've printed. They also start talking about skydiving, I think. I'm confused as to whether I had an active role or was just a third-party observer.

## Jungian Analysis

*Analyzed 2026-06-25. Personal associations first, then amplification against [[Dream-Analysis-and-Interpretation|Jung's method]] and the [[Symbols of Transformation]] / [[Mother Archetype]] material in the vault.*

### Symbols & Amplification

- **The excavated dark soil (prima materia).** Excavators raze the vegetation; what's left is brown, dark, *rich* earth. This is the alchemical opening move — the field cleared down to fertile raw material before anything can be built. The destruction is not loss; it exposes ground. Read against the day residue (aging father, sick stepmother, inheritance), the unconscious is staging the death of the parental generation as **clearing that makes the soil rich** — death turned toward fertility, not just grief.
- **The house = the Self / the psychic structure you're building.** Building a house is one of the most consistent dream-images for constructing the [[Self (archetype)|Self]]. The blunt fact of the dream: *you can't finish yours.* Jason's — bigger, vaulted, open — gets finished. Hold that without flinching: the dream is naming an unfinished structure.
- **Vinyl records given to "criminals."** Something personal and curated (taste, identity, what you'd keep) is handed off into a disreputable circle. A devaluing or careless dispersal of inherited goods — echoes the waking fear that the inheritance gets handed to the wrong hands.
- **The glass building that's really a bar.** You expect an *office* (work, the business, productive structure) and find a *bar* (consumption, dissolution) — and it's transparent, water and horizon visible straight through. The same drunk/dissolution note as the Thailand memory. Clarity (you can see right through) sits right next to the temptation to dissolve.
- **Waipiʻo Valley painted on the wall, name written.** Home — Hawaii — fixed on the wall, *named*. Roots and belonging are explicitly inscribed in the second dream's space even while the brothers fight over the business.
- **The train into the walking town.** A clean arrival — the track delivers you right into a small, walkable place. An image of an *easy, integrated* future with the anima. Wish, and possibly direction.

### Archetypal Figures

- **Jason Dunn — the [[Shadow]].** Your strongest memory of him is impulsive, transgressive, line-crossing: assault, arrest, criminality. In the dream *he* finishes the bigger house and *he* holds the anima first. That's the shadow carrying exactly the energy your ego can't access — the decisive, aggressive, "finish-it-even-if-it's-ugly" force. You can't complete your house; the disowned part of you can. Jung's order is the point: **you reach the [[Anima and Animus|anima]] through the shadow.** Jesse is "with Jason" before she's with you. Integration runs through that figure, not around him.
- **Jesse Adler — the [[Anima and Animus|Anima]], still fused with the [[Mother Archetype|Mother]].** You name her yourself as an anima projection, and your association is *mother/sister who took care of me.* In Jung's developmental sequence of the anima (Eve → Helen → Mary → Sophia), a caretaker/maternal figure is the **early, mother-bound stage** — even though the pull toward her *intellect and kindness* reaches toward the later, Sophia (wisdom) pole. She's the bridge figure between the two. The Thailand memory — meeting her again and making a fool of yourself drunk — is the anima encounter going wrong *while unconscious/intoxicated*. The dream offers a corrected take: intimacy finally possible, sober, alone. But it never happened in life because you were "always in a relationship" — the inner feminine kept at arm's length by outer attachments.
- **The video-game brother — the puer / unearned-inheritance figure.** Wants the company, strides in to claim it, then just plays games. The infantile claim on inheritance without the work. He gets shamed by the flyer. Notably you are a **passive observer** of the whole feud — which is precisely your stance toward the real inheritance situation with your stepbrother.

### Compensation / What the Unconscious Is Saying

Waking life frames inheritance as **conflict, loss, and a fight with a lawyer-stepbrother**. Both dreams compensate that:
- the contested estate appears as a finished house **freely given** (Jason donates it, doesn't want it) — inheritance as gift, not battle;
- the dead-cleared ground is **rich, not barren**;
- the feud you can't control in life, you watch with **detachment** in the dream.

But the compensation comes with a condition the dream states twice: **you don't finish your own house, and you only watch the brothers fight.** The unconscious is reframing the death/inheritance anxiety as fertile ground *and* flagging passivity — the same focus/follow-through gap that shows up in your waking work. Two dreams in one night both circle "who completes and claims the inheritance," and in both you are the one who *doesn't* finish or *doesn't* act.

### Movement Toward Individuation

This reads as a second-half-of-life turn ([[Individuation]]): the parental generation's growth is being razed, the soil is rich, the opus can begin. The tasks the dream lays out:
1. **Finish your own house.** Reclaim the shadow's completing-energy (Jason) — the decisive, finish-it force you've projected as "criminal." This is the same thing as the monetization/follow-through weakness; the dream put it in one sentence: *I can't finish my house.*
2. **Move the anima past the maternal stage.** Don't meet her drunk (Thailand / the bar). The sober, alone intimacy in the dream is the corrected image — relating to the inner feminine consciously rather than being possessed by her when your guard is down.
3. **Stop being only an observer of the feud.** Decide what role you actually take in the real inheritance question with your stepbrother.

### Open Questions

- The girl is delivered to you *by* the shadow. Where in waking life is the decisive/transgressive energy you've labeled "not me" but secretly need to finish things?
- Jason gives away the records *and* the house — he doesn't want the inheritance. Is some part of you wanting to refuse/walk away from the inheritance fight entirely? Is that freedom or avoidance?
- Office that turns out to be a bar: where are you expecting to do *work* but actually going to *dissolve*?
- In the feud you can't tell if you're a participant or a watcher. In the real situation — which do you want to be?
