# 2026-06-29 Monday

## The Dream

### Part 1 — the new mentor / cigarettes

I'm in New York City and I have a new mentor who's going to teach me transcendental meditation. James Monaghan, my old professor, was part of the same social circle and had been my mentor — but now I have this new mentor. We go around the city. His car breaks down on the side of the road and I offer to fix it. I don't remember a ton, but there was a truck in the road. A lot of the dream revolved around smoking — rolling cigarettes, smoking cigarettes, cleaning out ashtrays. (James, my old professor and mentor, did smoke like a chimney.) I had great admiration for this new mentor. He didn't seem to have much, but I had a lot of respect for him.

### Part 2 — the bar, the minivan, the chase

In another section, me and Drew and Kenny are out at a bar in New York City. We go outside to smoke and start walking down the street. Drew goes to his car — a green minivan — and the door is open. I ask if he left it like that; he says no. He locks it and closes it.

The dream takes a new tone and it gets dark. He goes to open his apartment building. There's a black man there, presumably trying to rob him. Drew yells at him to get away, but the man starts chasing us. I say "call the cops." We start running down the block, and I wake.
