**What it's doing:** The stepmother line hits its violent peak: he beats her, and the dream has him call it unfair. That word is the dream's own verdict.

### Series Context

- **Thread 9 escalation.** Interruption (09-19) → competition (10-01a) → declaration and exit (10-01b) → violence (tonight). Repair comes on 10-25, through the father.
- **Violence line.** War 09-22, fist-fight 09-24, now the beating.
- **The mailboxes.** On [[2026-11-01-niky-finds-messages|11-01]] the theme returns as messages discovered.

### Symbols & Amplification

- **Beating her "unfairly"** — the ego commits the act and judges it in the same breath. The rage exceeds its object.
- **Walkway → basement under the deck → the mailboxes** — from the path down into the underground, ending at the place where messages arrive. She's hurled into the communication point.
- **The negative mother.** The stepmother carries the mother archetype (CW 9i ¶155) in its rejected form ([[Terrible_Mother]]). Jung's warning applies: "a mother-complex is not got rid of by blindly reducing the mother to human proportions" (CW 9i ¶172). Hitting the person doesn't touch the archetype she's carrying.

### Archetypal Figures

- **Stepmom** — carrier of the negative-mother complex.
- **The beating ego** — shadow aggression, the same force as 09-24's fight.

### Compensation / What the Unconscious Is Saying

If the waking stance is polite distance ("everybody thinks it's absurd," 09-19), the dream shows the rage underneath, and labels it unfair. The excess is the clue: some of this anger belongs to an older loss than anything she did.

### Competing Reading

Objective: real anger at the stepmother (inheritance, coming between him and his dad). Fully legitimate. But the dream's own word "unfairly" separates what she did from the size of the anger.

### Movement Toward Individuation

Sort the anger: what she actually did versus what the rage is about. The part that exceeds her belongs to the mother who died. Given his mother died when he was five, the stepmother occupies a place no one could fill fairly.
