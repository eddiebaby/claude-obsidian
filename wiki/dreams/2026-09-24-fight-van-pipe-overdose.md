## Jungian Analysis

> Analyzed without associations — lower confidence.

**What it's doing:** It says concealment kills. He hides the stash to protect himself, and the other person overdoses. Then the cops search the van anyway.

### Series Context

- **Thread 7.** On 09-08 the substance was taken for the first time. Now the stash is in the van and he's hiding it. The weed/dealing line runs from here through 10-26, 10-28 and 10-31.
- **Police return.** The study called enforcement "gone" and was already corrected once. Here the cops are searching his vehicle.
- **Violence line.** A fist-fight opens the dream, two days after the beach war.

### Symbols & Amplification

- **Fighting "somebody"** — an unnamed same-sex opponent: the shadow in its plainest form ([[Shadow]]: the shadow typically appears as a threatening same-sex figure).
- **The van** — a carrying vehicle, work/communal capacity. That's where the stash is.
- **Hiding the marijuana and pipe** — the ego's act is concealment, not use and not disposal.
- **The other person ODs** — the cost of hiding lands on someone else. On the subjective level (CW 8 ¶509) the overdosing figure is a part of him that takes the overdose when the substance question is hidden instead of faced.
- **Cops searching the van** — what's concealed gets searched anyway. Jung to Bill W.: the craving is a thirst for wholeness, "spiritus contra spiritum" ([[Spiritus-contra-spiritum]]).

### Archetypal Figures

- **The opponent / the overdosing person** — probably one figure: the shadow fought, then the shadow dying of what was hidden.

### Compensation / What the Unconscious Is Saying

Against a sober self-image (sober over a year, per the study), the dream says the stash still exists in the vehicle, and the reflex is still to hide it.

### Competing Reading

Objective: memory of a real overdose or a real search from the using years. Or a simple anxiety dream about being caught. Associations would decide. Without them I favor the subjective reading, because the dream stages the ego's own act (hiding) as the cause.

### Movement Toward Individuation

Stop hiding. Disclosure is the move the dream withholds.
