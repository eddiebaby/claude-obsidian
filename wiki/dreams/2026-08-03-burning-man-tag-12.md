## The Dream

I was going to Burning Man with Ian and Abby (friends). I had to be careful not to get in trouble. A bouncer at the door gave me a tag with a "12" on it that could be used to help find my bag if I lost it.

Later my friends were building houses on the outskirts of the Burning Man city. The construction was still happening.

Later I was going to a party and wanted to bring my figaro silver chain, and was kind of disappointed I wasn't more cut, as I gained a little weight recently.

I needed to get my backpack so I went back to the entrance.

The number 12 stayed with me.

### Symbols & Amplification

**Twelve.** In that city the addresses run 2:00 to 10:00; twelve is the one hour with no streets and no camps — the open corridor, the axis where the Man stands and the Temple waits. The bouncer handed over **the coordinate of the center** as a claim check for what the ego carries and might lose. Aion (¶¶351–353): twelve as 3×4, thought-totality married to the earthly one, wholeness in the form of time; the city as mandala; the point of walking its streets is that you are circling something.

**The bouncer.** After weeks of ungoverned-space dreams, the psyche supplies a threshold guardian who is *not punishing* — first thought on arrival was caution about trouble, and the authority met answers with a token instead. **Going back for the backpack.** The only decisive movement in the dream: choosing the thing carried over the thing worn.
