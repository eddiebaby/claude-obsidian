## Jungian Analysis

> Analyzed without associations — lower confidence.

**What it's doing:** It says the truth he already knows: Angela never stopped seeing the man before him. Then it lets him get out of her house with all his baggage. On 07-11a the getaway stalled.

### Series Context

- **The ex's house, exited.** On [[2026-07-11-anima-in-exes-house|07-11a]] union happened in the ex's house, then a stalled getaway and parked surveillance. On [[2026-07-28-wyoming-storage-two-women|07-28b]] agency reached the departure and dissolved into appetite. Tonight the appetite is frustrated (the promised favor withheld) and the departure *completes*: elevator down, car, airport.
- **Chicago.** On [[2026-07-10-courthouse-river-fishing|07-10]] Scott called it the city he "missed out on the most": Thread 5's unlived city.
- **Angela's October line.** 10-13 she records his plate; tonight what was on file comes out.

### Symbols & Amplification

- **Cold Chicago winter after the festival** — the party's over, the season is cold.
- **The whole second floor, bought by the guy before me** — the ex housed by the rival's money. Her dwelling is someone else's purchase.
- **The details that make it obvious** — betrayal revealed in her own telling. Scott: "pretty much true in real life too." The dream confirms what was known, not a new suspicion.
- **Two people move in; he's annoyed** — occupation by newcomers. The space is no longer his to be in.
- **Frantically packing three, four, five suitcases** — the baggage, literally, and more of it than he thought.
- **The promised favor never given** — the appetite stays unpaid, and he leaves anyway.
- **Elevator down → car → airport** — descent and departure, carried out.
- **NYC, someone selling drugs, I needed a parka** — the old city, cold, with the dealer's warmth on offer. What he actually needs is a coat.

### Archetypal Figures

- **Angela** — the ex as revealer: she tells the truth and he goes.
