## The Dream

> Present tense, first person. Raw capture.

I'm going to a brunch with my brother, Bart. Beforehand I'm doing something kind of menial — I'm at a bar or a countertop, ordering something. Then I see my brother down below. I run down toward him, but the table in the middle is covered in a bunch of water. I run down along a stone wall and jump over onto the table, splashing. That's all I really remember.

## Jungian Analysis

> Grounded in the vault corpus. See Sources.

### Dramatic Structure

Exposition: the dream-ego occupied with something menial, ordering at a counter. Peripeteia: he spots Bart *below* and moves toward him. The obstacle: the shared table is flooded with water. Lysis (abrupt): he vaults the stone wall and lands **splashing** on the table. No resolution beyond the splash — the dream cuts on the impact, not on any reaction or repair.

### Dream-Ego Agency ([[Structural Dream Analysis]])

This is a high-agency dream — a **mobility** pattern (ego moving purposefully toward a destination, Bart) shading into **social interaction** (working to make contact). On Roesler's scale that's the active end (4–5), the "in-control" pole. Worth marking against the series: [[2026-06-25-montana-inheritance]] had you as a *passive watcher* (pattern 1). Here you jump the wall. **The agency is rising.** That's the prospective signal to track.

### Symbols & Amplification

- **Water in the middle of the table.** The [[Aqua-Permanens|water of the unconscious]] — the substance that "kills and vivifies simultaneously," standing between spirit and matter. It sits exactly on the shared surface: the brunch table, the place of communion/shared meal, is flooded with unconscious content. The meeting-place *is* the unconscious.
- **The stone wall you run along and jump.** A conscious boundary/defense. You don't walk through a gate — you leap the barrier. The crossing is athletic, forced, not negotiated.
- **The splash.** Involuntary irruption. You don't hand Bart the water; you land in it and it flies onto him. Your own association is exactly right: this is the unconscious hitting him uninvited.

### Archetypal Figures

**Bart, on the subjective level, is a part of you** — the skeptical, rationalist, extraverted brother-in-yourself who thinks the inner work is madness. Read that way, the splash isn't only about your literal brother. It's your own conscious/rational side getting doused by unconscious material and *not liking it*. The dream stages your internal resistance to what you're living and projects it onto the fraternal figure. The "you're losing your mind" verdict is partly your own ego's nervousness about how much unconscious water you're now standing in.

### Compensation / What the Unconscious Is Saying

Compensation asks: what waking attitude is this balancing? In waking life you tried to *carefully explain* the alchemical cycle — a controlled, didactic transmission. The dream refuses that image. It shows you not explaining but **splashing** — an uncontained overflow that lands on someone who didn't ask to get wet. The likely corrective: your transmission of the unconscious to Bart (and to the Bart in you) is arriving as *inundation, not offering*. The eagerness is real and the reach succeeds — you do get to him, you clear the wall — but the mode is a soaking, not a shared cup. The dream is less "your brother is closed" and more "notice the form your enthusiasm takes: it splashes."

### Movement Toward Individuation

The generous read pairs with the [[Nigredo]] material you were literally trying to teach: you're early in an opus, and early-stage water/blackness spills before it's contained. The task the dream points at is the **vessel** — holding the water rather than throwing it. Containment, not volume.

### Open Questions

- When you land splashing, do you *want* to reach Bart, or to convert him? Those are different aims and the dream may be flagging the second.
- Is "Bart" here more your literal brother, or the skeptic inside you who suspects the whole Jungian turn is a distraction from making money? (Compare your own CLAUDE.md tension.)
