# Dream Inbox

Fast capture from your phone (Obsidian mobile) or a voice-memo transcript. Don't format anything, just dump it under a date line. Then say `log dreams` in Claude: each entry becomes a proper dream note, goes through the association questions, and is cleared from here.

Format: a line with `## YYYY-MM-DD`, then the raw text. Add `ctx:` for what's live in your life, if you have it.
