## The Dream

I'm in New York, working in the photo industry. Leaving one of the studios I get on the elevator with some Russian male models. We size each other up and I kind of get in their face, but they defuse the situation and calm me down. We're cool. So I start taking them on an adventure.

We go to a section of the city next to the park. There are impressive buildings and I start to give an architecture tour. We go into one blue art-deco mosaic building and each room gets more grand, but we have to crawl through smaller and smaller cramped openings to get through the rooms — like crawling through passages in caves. The rooms are very beautiful and grand.

In the final section I'm with my friend Ian Fishman and he's crying. He's leaving.

**Remembered dream (~1 year ago, same dream-NYC):** With John Gillan and his NYC friends. We eat LSD, light off enormous amounts of fireworks, and commit arson — one dumpster set up in a massive blaze. I felt out of control but powerful.

## Waking Associations

- Ian is a real friend; we left NYC within a few years of each other and I saw him recently here in Hawaii.
- The most stunning part of the dream was the architecture.
