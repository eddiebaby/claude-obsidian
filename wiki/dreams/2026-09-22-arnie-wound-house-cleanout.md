## Jungian Analysis

> Analyzed without associations — lower confidence.

**What it's doing:** The same night as the war, it puts Scott and Niky back together, doing the housework of the past. The dead dog comes along with a wound to the bone, and nobody does anything about it.

### Series Context

- **Same night as [[2026-09-22-beach-war-separation|the beach war]].** The war splits the couple; this panel shows them together, clearing a house. Read the two as one statement.
- **Dog thread.** 09-12 dog-creature → 09-19 matching dogs → tonight the *dead* dog, wounded. Arnie returns on [[2026-10-23-bedstuy-bus-futon-arnie|10-23]], lost and then found.
- **House thread.** Clearing a house echoes the inheritance clearing of 06-25 and anticipates the sold grandparents' house of 10-23.

### Symbols & Amplification

- **Cleaning out a house** — the end of a residence: sorting what stays and what goes. Done as a couple.
- **Arnie, dead in waking life, present** — an instinct-companion from an earlier chapter returns. The dog is the psychopomp, the guide who gathers scattered pieces ([[Dog]], from CW 5). Here the guide himself is broken.
- **Wound through to the skull** — the head opened to the bone. It's described flatly, without affect, and that flatness is part of the image. Loss of soul is pictured as a dog running off from its master (CW 9i ¶213). Here the dog is still present but damaged.

### Archetypal Figures

- **Arnie** — animal-soul from the past, wounded at the head.
- **Niky** — partner in the clearing.

### Compensation / What the Unconscious Is Saying

Next to the war panel's separation, this panel says the couple's real work is clearing the old house. What the clearing uncovers is an old wound nobody has tended.

### Competing Reading

Simple grief: Arnie's death not fully mourned, reactivated by the clean-out. Or the head wound is instinct injured by too much head: the study-heavy months, life lived from the skull. I can't choose without knowing when and how Arnie died (backlog D4).

### Movement Toward Individuation

Look at the wound instead of past it. The dog came back; the dream wants him seen.
