## The Dream

### Part 1 — the dive bar becomes a cathedral

First, we're in a dumpy strip-mall dive bar. It's one of the guys' birthday. I suggest we go to the LA museum and then go wander around, do something fun. But he doesn't want to go to a museum. We [?] walk outside and it's no longer a strip mall. It's a giant Gothic cathedral like the one in Cologne, or the Duomo. I take a video because I want to send it to Niky, show her how beautiful it is. It's massive, even bigger than either of those churches. I go get on a bike and start riding it backwards.

### Part 2 — the DoorDash party on the Riviera

I'm doing DoorDash deliveries and one of the people sends me pictures of all these beautiful women that are going to be there, and they say come join the party. I also have my dog. I wind up hanging out, meeting some of the women, and they're super nice and bring me in. They're very rich, a beautiful property, and it's right down the street from me, but it's like the Riviera or something. You can look out and see beautiful architecture on both sides of the river. I start talking to this one woman and we kind of start flirting, talking about life. She loves the dog, and so do I. We sit down in this big chaise lounge and the dog curls up next to us, and then we start talking to this other guy about Thai chili peppers and khat [?]. How good! I remind him that they're called bird's eye chilies, and then he says how he loves how sharp they are. I fumble over the word capsaicin. I look out over the architecture and it's gorgeous. I keep talking with the woman and say I just live up the road, [up?] my property, and she says she lives up there too. We talk about the big obnoxious house that is green, and she says, oh no, I don't live where you live. I never go up that far.

### Symbols & Amplification

- **Dive bar → giant Gothic cathedral** — the profane turns into the sacred by walking out the door ([[Enantiodromia]]; [[Numinous Experience]]). The birthday guy refused the museum; the numinous arrives anyway, bigger than culture's best examples.
- **Video for Niky** — the first impulse at the numinous is to share it with the relationship. The photographer's eye in service of love, not a client.
- **Riding a bike backwards** — after the peak, regressive or playful motion: forward travel facing what's behind.
- **DoorDash delivery → invited in by rich, kind women** — the courier admitted to wealth "right down the street," the Riviera next door.
- **The woman loves the dog; the dog curls up by them** — after 09-28's dog fighting the cops, here the anima welcomes the instinct.
- **Bird's-eye chilies, "how sharp," capsaicin fumbled** — fire as pleasure, the burning compound unpronounceable. The calcinatio thread's agent, met socially and not used.
- **"I don't live where you live. I never go up that far."** — her ground is the lowland Riviera; his is up the road, past the big obnoxious green house. The distinction is the dream's last word.

### Compensation / What the Unconscious Is Saying

Against a frugal, remote life up the hill, the dream shows wealth and beauty just down the road, welcoming him as a guest. And it has the woman mark the difference: she doesn't go up to where he lives. His ground is his.

### Competing Reading

Wish-fulfillment: rich beautiful women, a mansion, a cathedral. That's true on the surface, but the dream's last line refuses the fantasy (she won't come up), which a wish dream wouldn't do.

### Movement Toward Individuation

Record the numinous and share it. Then be a guest without wanting to move in: the dream locates him up the road, on his own land.
