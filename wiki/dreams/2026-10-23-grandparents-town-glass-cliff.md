> Imported 2026-11-01 from the Claude.ai Dreams project. Reported 2026-10-23 06:39 HST.

## The Dream

In this one I was in the town my grandparents lived in. But it had changed drastically. It had turned into some kind of hipster Brooklyn type place. I started walking around the town and I saw this nice new hotel that had just opened, and there were two men that looked like twin brothers going to their room. They had mustaches. I left that sort of strip-mall-looking area and started walking around the town more. I couldn't help but think how sad it was that they sold my grandparents' house. If my family had just kept it for a couple years I probably would have moved there, or kept it myself as a vacation house. I could have stayed in New York City and also lived there. But I was only 20 and didn't really know anything about how the world worked.

So I keep walking around the town and I come across a cool, kind of hipster-y vintage store. They sold a lot of jewelry. I'm looking in there for something to get Niky. And I see a really beautiful woman. She distracts me. I go outside. I look up and there are these beautiful rock cliff faces above the building. They're almost perfectly vertical, like the blasted rock along the side of a highway. Then on the other side I see some more, and I'm in a valley. There's some other store across there, I think a denim store, and it's set perfectly into the cliffside. Very modern architecture, all glass. I notice how well the glass fits right into the side of the cliff, these big boulders almost, and how seamless the architecture is.
