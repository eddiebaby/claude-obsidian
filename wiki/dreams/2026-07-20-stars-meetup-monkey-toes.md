# 2026-07-20 Monday

> Backlog ingest 2026-09-15: two dreams, one night, logged in the Claude app Dreams project. Analysis ported and condensed from that session.

## The Dream

**First dream.** I'm with friends from my college days, Nate and Griffin, visiting them in San Francisco. We talk about my childhood friend that they don't know — Keith Rukowski. Keith is doing well for himself after being a monk in the woods or something. We go to an event, sort of like a Meetup, and we're trying to see the stars on the ceiling of this building, but we have to turn the lights out. By the time we get the lights out, the sun's already coming up and we can't see the stars anymore. I try to give the address for a different Meetup where we can see the ocean, but it falls flat — nobody seems to care — but I put it on the board anyway. Leaving, I bump into one of the cooks and we have a bit of a physical altercation, chasing each other around, but not in a scary way. More like a mosh pit.

**Second dream.** With Niky at a shop with greeting cards. I pick one up — something about monkey toes — and the joke doesn't land. I bring it to the clerk and they don't get it either; they invent an excuse for the joke but don't know what it means. Niky says she has monkey toes and picks stuff up with them.

Panel two: manufactured sentiment fails (the card) answered by living instinct (Niky's toes). Both panels run the same engine: **dead collective container → living individual fact.**
