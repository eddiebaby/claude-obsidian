## The Dream

There's two different women in Wyoming. I left the first one, and then I was very erotically attracted to the second one and we kept grabbing on each other. There was something to do with my storage space out there, and later in the day I got a call about it. It was as if the dream was forward-looking.

## Jungian Analysis

### Dramatic Structure

Choice (leaving the first woman) → possession (the grabbing) → anchor-image (the storage unit) → outer echo (the same-day call). The agency curve is the diagnostic: a willed departure immediately swallowed by compulsion — per [[Structural Dream Analysis]], the ego-position collapsing from actor to acted-upon *within one scene*.

- **Wyoming** — the high, dry, emptied interior: the opposite pole of green Hawaii / where the belongings sit — the biographical layer between lives (the Blackfeet years surface three days later, 07-31: Wyoming/Montana is the *no-rules country* of his history) / libido's cold-storage climate.
- **The storage unit** — paid for, unopened, another climate: suspended life, deferred rather than discarded / the series' vessel-thread inverted: not a container that transforms ([[Vessel-Symbol]]) but one that merely *holds* — rent without process; the safe (07-13) at least belonged to someone; the unit is anonymous holding / candidate for the most literal dream-task of the arc: open it or end the lease.

- **The same-day phone call** — [[jung-synchronicity]]: the scarab pattern — an image constellated in the unconscious surfacing hours before the outer event that matches it; bound by meaning, not cause / what it certifies is the *charge on the unit*: this complex is live and pressing toward consciousness now.

### Archetypal Figures

The paired women as split anima: the held-and-dulled aspect and the charged-and-anonymous aspect — the same doubling that returns at the glass door (09-12). While libido is warehoused, she can only appear as compulsion; the unit and the grabbing are one system.

### Compensation / What the Unconscious Is Saying

Against the tidy narrative of the completed move (mainland closed, island life on): a rented box of undissolved past still draws monthly payment — financial and psychic. The unconscious pairs it with ungovernable hunger to show the bookkeeping: **stored ≠ resolved; suspended libido converts to fascination.** The phone call is the psyche escalating from image to event: *this one, now.*

**Objective level:** the unit is real, the call was real. The dream-task is unusually concrete: inventory it — physically going through what's stored (keep/sell/burn) would be arc-work performed with the hands, the exact analog of the three-winged house's records-vs-flowers sorting (07-23, same night's neighbor dream).

**Subjective level:** the unit holds the unlived/unmourned middle period (post-NYC, pre-Hawaii); the two women are its stored charge — what was erotically, vocationally unfinished in those years, still paying negative rent.

### Movement Toward Individuation

Freeing the warehoused quantum — by literal sorting or by grief-work on that period — is the prerequisite for the anima appearing related rather than compulsive.
