### Symbols & Amplification

- **The gay escapade** — on the subjective level a same-sex figure is the shadow ([[Shadow]]: the shadow is same-sex; the anima contrasexual). Erotic union with the shadow is the most intimate form of meeting it. Read that way, the dream isn't primarily about orientation. It stages *contact* with the disowned masculine, under intoxication.
- **Cocaine, drinking, hoping for vodka** — the old route to intensity, all of it at once. Jung's formula to Bill W.: the craving is the spirit sought in the wrong form, *spiritus contra spiritum* ([[Spiritus-contra-spiritum]]).
- **The apocalyptic street, rock strewn, sliding** — the aftermath landscape. The ground broken up, no traction.
- **The woman in the bodysuit, curled by a tree, rolls to show herself, rolls back** — the anima abandoned in the wreckage, in the corner with a [[Tree]] (the tree as mother/Self symbol). She shows her face and withdraws.
