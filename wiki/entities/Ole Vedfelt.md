# Ole Vedfelt

## Overview

Danish Jungian psychoanalyst (based in Hillerød, Denmark) and one of contemporary analytical psychology's most systematic **integrative dream theorists**. Vedfelt's project is to reconcile the historically warring dream-theory schools — Freudian, Jungian, existential, cognitive — with each other and with neuroscience, by treating the psyche as a **[[Cybernetic Dream Theory|cybernetic, self-optimizing information network]]**. Best known for delineating the **[[Ten Core Qualities of Dreams]]** and for a body of work arguing that dreams express an "unconscious intelligence" that "knows more than you think."

## Key Facts

- Key book (English): *A Guide to the World of Dreams — An Integrative Approach to Dreamwork* (Routledge, 2017). Earlier works include *The Dimensions of Dreams* (1994/2002), *Consciousness* (1996/2019), and *Unconscious Intelligence: You Know More Than You Think* (2002/2018).
- Central paper in the vault: "[[vedfelt2020-integration-vs-conflict-dream-theory|Integration versus conflict between schools of dream theory and dreamwork]]," *Journal of Analytical Psychology* (2020), 65(1):88–115 — the compact statement of the ten core qualities + the [[Fourfold Brain]] model.
- Coined the term **[[Supramodal Space]]** (2001) for the multi-modal field of dreamwork in which image, body-sensation, movement, emotion, and thought are given equal attention.
- Frames good dreamwork as **"cybernetic reorganization"**: strengthening resourceful neural/psychic connections and weakening self-destructive ones, guided by the network's own drive toward higher-order self-organization.
- Draws heavily on affective neuroscience (Panksepp, Solms & Turnbull, Damasio, Schore) and on parallel-distributed-processing / complex-systems theory (Rumelhart, Ashby, Wiener, Luhmann, Edelman) — an unusually science-literate voice within analytical psychology.
- Practices and supervises across a wide range of dreamwork settings (clinical, community dream groups, social dream matrix, spiritual traditions), which is the experiential base for his integration thesis.

## Position in the Field

- A **breadth-first integrator** where [[Christian-Roesler]] is a depth-first empiricist. Both represent the contemporary evidence-minded wing of Jungian dream work; Vedfelt synthesizes many schools + neuroscience into ten qualities, while Roesler operationalizes one narrow, testable variable ([[Structural Dream Analysis|dream-ego agency]]). They converge on dreams tracking the real state and movement of the psyche.
- Extends [[C.G. Jung|Jung's]] constructive/prospective view of dreams (dreams as anticipatory, resource-bearing, oriented to development) rather than the Freudian disguise-and-censorship model, but explicitly refuses to discard Freud, folding psychoanalytic findings into the network frame.

## Connections

- Author of the [[Ten Core Qualities of Dreams]] and the [[Fourfold Brain]] model.
- His [[Cybernetic Dream Theory]] is the frame; [[Supramodal Space]] is its clinical method.
- Uses [[Active Imagination]], drawing, and body-sensing as primary dreamwork tools.
