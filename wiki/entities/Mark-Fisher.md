# Mark Fisher

British cultural theorist and critic (1968–2017). *Capitalist Realism* (2009): "it is easier to imagine the end of the world than the end of capitalism." Argues capitalism doesn't merely dominate the present but **colonizes the imagination of time itself**, cancelling the future — tomorrow becomes nothing but an endlessly remixed version of today. Cited in [[yago2026-hour-that-never-comes-time-that-remains]] as one of three namings (with Pelbart and [[Byung-Chul Han]]) of the same wound underlying [[The Time That Remains]]: the stiffening of temporality where the subject "no longer dreams because they no longer believe in a symbolic space where dreams can find realization."
