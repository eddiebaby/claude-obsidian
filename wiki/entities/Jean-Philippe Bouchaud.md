# Jean-Philippe Bouchaud

## Overview

French physicist and one of the founding figures of econophysics — the application of statistical-physics methods to financial markets. Chairman of [[Capital Fund Management]] (CFM), the Paris-based quantitative hedge fund, and affiliated with the Institut Louis Bachelier and the Académie des Sciences. Senior/corresponding author on [[kurth2026-trend-following-demise]], the paper explaining the post-2008 collapse of short-term trend-following via a market-microstructure mechanism.

## Key Facts

- Affiliations listed in the paper: Capital Fund Management (23 Rue de l'Université, Paris), Institut Louis Bachelier (Econophysics Lab), and Académie des Sciences.
- Co-author of *Trades, Quotes and Prices: Financial Markets Under the Microscope* (Cambridge University Press, 2018) — the standard reference on market microstructure and price impact cited repeatedly in [[kurth2026-trend-following-demise]] for the square-root impact law and the general theory of aggressive-flow-to-price translation.
- Prior work directly underlying this paper's framework: Tóth, Lemperiere, Deremble, De Lataillade, Kockelkoren, Bouchaud (2011), "Anomalous price impact and the critical nature of liquidity in financial markets" (Physical Review X) — the square-root impact model used to reject the capacity-constraint hypothesis (H1).
- Co-author of Van der Beck, Bouchaud, Villamaina (2024), "Ponzi funds" (arXiv 2405.12768) — develops the self-fulfilling impact-loop framing in the ETF context, which [[kurth2026-trend-following-demise]] extends to trend-following/CTA strategies.
- Co-author of Lempérière, Deremble, Seager, Potters, Bouchaud (2014), "Two centuries of trend following" — the historical documentation of trend's long-run robustness that motivates the puzzle this 2026 paper addresses.
- Co-author of Bouchaud, Krueger, Landier, Thesmar (2019), "Sticky expectations and the profitability anomaly" (Journal of Finance) — cited as one of the behavioural-underreaction alternatives to the impact-loop mechanism.
- Co-author of Patzelt and Bouchaud (2018), "Universal scaling and nonlinearity of aggregate price impact in financial markets" (Physical Review E) — microstructural evidence for the return-vs-trade-imbalance relationship central to Section 6.3 of the 2026 paper.

## Role in kurth2026-trend-following-demise

Senior author providing the theoretical framing (self-fulfilling impact loop, price-impact mechanism) and the connection to his own body of prior work on market microstructure and impact functions. The paper's central interpretive claim — that trend's profitability and its very existence are two faces of the same impact-mediated feedback loop — extends the Van der Beck-Bouchaud "Ponzi funds" framework from ETFs to CTA-style trend-following.
