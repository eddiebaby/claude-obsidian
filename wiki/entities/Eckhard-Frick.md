# Eckhard Frick

Munich-based Jungian analyst and **Jesuit priest** who accompanies Ignatian retreats ("Spiritual Exercises"), working at the intersection of analytical psychology, psychosomatic medicine, and spiritual care. Author of "[[frick2025-spiritual-path-transformation|The Spiritual Path of Transformation]]" (*Journal of Analytical Psychology*, 2025), which reads Jungian transformation as a circular, spiritual process using [[Wilfred Bion|Bion's]] concept of [[Faith in O|O]], [[Ignatius of Loyola|Ignatian]] [[Discernment of Spirits|discernment of spirits]], and [[Spiritus contra spiritum]]. His dual formation (analyst + Jesuit) makes him a distinctive bridge figure between depth psychology and Christian contemplative practice; he emphasizes that spirituality must not bypass the body and instinct.
