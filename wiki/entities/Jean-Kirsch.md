# Jean Kirsch

## Overview

Contemporary Jungian analyst whose worked clinical case [[zeng2023-i-ching-jungian-application|Zeng (2023)]] uses to show all three of Jung's I Ching dimensions operating at once in the consulting room.

## The Case

A client's plan to leave for Jungian training in Zurich triggered Kirsch's own unrecognized wish for his growth. Consulting the **[[I Ching Divination|I Ching]]** — addressing it directly as an animated interlocutor ("Respond, please, I Ching") — she drew **hexagram 14, Dayou / "Possession in Great Measure."** Zeng reads the case as displaying:

- **Psychical** — treating the oracle as a being she can speak to;
- **Psychological** — Wilhelm's commentary surfacing her countertransference;
- **Physical** — the client did in fact go on to complete his Jungian training.

## Zeng's Critique

It is unclear whether Kirsch's quotations come from the original I Ching text or from Wilhelm's commentary, and she conflates the Chinese ***junzi*** (君子, conventionally "superior man") with the Jungian **wise old man / sage** archetype — a translation slippage, since *junzi* does not carry the "superior" connotation in Chinese. A representative instance of the intercultural-communication problems Zeng argues must be cleared before the I Ching can be adopted responsibly as a Jungian tool.
