# Andrew Samuels

British Jungian analyst; Professor Emeritus of Analytical Psychology, University of Essex. Prominent figure in the "political psyche" strand of post-Jungian thought, and a leading voice in the contemporary reckoning with Jung's writing on race.

Cited in: [[JOAP-68-1-Charura-Bushell-Duoethnographic-Colonialism]] (*JAP* 68/1, 2023).

---

## Relevant Works (as cited in the source)

- Samuels, A. (2018). "Jung and 'Africans': a critical and contemporary review of some of the issues." *International Journal of Jungian Studies*, 10(4), 23-34.
- Samuels, A. (2019). "Open letter from a group of Jungians on the question of Jung's writings on and theories about 'Africans'." *Jung Journal*, 13(1), 27-34.

The 2019 Open Letter, which Samuels led, acknowledges "the lack of regard in Jung of the psyche of black people" while also naming the ongoing structures of discrimination and white domination present in many psychotherapeutic and analytic training institutions — not treating the critique of Jung as separable from a critique of the institutions that followed him.

---

## Role in the Source Paper

[[Divine Charura]] and [[Stephen Bushell]] take an explicit position relative to Samuels: they concur with him that "naming Jung 'colonial' and 'racist' and leaving it there is simplistic," just as arguing Jung was "simply a man of his time" is inadequate. Samuels' 2018/2019 work functions as the paper's anchor citation for the legitimacy of the race critique of Jung, which the authors then build past rather than either dismissing or resting on.
