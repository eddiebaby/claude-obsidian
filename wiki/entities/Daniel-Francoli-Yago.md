# Daniel Françoli Yago

Jungian analyst; author of "The Hour that Never Comes and the Time that Remains" (*Journal of Analytical Psychology*, 2026), proposing [[The Hour That Never Comes]] and [[The Time That Remains]] as two archetypal experiences of psychic temporality, and [[Orphic Listening]] as the clinical stance appropriate to both. Argues linear/goal-directed cure logic is a category error for suffering structured by absence or by collapse, and grounds the essay in three worked clinical vignettes alongside Jung (*Memories, Dreams, Reflections*, *Psychological Types*, *Psychology and Alchemy*, *Red Book*) plus a wide contemporary and philosophical citation base (Benjamin, Bergson, Fisher, Han, Pelbart, Kalsched, Caruth, Rolnik, Martins, Dufourmantelle, Didi-Huberman).
