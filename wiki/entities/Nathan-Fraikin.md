# Nathan Fraikin

Researcher in post-Jungian studies and synchronicity; co-author (with [[Jingchao Zeng]]) of "[[zeng-fraikin2026-synchronicity-postjungian-astrology|Synchronicity in Post-Jungian Astrology: A Cosmological Quest]]" (JAP 2026), which reasserts the empirical, numinous threshold of Jung's [[Synchronicity|synchronicity]] against its cosmological over-extension in post-Jungian astrology, and proposes recovering it through the participatory, present-moment act of interpretation (Maggie Hyde's "Synchronicity II").
