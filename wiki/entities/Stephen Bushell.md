# Stephen Bushell

White British psychotherapist, based in Girona, Spain at time of writing (2023); raised in the southeast of England. Co-author with [[Divine Charura]] of "A duoethnographic exploration of colonialism in the cultural layer of the objective psyche" ([[JOAP-68-1-Charura-Bushell-Duoethnographic-Colonialism]], *Journal of Analytical Psychology* 68/1, 2023) — introducing the [[Colonizer Archetype]] and [[Ethnic Shadow]] concepts.

---

## Background (from the source paper)

- Maternal grandmother "Nan J," born and raised in London's East End, one of 13 children in working-class poverty; later expressed resentment toward 1960s South Asian immigration to Tower Hamlets — material Bushell uses in the paper's "grandmothers" comparison with Charura's great-grandmother Nana T.
- In autumn 2018, at over 50 years old, visited the International Museum of Slavery in Liverpool for the first time — a catalyzing encounter with "centuries of cultural avoidance" that he describes as bringing up ancestral shame and grief tied to British ships' role in the transatlantic slave trade (50% of all slave transportations; half from Liverpool).
- Invited Charura to facilitate a diversity training session for a London-based Jungian organization, the encounter that catalyzed their two-year duoethnographic dialogue (see [[Duoethnography]]).
- Voices explicit appreciation, in the paper, of Andrew Samuels' 2019 Open Letter on Jung's writings on "Africans," while insisting that self-critique ("if I point the finger at Jung, I must also point it at myself") is the necessary next step beyond naming Jung's failures.

---

## Role in the Source Paper

Bushell's account of the Liverpool museum visit and his reflections on inherited privilege/complicity ("in me was the slave trader, the plantation owner, the importer, and manufacturer") anchor the paper's treatment of the colonizer-side [[Ethnic Shadow]] and the claim that "the West faces total spiritual bankruptcy" without a reckoning with this material.
