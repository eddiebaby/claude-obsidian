# Richard Tarnas

American cultural historian and philosopher (b. 1950), author of *The Passion of the Western Mind* (1991) and *Cosmos and Psyche* (2006), the founding text of **archetypal astrology/cosmology**. In [[zeng-fraikin2026-synchronicity-postjungian-astrology|Zeng & Fraikin (2026)]] his synthesis of [[Synchronicity|synchronicity]] is judged faithful to Jung's *summary* but turned to a different purpose: synchronicity becomes a **metaphysical axiom** and formal-causal principle by which planetary archetypes order collective human history. The authors' critique is that this makes synchronistic events *predictable* across past and future — so "the abnormal becomes normal" and the rare, numinous, present-moment character Jung insisted on is lost. Tarnas notably omits the inconclusive result of Jung's astrological experiment. He explicitly positions himself as a continuator of von Franz's call to "work out" synchronicity.
