# Helge Michael Osterhold

Jungian-oriented clinician/researcher based in San Francisco, USA. Co-author (with [[Gisele Fernandes-Osterhold]]) of "Chasing the Numinous: Hungry Ghosts in the Shadow of the Psychedelic Renaissance," *Journal of Analytical Psychology* 68(4), 2023 — see [[osterhold2023-chasing-the-numinous]].

Navigation: [[depth-psychology]] | [[osterhold2023-chasing-the-numinous]]

---

## Contribution

Applies Jung's diagnosis of "modern man" and the Buddhist [[Hungry Ghost]] archetype to the 2020s psychedelic-assisted therapy movement, arguing that the collective fascination with psychedelic medicines is shadowed by a capitalist "gold rush" (commodification, patenting, cultural appropriation, ethical scandals) that reflects the same disconnection from psychic depth that produces the fascination in the first place. Names the intersecting drivers of this dynamic a **Western meta-cultural complex** — outward/empirical epistemology, heroic conviction, and deficiency-solution orientation. See [[Cultural Complex]] and [[Psychedelic Renaissance]].
