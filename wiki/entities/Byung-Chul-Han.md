# Byung-Chul Han

South Korean-German philosopher. *The Transparency Society* (2012): the "society of the absolute present," hyperexposure and continuous performance, no intervals and no backstage — "the difference between future and past disappears." Cited in [[yago2026-hour-that-never-comes-time-that-remains]] as the anxious-paralysis face of [[The Hour That Never Comes]] (time that loops rather than passes). *The Expulsion of the Other* (2016) names a further register for [[The Time That Remains]]: "hyperactive stagnation" — the narcissistic repetition of the same, continuously updated yet nothing truly happening. "The acceleration of time does not generate future, but repetitions."
