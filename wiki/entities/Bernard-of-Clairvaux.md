# Saint Bernard of Clairvaux (1090-1153 CE)

## Historical Overview

Cistercian abbot, mystic theologian, and political figure in medieval Europe. Born to a noble family near Dijon; his mother, Aleth, was of ancient noble lineage and famous for her piety.

Entered the Cistercian monastery at Clteaux (despite initial reluctance); later founded and became abbot of Clairvaux, where he established the abbey as a center of Christian mysticism and learning.

## Public Role

- Preached the Second Crusade at the Council of Troyes
- Participated in theological debates defending orthodoxy against heresy (Abelard, the Albigensians)
- Influenced church politics throughout Europe
- Wrote extensively on mystical theology and Marian devotion

Yet Bernard struggled with his worldly success. He felt pulled between contemplation (his inner calling) and action (his public obligations). This inner tension shapes the dream material.

## The Dream of Bernard's Mother (Aleth)

Before Bernard was conceived, his mother dreamed that she gave birth to a small white dog whose back was of a reddish color, and the dog barked very loudly.

The dream is **prophetic**, revealing the nature of the unborn child:
- **White dog**: The loyal servant of Christ (white = purity, divinity; dog = loyalty, servitude)
- **Reddish back**: The alchemical rubedo (redness) = relating to life, action, and the body
- **Loud barking**: His powerful voice and his role as a preacher/activist in the world

## Mother Complex in Bernard

Bernard's relationship to his mother and his own feminine side was **deeply complex**:
- His mother's piety and devotion to the church shaped him profoundly
- He identified with the feminine mystical tradition (devoted to the Virgin Mary, wrote hymns to Mary)
- Yet he was driven to action, to "bark" loudly in the world (crusades, theology, politics)
- This created an internal split: the contemplative monk vs. the activist abbot

## Von Franz's Analysis

The dream of the white and red dog reveals Bernard as **caught between two principles**:
1. **Feminine/Anima**: Contemplation, mystical devotion, inner life (the white dog = purity, the mother's legacy)
2. **Masculine/Active**: The call to action, barking loudly, engaging in worldly crusades (the red back = passion, action)

Bernard tried to live both, but this created psychological tension. He was a feeling-type, devoted to the emotional and mystical aspects of Christianity, yet thrown into the role of a warrior-theologian forced to "bark" at heretics and preach crusades.

## The Shadow of His Mission

The dream also contains a shadow: Bernard's role in the Albigensian Crusade (fighting the Cathar heretics) was brutal and fanatical. His "barking" at heresy had tragic consequences—many innocents died.

Von Franz notes this: **A man who cannot integrate his own inner conflict will project it onto the external enemy.** Bernard's inability to reconcile his contemplative nature with his activist role led him to project this inner warfare onto external enemies (heretics) whom he then destroyed.

## The Alchemical Color

Bernard's dog is white and red—the albedo and rubedo of alchemy:
- **Albedo (whitening)**: Illumination, clarification, detachment from the world
- **Rubedo (reddening)**: Return to life, relating, the Self in the world

The dream prophesied that Bernard would experience both: years of inner illumination in the monastery, followed by decades of engagement with the world. Yet these remained split in him rather than integrated.
