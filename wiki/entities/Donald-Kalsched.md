# Donald Kalsched

Jungian analyst and trauma theorist. *The Inner World of Trauma* (1996, cited in [[yago2026-hour-that-never-comes-time-that-remains]]) explores early trauma as a deep psychic rupture requiring dissociative defences "often animated by archetypal forces." Coins the **"personal spirit"**: the most sensitive and imaginative part of the child, which trauma fragments and then protects behind highly organized internal symbolic structures. These defences preserve psychic life but trap the subject in a **dissociated, frozen temporality** — the trauma remains "frozen" as image rather than metabolized as memory. Direct clinical ancestor of [[The Hour That Never Comes]]'s "trauma without an event."
