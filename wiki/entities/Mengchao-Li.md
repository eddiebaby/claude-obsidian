# Mengchao Li

## Overview

Chinese psychiatrist working in both **Jungian and Kleinian** registers, who applies a psychologized version of the **[[Eight Heart Methods of the I Ching]]** — the Yuan-dynasty interpretive system originated by the scholar Ba Bao and adapted by Heyong Shen ("the first Chinese Jungian analyst") — to transference and countertransference material in therapy.

## The Case

A client's maternal projection worked through the [[Eight Heart Methods of the I Ching|Eight Heart Methods]], reading **hexagram 2 (Kun, the Receptive/Earth) changing into hexagram 7 (Shih, the Army)** — the shift from a purely receptive maternal ground to organized, disciplined force. See [[Eight Heart Methods of the I Ching]] for the full six-stage method.

## Zeng's Critique

Li's application is creative and systematic, but blends **Kleinian** (projection, positions), **Jungian** (Great Mother, hero), and purely **Confucian** (moral-cultivation) elements from the Eight Heart Methods without marking the boundaries between them — a synthesis whose seams Zeng thinks should be made explicit.
