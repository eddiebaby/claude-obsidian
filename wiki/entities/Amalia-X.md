# Amalia X

"The best investigated case in the history of psychotherapy research" — a Freudian psychoanalysis of 500+ sessions, fully documented on video and transcribed in the Ulm Textbank archive, subject of numerous empirical dream studies including [[Christian-Roesler|Roesler's]] [[Structural Dream Analysis|Structural Dream Analysis]]. Female patient, 35 at treatment start, presenting with disordered self-esteem, recurring depression, and social/sexual withdrawal rooted in hirsutism (virile body-hair growth since puberty) and related defense mechanisms (compulsion neurosis, erythrophobia).

## Dream Series (95 dreams)

Analyzed against SDA's 6-pattern typology: **Pattern 2 (threatened)** dominates the first half of therapy; **Pattern 5 (social interaction)** dominates the second. Treatment produced significant, follow-up-stable improvement in psychosomatic symptoms, mood, self-esteem, and extraversion. One of the final dreams in the series is a clean **Pattern 6 (autonomy)** image: she walks — on her own hair, the exact material of her presenting symptom — into her analyst's office, says goodbye, and leaves on her own initiative. The dream series independently "empirically confirmed" the passive-to-active movement Roesler's model predicts, and its content "refute[s] the hypothesis of wish fulfilment" with "no evidence of censorship" — used as core supporting data against Freudian dream-censorship theory and for Jung's subjective-level, self-representation model.
