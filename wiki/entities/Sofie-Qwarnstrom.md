# Sofie Qwarnström

Author of "One-Sidedness and the Inferior Function in *Coriolanus* and *Timon of Athens*" (*Journal of Analytical Psychology*, 2026), reading the two Shakespeare tragedies as mirror-image case studies in Jungian [[One-Sidedness|one-sidedness]] — building an armillary-sphere model of the eight [[Psychological Types|psychological types]] and mapping the protagonists onto opposite poles of the same Introverted-Thinking/Extraverted-Feeling axis. Draws heavily on Marie-Louise von Franz's clinical lectures on the inferior function (the "eagle seizing a mouse" capture dynamic, the "mixtum compositum" integration state) and closes with [[Enantiodromia]] as the mechanism of both plays' catastrophic endings. References a further essay in press (Qwarnström, forthcoming) extending the same reading.
