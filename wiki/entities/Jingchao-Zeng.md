# Jingchao Zeng

Researcher on Jung, divination, synchronicity, and astrology, associated with the Synchronicity Research Group at the University of Essex (Colchester, UK). Two papers in the vault, from adjacent research territory:

- **2023 (solo):** "[[zeng2023-i-ching-jungian-application|The I Ching as a Potential Jungian Application: History and Practice]]" (JAP 68/5) — a historical and critical study of Jung's decades of practical [[I Ching Divination|I Ching]] use, organized into three dimensions (physical / psychological / psychical), plus an assessment of three contemporary Jungian clinicians ([[Jean-Kirsch]], [[Mengchao-Li]], [[Dennis-Merritt]]) and the cross-cultural conflations each imports.
- **2026 (with [[Nathan Fraikin]]):** "[[zeng-fraikin2026-synchronicity-postjungian-astrology|Synchronicity in Post-Jungian Astrology: A Cosmological Quest]]" (JAP) — argues Jung conceived [[Synchronicity|synchronicity]] as a cautious empirical claim about rare numinous coincidences, and that post-Jungian astrologers ([[Liz Greene]], [[Richard Tarnas]]) overstated it into cosmology by reviving the [[Qualitative Time|qualitative-time]] model Jung abandoned.

The through-line across both: how meaning gets read into acausal or symbolically-structured events (divination, synchronicity), and where Jungian practice conflates a tradition's native worldview with Jungian psychology. The 2023 paper is the earlier-career, single-authored work; the byline "Colchester, UK" matches the 2026 paper's Essex affiliation — same author.
