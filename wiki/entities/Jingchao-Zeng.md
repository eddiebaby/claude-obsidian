# Jingchao Zeng

Researcher on Jung, synchronicity, and astrology, associated with the Synchronicity Research Group at the University of Essex. Co-author (with [[Nathan Fraikin]]) of "[[zeng-fraikin2026-synchronicity-postjungian-astrology|Synchronicity in Post-Jungian Astrology: A Cosmological Quest]]" (JAP 2026), arguing that Jung conceived [[Synchronicity|synchronicity]] as a cautious empirical claim about rare numinous coincidences and that post-Jungian astrologers ([[Liz Greene]], [[Richard Tarnas]]) overstated it into cosmology by reviving the [[Qualitative Time|qualitative-time]] model Jung abandoned.
