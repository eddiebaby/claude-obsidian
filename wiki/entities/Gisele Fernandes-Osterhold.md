# Gisele Fernandes-Osterhold

Jungian-oriented clinician/researcher based in San Francisco, USA. Co-author (with [[Helge Michael Osterhold]]) of "Chasing the Numinous: Hungry Ghosts in the Shadow of the Psychedelic Renaissance," *Journal of Analytical Psychology* 68(4), 2023 — see [[osterhold2023-chasing-the-numinous]].

Navigation: [[depth-psychology]] | [[osterhold2023-chasing-the-numinous]]

---

## Contribution

Co-develops the paper's central argument: that the psychedelic renaissance's promise (a non-doctrinal, experiential channel to the numinous) is shadowed by a cultural-complex-driven gold rush, diagnosable through the Buddhist [[Hungry Ghost]] archetype — insatiable hunger produced by an inability to metabolize what is consumed. See [[Cultural Complex]] and [[Psychedelic Renaissance]].
