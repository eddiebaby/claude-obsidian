# Capital Fund Management

## Overview

Capital Fund Management (CFM) is a Paris-based systematic/quantitative hedge fund (23 Rue de l'Université, 75007 Paris), a hub of the "econophysics" research tradition applying statistical-physics methods to markets. Two of the four authors of [[kurth2026-trend-following-demise]] — Adam Rej and [[Jean-Philippe Bouchaud]] (chairman) — are CFM-affiliated, and the firm supplied the proprietary 5-minute intraday bar data underlying the paper's order-flow and price-impact analysis.

## Key Facts

- Authors affiliated with CFM in [[kurth2026-trend-following-demise]]: Adam Rej and [[Jean-Philippe Bouchaud]] (also Institut Louis Bachelier / Académie des Sciences).
- Supplied proprietary 5-minute bar data for 53 futures contracts (subset of the paper's 101-contract daily universe), restricted to contracts with coverage starting no later than 2004 to guarantee 5+ years of pre-2008-break data. Each bar reports total volume, buyer/seller-initiated volume, and end-of-interval resting volume at best bid/ask — the data underlying the order-book-imbalance and price-impact analysis in Sections 4.3 and 6.
- Institutional home of the econophysics research lineage behind the square-root price-impact law (Tóth et al. 2011) and the "Ponzi funds" self-fulfilling-loop framework (Van der Beck, Bouchaud, Villamaina 2024) — both directly load-bearing for this paper's mechanism.
- The paper's CTA industry proxy (Eq. 4) is cross-validated against independent third-party CTA participation-rate estimates (Quantica Capital 2022, Lorenzen et al./Morgan Stanley 2025), giving external validation that CFM's internal proxy construction is representative of the broader CTA industry, not just CFM's own book.
