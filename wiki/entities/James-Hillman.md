# James Hillman

## Overview

American post-Jungian psychologist and founder of **archetypal psychology**. In dream work he is the sharpest voice against reductive interpretation — insisting the analyst keep the image alive rather than translate it into a fixed meaning.

## Key Facts

- Signature caution: **"The moment you've defined the snake, interpreted it, you've lost the snake… The task of analysis is to keep the snake there."**
- Pushes [[Amplification]] toward "sticking with the image" — meaning is in dwelling with the image, not in decoding it to a concept.
- A central authority for the app's anti-symbol-dictionary stance: any meaning that closes the image is a loss.

## Connections

- Reinforces [[Amplification]] and the anti-dictionary principle in [[Dream-Analysis-and-Interpretation]].
- Post-Jungian extension of [[C.G. Jung]].
