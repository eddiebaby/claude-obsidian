# Dennis Merritt

## Overview

Jungian analyst who has researched the **[[I Ching Divination|I Ching]]** in analytic practice since around 1983, and who connects the Chinese holistic worldview to Jung's **[[Unus Mundus]]** and physicist David Bohm's implicate order.

## Two Clinical Formats

1. **Single three-hour consultation** — clarify the client's real question → yarrow-stalk casting treated as itself meditative and therapeutic → read the result across three English translations.
2. **Ongoing format for I-Ching-literate clients** — the client divines between sessions and brings the result back for discussion.

**Worked case:** a young man's marijuana use; a dream of a stillborn baby delivered by a stoned doctor; **hexagram 12 (Pi / Stagnation)** read as "getting high" as an escape from depressive feeling. The client quit smoking within four months.

## Zeng's Critique

Merritt claims the I Ching "originates from the archetypal depths of the human psyche and the psychoid dimensions of the Self" (2001, p. 55) — a psychologizing move Zeng finds philosophically questionable against Yew Wai Ko's *Jung on Synchronicity and Yijing* (2011), which argues the I Ching's native worldview "does not assume any transcendental realm of time" the way Merritt's framing implies. His *A Guide to the I Ching* is also flagged for over-translating "inferior people" into Western therapeutic vocabulary ("anxieties," "fears").
