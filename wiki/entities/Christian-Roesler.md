# Christian Roesler

## Overview

Contemporary Jungian analyst and clinical psychology researcher who developed **[[Structural Dream Analysis]]** (SDA), a narrative research method that puts Jungian dream theory on an empirical footing by analyzing the dream-ego's agency and its relationships to other dream figures.

## Key Facts

- Key papers: "Structural Dream Analysis: a narrative research method…," *International Journal of Dream Research* (2018), 11(1):21–29; "Jungian theory of dreaming and contemporary dream research…," *Journal of Analytical Psychology* (2020), 65(1):44–62.
- Identified **five dream-ego agency patterns**; showed a dreamer's series is dominated by one or two patterns tied to their psychological problems, and that patterns shift from passive → active over *successful* therapy.
- Tested the method on the Freudian specimen case **Amalia X** (93 dreams), "empirically confirming" the passive→active movement and finding data that "refute the hypothesis of wish fulfilment" with "no evidence of censorship."
- Argues contemporary evidence supports Jung's **continuity** view (per Domhoff 2017) but **qualifies strict compensation** — finds "no real evidence for a compensating activity," favoring the *completion* (holistic-picture) reading instead.
