# Edward F. Edinger

Edward Ferdinand Edinger (1922–1998). American Jungian analyst; one of the most prominent interpreters of Jung's late work in the English-speaking world. Based in Los Angeles.

---

## Biography

- Born 1922; died 1998
- One of the founders of the C.G. Jung Foundation of New York (with colleagues including Joseph Wheelwright)
- Practiced as a Jungian analyst in New York for many years, then relocated to Los Angeles
- Delivered influential lecture series at the C.G. Jung Institute of Los Angeles throughout the 1980s–90s
- His lectures on Jung's *Mysterium Coniunctionis* became *Mysterium Lectures* (1995); his lectures on *Aion* became *The Aion Lectures* (1996)

---

## Intellectual Profile

Edinger's central project was making Jung's most difficult late works accessible without sacrificing their psychological depth. He combined:

- **Clinical grounding**: many of his best interpretations arise from analytic practice; patients' dreams appear throughout his lectures as parallel instances of the material being discussed
- **Historical scholarship**: he fills the classical, medieval, and alchemical background that Jung alludes to but doesn't explicate
- **Psychological application**: he consistently asks "what does this mean for the analytic hour?" — not just what it means culturally

His own key contribution to Jungian vocabulary is the **Ego-Self Axis**: the conscious connecting link between the ego and the Self, which must become a live psychological reality for individuation to proceed. See [[Ego-Self-Axis]].

| Title | Year | Publisher | Subject |
|-------|------|-----------|---------|
| *Ego and Archetype* | 1972 | Putnam/Penguin | Ego-Self relationship; individuation; central theoretical work |
| *Anatomy of the Psyche: Alchemical Symbolism in Psychotherapy* | 1985 | Open Court | Alchemical operations as individuation stages |
| *The Bible and the Psyche* | 1986 | Inner City | OT and NT as individuation symbolism |
| *The Christian Archetype* | 1987 | Inner City | Christ-image as Self-symbol |
| *Transformation of the God-Image* | 1992 | Inner City | Jung's *Answer to Job* explicated |
| *Mysterium Lectures* | 1995 | Inner City | Lectures on Jung's *Mysterium Coniunctionis* |
| *The Aion Lectures* | 1996 | Inner City | Lectures on Jung's *Aion* (CW 9ii) |

*Ego and Archetype* is his most foundational work; it introduces the Ego-Self Axis diagram and the Psychic Life Cycle that appear throughout his later teaching.

---

## The Aion Lectures (1988–89)

Delivered at the C.G. Jung Institute of Los Angeles to an audience of analysts-in-training and interested lay people. Based on Jung's *Aion* (CW 9ii), which Edinger called "the foundation for a whole new department of human knowledge — archetypal psychohistory."

Key contribution: translating the dense historical survey of *Aion* into a developmental sequence visible both in individual lives and in collective history. The Christian aeon (2,000 years of Pisces) is the story of the Self being held in religious projection, and the present moment is the story of that projection withdrawing — requiring individuals to realize the Self psychologically rather than collectively. See [[edinger-aion-lectures]], [[Pisces-Aeon]].
