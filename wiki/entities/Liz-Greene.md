# Liz Greene

British Jungian analyst (b. 1946) and a founder of modern **psychological astrology**; author of *Saturn* (1976), *Relating* (1978), *The Astrology of Fate* (1984), and studies of Jung's own astrology and *Liber Novus*. In [[zeng-fraikin2026-synchronicity-postjungian-astrology|Zeng & Fraikin (2026)]] she is the primary case of a post-Jungian who adapted [[Synchronicity|synchronicity]] to astrology — affirming its *psychical* aspect (meaningful coincidence, *abaissement du niveau mental*) but sliding into the *physical/metaphysical* [[Qualitative Time|qualitative-time]] model Jung had abandoned, in which the birth moment's "magical timing" predetermines later events, buttressed by Hermeticism and Paracelsus ("as above, so below"). The authors read her as recovering, likely inadvertently, exactly the model Jung discarded after his failed astrological experiment.
