# Pisces Aeon

The Pisces Aeon is Jung's term for the approximately 2,000-year period of Western history coinciding with the precession of the spring equinox through the constellation Pisces (roughly 1 AD to 2000 AD). It is coextensive with the Christian era. Jung's *Aion* is largely a study of the Self as it expressed itself through the symbols and events of this aeon.

Source: [[edinger-aion-lectures]] (Lects. 2, 9–13); [[jung-cw9ii-aion]].

---

## The Astronomical Foundation

The precession of the equinoxes: the earth's axis wobbles slowly, so that the background constellation visible at the spring equinox shifts over millennia. One full circuit = 26,000 years (the Platonic year). One zodiacal sign = one Platonic month ≈ 2,000 years.

Timeline (approximate):
- 4000 BC: Eon of Taurus
- 2000 BC: Eon of Aries (the Bull and the Ram in Old Testament symbolism)
- 146 BC: Spring equinox crosses into Pisces (Star Alpha 113)
- 1 AD: Birth of Christ; fully in Pisces
- 1000 AD: Midpoint of the aeon; first fish ending, second beginning
- 1500 AD: Three-quarter point; major nodal events (Reformation, Renaissance)
- 2000 AD: Spring equinox leaving Pisces, entering Aquarius

Jung attaches synchronistic significance to this pattern — not mechanistic causation but meaningful coincidence between the astronomical configuration and the psychological/historical events it coincides with.

## The Two Fishes

The zodiacal sign Pisces consists of two fish pointing in opposite directions, joined by a cord. Jung reads these as symbols of the two phases of the Christian aeon:

- **First fish** (vertical, upward): Christ; the spiritual striving of the first millennium; "Gothic" upward movement; the ecclesiastical, otherworldly emphasis
- **Second fish** (horizontal, outward): the Antichrist; the empirical, worldly movement beginning ~1000 AD; outward conquest (crusades, exploration), then science, then secularism

The conjunction of Jupiter and Saturn in Pisces (7 BC) is considered to have signaled the birth of Christ. Jupiter = justice/the supreme being; Saturn = the evil planet. Their conjunction = extreme opposites meeting = the symbol of the two fishes, good and evil, life and death.

## Pattern of the Christian Aeon

Nodal points where major psychological events cluster (Figure 12):

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1 AD | Birth of Christ; beginning of the Pisces aeon |
| ~500 AD | St. Benedict (~480–547); founding of Western monasticism (529 AD); conjunction Jupiter/Saturn in Gemini |
| ~1000 AD | End of the Age of the First Fish; Joachim of Flora; flowering of heretical cults (Cathars, Waldensians, Beghards) |
| ~1500 AD | Age of Exploration; Renaissance; Reformation; emergence of science; Faust legend begins |
| ~2000 AD | End of the Age of the Second Fish; transition to Aquarius |

Edinger: "Our course of religious history... could have been predicted, both as regards time and content, from the precession of the equinoxes through Pisces." The pattern is not a mechanical prediction but an archetypal blueprint laid down by the initial constellation of the aeon.

## Christ and Antichrist as the Pair of Fishes

The two-fish structure maps onto the unresolved problem of evil in Christian symbolism:

**Christ** (first fish): the light aspect of the Self; *privatio boni* (evil as mere absence of good); the purely positive God-image. 2,000 years of striving toward the heights.

**Antichrist** (second fish): the dark aspect; what was excluded from the Christian God-image; the shadow of the collective. Emerges at ~1000 AD not as a figure but as the collective psyche's dark side demanding recognition.

Psychologically: the Christian God-image (Self-projection) was incomplete because it excluded evil. The second fish is the return of the excluded shadow at the collective level. The resolution requires a *coniunctio oppositorum* — a union of Christ and Antichrist symbolically — which neither mainstream Christianity nor secular modernity has achieved. Alchemy tried to accomplish this symbolically with the *lapis* (philosopher's stone), which explicitly united opposites. See [[Self (archetype)]].

## Joachim of Flora and the Three Ages

Joachim of Flora (~1132–1202): a forerunner of Jung and of psychological thinking. He saw the Trinity not as a static entity but as a *historical sequence*:

- **Age of the Father** (OT period): law and fear; pristine oneness with Nature; pre-critical
- **Age of the Son** (NT through ~1000 AD): grace and faith; conflict of opposites; longing for the Father's world
- **Age of the Holy Ghost** (begun ~1000 AD): love and spirit; *ecclesia spiritualis* (each individual has a unique relation to the Holy Ghost)

Jung: the Age of the Holy Ghost is the age of individuation — the recognition of the unconscious, the subordination of exclusive independence to a supraordinate totality. Joachim called it *ecclesia spiritualis*; Jung called it individuation.

The heretical sects (Cathars, Waldensians, Beghards, Brethren of the Free Spirit) at 1000 AD were the premature, inflated eruption of the Holy Ghost energy — they believed themselves to be God by nature, immutable in the new rock, needing no external God. "A religious inflation instead of the rationalistic and political psychosis that is the affliction of our day."

## Monasticism as Collective Psychic Transformation

St. Benedict (~480–547) at the 500 AD nodal point: the founding of Western monasticism. Edinger: "The monastic phenomenon performed a profound service in the transformation of the collective psyche through the centuries." The three vows — poverty, chastity, obedience — are radical frustrations of greed, lust, and the desire for power. Monasticism was a collective operation for taming the instinctual psyche and subjecting it to a spiritual counterpole. The conjunction of Jupiter and Saturn in Gemini (531 AD) synchronistically presaged it.

## The ~1500 Nodal Point

The Reformation (~1500 AD): "An enantiodromic process set in which, in contrast to the Gothic striving *upwards* to the heights, could be described as a horizontal movement *outwards*." The Age of Exploration, the conquest of Nature, science, and the Renaissance all manifest as the second fish coming fully into its own.

Nostradamus (1503–1566) — born exactly at this nodal point. His prophetic writings were gripped by an activated unconscious; he was convinced his prophecies were divinely inspired. Jung devotes a chapter of *Aion* to him because he stood at the crossing point where the anti-Christian trend became visible, and his prophecies were attempts to give voice to what the psyche was detecting.

The French Revolution (~1789): the **enthronement of the Goddess Reason** in Notre Dame (previously a cathedral to the Mother of God). Jung: this was a "symbolic gesture of great significance for the Western world — rather like the hewing down of Wotan's oak by Christian missionaries." It was an anticipation of the anti-Christian trend. Psychologically: when the spiritual value previously carried by Christianity was so severely deprecated, the East (Vedanta, the Upanishads) arrived as compensation — brought back by Anquetil du Perron in 1801.

## Planetary Conjunctions and the Origins of Religions

Jung argues that the major Western religions originated from specific planetary conjunctions — the astrological meeting of two planets, which regularly evokes collective projections and marks major psychological events. The underlying principle: wherever opposites come together (*coniunctio*), something new is generated.

| Religion | Conjunction | Psychological Character |
|----------|-------------|------------------------|
| Judaism | Jupiter + Saturn | Extreme opposites: justice (supreme being) + evil (malefic planet). Both sides held in tension; the Law manages irresolvable opposition. Contains the greatest possible oppositeness. |
| Islam | Jupiter + Venus | Venus = eros, desire, relationship. Islam's derivation from eros explains the crescent moon on Islamic flags. "Arabic Islam is an eros religion." The greatest eros content of the three. |
| Christianity | Jupiter + Mercury | Mercury = spirit, language, intellect. Christianity emphasizes spirituality at the maximum; has the greatest denial of the body of any major religion. |
| Antichrist | Jupiter + Moon | Moon = the most ambiguous, dubious, mutable entity. The Antichrist will incorporate all that is most ambiguous about lunar symbolism. Not simply evil — irreducibly ambiguous. |

(Jung, par. 130; Edinger, pp. 68-69)

The conjunction of Jupiter and Saturn in Pisces in seven BC signaled the birth of Christ. Jupiter = justice; Saturn = the evil planet. Their meeting = extreme opposites coming together = the symbolic image of the two fishes (good and evil, life and death) laid down at the beginning of the aeon that would spend 2,000 years playing out their opposition.

A medieval tradition extends this: "The religion of the Jews originated in a conjunction of Jupiter with Saturn, Islam in [Jupiter with Venus], Christianity in [Jupiter with Mercury], and the Antichrist in [Jupiter with the moon]." (Jung, par. 130)

Psychologically, the compensation mechanism operates at the level of civilizations: when Christianity's body-denying, spirituality-maximizing conjunction with Mercury had run its course and the spiritual value it carried was being depreciated (French Revolution, Enlightenment), the East (Vedanta, Upanishads) arrived as compensation — brought back from India by Anquetil du Perron at exactly the right historical moment. "No psychic value can disappear without being replaced by another of equivalent intensity." (Jung)

## The Transition to Aquarius

We are at the end of the Pisces aeon, entering Aquarius. Fish live *within* water, contained by it; Aquarius *carries* water — a totally different relation to the psyche. Fish = unconscious identification with the collective container; water carrier = conscious, individual relation to the unconscious.

### The Three Meanings of "Aquarius"

The word carries three distinct images, each pointing to a different aspect of the coming aeon (Edinger, p. 192):

1. **Water man** — a human figure, not a fish or animal. The aeon of Aquarius will be of human nature, not less than human. Where the Pisces aeon was about being immersed in a transpersonal medium (religion, the collective), Aquarius is about remaining human while in relation to that medium.

2. **Water carrier** — carrying water rather than being immersed in it. The individual becomes a conscious carrier and dispenser of the psyche, rather than a fish swimming in the psychic fish-pond of the church or collective. "Instead of being a fish contained in a psychic fish pond, the individual becomes a conscious carrier and dispenser of the psyche." (Edinger, p. 193)

3. **Water pourer / vessel** — the image of a vessel, the alchemical *vas*. Connection to the [[Vessel-Symbol]]: the individual psyche itself becomes the vessel that contains, rather than needing an external container (institution, relationship, group).

### Jung's Personal Encounter with the Aion Symbol

The frontispiece of *Aion* is a picture of the Mithraic god Aion: a human figure, winged, with a lion's head, encircled by a great serpent. This image appeared directly in Jung's own unconscious experience — not in *Memories, Dreams, Reflections* but in the 1925 seminar account (*Analytical Psychology*, Notes of the Seminar Given in 1925, p. 96):

Jung descended to the unconscious and met Salome and Elijah; there was also a serpent with them. Then:

> "A most disagreeable thing happened. Salome became very interested in me, and she assumed that I could cure her blindness. She began to worship me. I said, 'Why do you worship me?' She replied, 'You are Christ.' In spite of my objections she maintained this. I said, 'This is madness,' and became filled with skeptical resistance. Then I saw the snake approach me. She came close and began to encircle me and press me in her coils. The coils reached up to my heart. I realized as I straggled that I had assumed the attitude of the Crucifixion. In the agony and the straggle, I sweated so profusely that the water flowed down on all sides of me. Then Salome rose, and she could see. While the snake was pressing me, I felt that my face had taken on the face of an animal of prey, a lion or a tiger."

This parallels the Aion image exactly: the lion-face, the serpent encircling, the figure of a man. **Jung IS the Mithraic Aion in this vision.** Edinger's interpretation: Jung is the first representative of the new aeon. Just as Christ was the first to enter the aeon of Pisces, Jung was first to inaugurate the aeon of Aquarius. "Jung could not have perceived and summarized the content of the aeon of Pisces unless he was already outside it. You cannot see something in its totality, objectively, until you are already out of it."

### Christ Foreshadowing Aquarius

In both Mark (14:13) and Luke (22:10), Christ directs two disciples: "Go into the city and you will meet a man carrying a pitcher of water. Follow him." This man leads them to the house where the Last Supper will be held. Edinger: "This is an image of the aeon to come 2,000 years later, visible even at the opening of the aeon of Pisces."

Christ himself was at times a water dispenser — to the Samaritan woman (John 4:10), and the stream of living water flowing from his side when pierced at the Crucifixion (John 19:34). But the water he dispensed **generated fishes**, not water carriers — it generated the church, the fish pond in which the faithful fish could swim. The church became the water carrier; the individual became the fish within it.

**"Jung discovered water."** Edinger's pithy formulation: we know who did *not* discover the fish — it was not Aquarius. The person who discovered water was Aquarius. Jung discovered the water of the unconscious as a *conscious* reality, not just swam in it.

### The Continuing Incarnation

The aeon of Aquarius will generate individual water carriers. The numinous reality of the psyche will no longer be carried by religious communities (the church, synagogue, mosque) but by **conscious individuals**. Edinger (p. 193):

> "The numinous reality of the psyche will no longer be carried by religious communities — the church, the synagogue or the mosque — but it will be carried by conscious individuals. This is the idea Jung puts forward in his notion of a continuing incarnation, the idea that individuals are to become incarnating vessels of the Holy Spirit on an ongoing basis. He developed this idea more fully in the next work he wrote, *Answer to Job*."

The Aquarian individual is not self-sufficient. They are a carrier, not a source. The water comes from somewhere; the vessel holds and dispenses it. This is the individuation task in Aquarian form: not to *be* the psychic reality (inflation) nor to swim unconsciously in it (identification) but to carry it consciously.

> [!key-insight] This is the context for all depth psychology
> The entire project of Jungian analysis — ego development, shadow integration, anima/animus work, Self encounter — is not a private therapeutic procedure. It is the individual's participation in the larger cultural task of the aeon transition: realizing psychologically what was previously held collectively in religious projection. The destruction of the God-image is not a disaster to be reversed; it is the precondition for finding the Self as a direct psychological reality. Each person who does this work becomes a water carrier of the new aeon.
