# Fourfold Quaternio

Jung's most elaborated structural image of the Self, developed across *Aion* Chs 13-14 (paras 338-401). Four quaternios stacked vertically, connected by shared terms, that together constitute a complete psychological map of the levels at which the Self can manifest. They finally close into a circle (because the highest and lowest terms coincide) and abstract into the **Formula of the Self** (Fig. 28).

Primary source: [[edinger-aion-lectures]] (Lectures 22-24, paras 338-401, directly read). Primary text: [[jung-cw9ii-aion]] Chs 13-14.

---

## The Four Quaternios

Each quaternio has a unity at top, four differentiated terms in the middle, and a unity at the bottom. "At each level an original unity divides into four parts, each expressive of an aspect of the original totality. This allows the different qualities to become more conscious, but since once separated they are in conflict with each other, they must be again brought into unity at the bottom of the figure." (Edinger, Fig. 21 caption)

The four share a chain of terms: **Anthropos → Man → Serpent → Lapis → Rotundum**. Jung then curves this chain into a circle by putting the tail (Rotundum) into the mouth of the first figure (Anthropos). Beginning and end coincide: the cosmic and the mineral are the same totality.

---

### 1. Anthropos Quaternio (Upper Moses Quaternio)

**Spiritual / Pneumatic realm**

| Top | Four terms | Bottom |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| ANTHROPOS (Higher Adam) | Higher Jethro, Positive Miriam, Wise Zipporah, Higher Moses | MAN (Lower Adam, ordinary man) |

The four figures of the Moses family: Moses kills the slave driver and flees to Midian; marries Zipporah daughter of Jethro; fourth figure is Miriam, Moses' sister-turned-prophetess. Each figure has a higher (spiritual) and lower (carnal) aspect.

**Imagery of this realm**: celestial, light, cosmic. Henry Vaughn's "I saw Eternity the other night / Like a great Ring of pure and endless light." Dante's rose at the close of the *Divine Comedy*. The sublimatio process is at home here.

---

### 2. Shadow Quaternio (Lower Moses Quaternio)

**Animal / Chthonic realm**

| Top | Four terms | Bottom |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| MAN (Lower Adam, the ego) | Lower Jethro, Negative Miriam, Ethiopian Woman (Zipporah in negative form), Moses as Carnal Man | SERPENT (Instinct) |

"The lower Adam represents the ego, which occupies a mid position between the spiritual quaternity and the shadow and instinct level." (Fig. 22 caption) The ego is literally in the middle of this structure — above it is the spiritual quaternio, below it the animal.

**Imagery of this realm**: dubious or ambiguous human beings, warm-blooded and cold-blooded animals, chthonic figures. Classical example: the four sons of Horus who were divine guardians of the deceased — one with a jackal head, one falcon, one dog, one human. Ezekiel's four living creatures (lion, ox, eagle, man) are structurally identical. The four Evangelists (Matthew=man, Mark=lion, Luke=ox, John=eagle) continue this symbolism in Christianity.

**Chirographum** (handwriting): the negative version of this quaternio appears as the archetypal "handwriting" impressed on the soul during its descent through the planetary spheres (from Colossians 2:10-15 and Gnostic sources). Each planetary archon bestows an attribute: Saturn = reason (logistikon), Jupiter = power to act (praktikon), Mars = bold spirit (thymikon), Sun = sense-perception and imagination (aisthetikon, phantastikon), Venus = passion (epithymetikon), Mercury = speech (hermeneutikon), Moon = bodily formation (phytikon). Psychologically: our ancestral and archetypal background that has imprinted us with its patterns — what Basileides called the "excrescent soul" (*prosphues phuche*), the soul's appendages accumulated on its way into incarnation.

Belshazzar's feast: the handwriting on the wall = destiny announced from the depths of the unconscious. Modern parallel Edinger cited: a man discovers writing on the wall of his clothes closet, growing daily, increasingly ominous — his wife going mad was writing there.

---

### 3. Paradise Quaternio (Four Rivers of Eden)

**Vegetable / Plant realm**

| Top | Four terms | Bottom |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| SERPENT (Instinct) | Pison (eye/vision), Gihon (ear/hearing), Tigris (smell/nose), Euphrates (mouth/nourishment) | LAPIS (Matter) |

From the Naassene Gnostic interpretation: the Garden of Eden has a brain at its center ("Edem is the brain, tightly fastened in encircling robes, as if in heaven"), from which four rivers flow. Psychologically: sensory consciousness as four outflowings from a central unconscious source. The serpent divides into the four rivers at the instinctual level; they reunite below as the lapis.

**Imagery**: trees, gardens, plant and vegetable symbolism. Jung's Liverpool dream (the magnolia tree on an island illuminated in perpetual sunshine). Gardens under the sea, green plants growing out of spheres, gardens with central trees.

The serpent = the tree's voice (para 372) — "It is the tree's voice, which persuades Eve." The serpent is the personified tree-numen, the voice of instinct embedded in the vegetable world.

---

### 4. Lapis Quaternio

**Mineral / Inorganic realm**

| Top | Four terms | Bottom |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| LAPIS (Matter) | Air, Water, Fire, Earth | ROTUNDUM (Chaos, the original inchoate state) |

"The snake symbol brings us to the images of Paradise, tree, and earth. This amounts to an evolutionary regression from the animal kingdom back to plants and inorganic nature, epitomized in alchemy by the secret of matter, the *lapis*." (para 374)

The *lapis* here is NOT the finished alchemical opus but the **prima materia** — elemental, stony stuff, "a bit of the original chaos." The *separatio* process splits this prima materia into the four elements; they then reunite in the **rotundum** below. "In the chaos the elements are not united. They represent an original state of conflict and mutual repulsion." (para 375)

The sequence in this quaternio describes a twofold process:
1. **Circle → Square → Circle** (psychological notation): original unity (circle) → separatio into four elements (square, the four corners) → coniunctio back into rotundum (circle again). This is simultaneously the alchemical process map and the psychological development map.

Zosimos calls the *rotundum* "the omega element... which probably signifies the head." The rotundum = the head = the self-containing vessel = the beginning.

**Imagery**: stones, jewels, crystals, inorganic structures. The heavenly Jerusalem (Rev. 21:10-19): walls of jasper, gold like glass, twelve gates, foundations of precious stones — a fourfold structure composed of inorganic stuff.

## The Circle: Beginning = End

Jung curves the chain (Anthropos → Man → Serpent → Lapis → Rotundum) into a circle by identifying Rotundum with Anthropos. The lowest (original chaos, the Rotundum) and the highest (the cosmic man, Anthropos) are the same. This is the *circulatio* — the Ouroboros at the structural level.

Figure 26 (Fourfold Quaternio) shows this: the four quaternios connected vertically, then the diagram on the left showing the circle in which Rotundum/Anthropos = the same point at top, Man and Lapis at the sides, Serpent at the bottom.

## The Four Realms Summarized

| Quaternio | Realm | Typical imagery | Triad within |
|-----------|-------|----------------|--------------|
| Anthropos | Spiritual / Celestial | Light, cosmos, Eternity, divine figures | Father-Son-Spirit developmental sequence |
| Shadow | Animal / Chthonic | Animals, dubious humans, instinctual figures | Nigredo phases of the shadow work |
| Paradise | Vegetable / Plant | Trees, rivers, gardens, sense organs | The four senses as fourfold differentiation |
| Lapis | Mineral / Inorganic | Stones, crystals, cities, elements | Circle-square-circle of separatio/coniunctio |

"Each of these realms has a triad built into its structure; not only is it a quaternity, but it also contains a triadic or developmental process. So we have a quaternio representing the static aspect of that realm, and a triad representing the developmental process whereby that realm unfolds." (Edinger, p. 178)

## Historical Correspondence in the Christian Aeon

Jung maps the four quaternios onto the four 500-year periods of the Pisces Aeon (paras 403-407):

| Period | Quaternio | Historical character |
|--------|-----------|---------------------|
| 1–500 AD | Anthropos | Pneumatic/spiritual; the Church is otherworldly; martyrs, Neoplatonism |
| 500–1000 AD | Shadow | The Church becomes worldly/carnal; some Popes exemplify the descent |
| 1000–1500 AD | Paradise | Alchemy emerges; vegetative/plant symbolism; nature begins to be explored |
| 1500–2000 AD | Lapis | Scientific materialism; deification of matter; age of inorganic analysis |

"Jung's idea is that the whole aeon, so far as the collective psyche is concerned, has been a *circulatio* process through those four quaternities." (Edinger, p. 187)

This connects directly to [[Pisces-Aeon]]: the nodal points at 500, 1000, 1500 AD correspond exactly to the transitions between the four quaternios.

## Ezekiel's Vision

The most differentiated expression of the God-image in the entire Old Testament; "central to all of Western civilization" (Edinger). Ezekiel's vision (Ezek. 1:4-28):

Four living creatures, each with four faces (man in front, ox on right, lion on left, eagle at back). Four wings. Wheels with eyes. Firmament of crystal. Sapphire throne. The figure of a man above the throne, surrounded by fire and the likeness of a rainbow.

Structurally: the four creatures are a quaternio, and each has a wheel beside it (a subordinate quaternio) — this is the Fourfold Quaternio structure at the level of divine imagery. "The formula has achieved a higher level of differentiation than that achieved by the vision" because in the vision the wheels are all the same; in the abstract formula the letters are different.

The same symbolism: four sons of Horus (jackal/Imsety, falcon/Horus son, dog/Duamutef, human/Hapy) → four Evangelists (Matthew=man, Mark=lion, Luke=ox, John=eagle) → Christian mandalas.

## The Formula of the Self (Fig. 28)

Abstracted from the four joined quaternios (para 410). An abstract fourfold quaternio where each of the four corners (A, B, C, D) differentiates into four sub-terms (a1, a2, a3; b1, b2, b3; etc.) with shared edges.

- A = Fire; B = Air; C = Water; D = Earth
- A differentiates into: fiery fire, airy fire, watery fire, earthy fire
- B differentiates into: fiery air, airy air, watery air, earthy air
- And so on for C and D

The same formula works with the four psychological functions: if A = intuition, B = thinking, C = feeling, D = sensation — each undergoes fourfold differentiation in the course of the *circulatio* of individuation.

> "When psychic contents are split up into four aspects, it means that they have been subjected to discrimination by the four orienting functions of consciousness." (para 410)

The formula is a circular process — A through D and then back again, continually. It is both static (a map) and dynamic (a process).

## The Space-Time Quaternio

Para 397-398. Jung identifies the quaternary structure with Kant's a priori forms of perception:

"The space-time quaternio is the archetypal *sine qua non* for any apprehension of the physical world — indeed, the very possibility of apprehending it. It is the organizing schema par excellence among the psychic quaternities." (para 398)

Kant discovered that the mind imposes its own spatial and temporal structure on raw sensory data. Jung's implication: the quaternary structure of space (three dimensions + time) is the Self's organizing schema projected onto the physical world. Our ability to perceive the world as ordered is an expression of the Self's quaternary nature. "Perception of the psyche and perception of the physical world overlap. Each is perceived through the organ of the Self."

Near-death experience of philosopher A.J. Ayer (reported in the *Los Angeles Times*, October 1988): a rigid atheist, Ayer encountered during cardiac arrest "a red light, exceedingly bright and painful," ministered by two creatures responsible for space — which was "awry." He tried to signal the ministers of time (his watch) to fix space. Edinger's gloss: Ayer encountered the missing fourth element — time — in the space-time quaternio.

## Clementine Creation Image (Fig. 27)

Unitary God → two sons: Good Son (pneuma/spirit) and Bad Son (matter). Matter further divides into four elements, from which the Devil emerges. Other opposites also emanate: heaven/earth, day/night, male/female, light/fire, sun/moon, life/death, Adam/Eve.

This is psychologically important because it is an *early Christian* image that already contained the opposites within the God-image — it did not split them off. It foreshadowed Jung's conception of the Self as the reconciliation of opposites, rather than the privatio boni splitting that mainstream Christianity imposed. Parallel to the Kabbalistic Sefirotic Tree.

## Parallel Systems: 16-Term Diagram and Kircher's Quaternary

Jung offers two further parallels to confirm the psyche's tendency to generate fourfold structures (from *Psychology and Alchemy*, CW 12, pars. 366ff., discussed in Edinger's Lecture 25):

### The 16-Term Diagram (Figure 29)

A 4×4 table from an alchemical text. Four rows × four columns = 16 terms. The fourth term in each sequence is "the mysterious one, the ambiguous one, just like the fourth term of a sequence of four. It is the mystery, the goal of all the previous ones. It is the arcanum."

| 4 Levels of Alchemical Work | Elements | Concepts | Psychological Aspects |
|----------------------------|----------|----------|-----------------------|
| Work on natural things | Water | Ideas about composites/natural things | Sense perception |
| Work to discriminate parts of natural things | Earth | Ideas about discriminated things | Intellectual discrimination |
| Work to discriminate the soul from nature | Air | Concepts of "simple things" (Platonic ideas) | Reason |
| Work by the intellect on nature | Fire | Concepts about simplest things; ether, the quintessence | **Arcanum** |

The arcanum = the sixteenth = the unnameable mystery = the Self. The same logic as the quaternity: three terms are comprehensible; the fourth is paradoxical, the one that can't be named but draws everything toward it.

### Kircher's Quaternary System

Athanasius Kircher's numerical parallel: four levels associated with 1, 10, 100, 1000.
- **1** = God, root of all things
- **10** = the duality, the spiritual world
- **100** = the realm of soul and intelligence  
- **1000** = the realm of body and all concrete things

This is the same descent: from pure unity (1) through spiritual world (10) through soul (100) to matter (1000). The same pattern as Anthropos → Man → Serpent → Lapis → Rotundum.

Edinger's summary: "Here again one sees the same kind of sequence from above downward, which Jung applied to the fourfold quaternio. Jung is trying to demonstrate, through these parallels, that the psyche has a tendency to generate fourfold quaternios."

## Psychological Significance

The Fourfold Quaternio is Jung's most developed answer to the question: what is the structure of totality? Not a circle (too simple), not a trinity (incomplete — excludes the chthonic/evil), not a static entity, but a **fourfold structure with developmental process at each level, closing into a circle of descent and ascent**.

For Scott's individuation: the quaternio answers "what is the Self?" in the most differentiated way possible. The ego (Lower Adam) sits mid-quaternio, between the spiritual above and the instinctual below. Individuation = consciously occupying that mid-position rather than inflating upward into spiritual identification or regressing downward into instinctual compulsion. The *circulatio* — going through all four levels — is what the process requires.

> [!key-insight] Where you sit in the quaternio
> The ordinary ego = the Lower Adam = Man. It can look upward to the Anthropos (the higher self, the spiritual) or downward to the Serpent (instinct, shadow, the excrescent soul). The individuation task is to develop the capacity to look both ways consciously, without inflating into either direction. The analytical work is a *circulatio*: through all four realms, not just the one the ego finds most comfortable.
