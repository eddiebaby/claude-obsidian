# Humidum Radicale

**Humidum radicale** ("radical moisture" or "root-moisture") is the alchemical doctrine of an original, primordial moisture from which all life derives and which sustains it through the natural lifespan. It is one of the primary names for [[Mercurius]] in his water aspect, and stands behind the broader concept of [[Aqua-Permanens]].

Primary CW 13 source: [[jung-cw13-alchemical-studies]] Essay IV §2, par. 255.

---

## The Doctrine

The doctrine of *humidum radicale* has roots in Aristotelian biology (the *rhizoma hygron* or radical moisture underlying vital heat) and was central to medieval medical theory as the *humiditas radicalis* — the innate moisture with which the organism is born and which gradually dries up through life's heat, causing aging and eventually death.

In alchemy, the concept was elevated into a metaphysical principle. Par. 255 gives its alchemical names:
- *Humiditas radicalis* — the radical moisture
- *Incombustibilis et unctuosa* — incombustible and unctuous (oily, self-sustaining)
- *Aqua vitae* — water of life
- *Aqua alba* — white water (purified)
- *Aqua sicca* — dry water (paradoxical: moisture that is itself dried)
- *Aqua septies distillata* — seven-times-distilled water (purified to the point of spiritualization)
- *Aqua aurea* — golden water (the highest tincture)

All of these name the same primordial substance: the original, imperishable moisture at the root of all living things.

---

## As Mercurius

The *humidum radicale* is identified with Mercurius as his water aspect. Par. 255: "Mercurius arises from the moisture like a vapour — indicating his spiritual nature even in his water aspect." The vapor rising from moisture is the familiar alchemical image of spirit emerging from matter: the water is not simply water but the *spiritus* becoming visible.

The *aqua manus non madefaciens* ("the water that does not make the hands wet," par. 255) is the paradoxical alchemical formulation: the radical moisture is a spiritual water, not a physical one. It wets nothing external yet sustains all life internally.

---

## Connection to the *Aqua Permanens*

The *humidum radicale* and the *aqua permanens* (permanent, divine water) are closely related — both designate the primordial water that is the vehicle of Mercurius's transformative power. The difference is one of emphasis:

- *Aqua permanens*: emphasizes eternity and divinity — the water that does not change, does not evaporate, persists through all transformations
- *Humidum radicale*: emphasizes the biological-cosmological root — the moisture that underlies organic life from its very origin

In practice the alchemists used them interchangeably. Both name the hidden, sustaining, life-giving principle within matter that the *opus* seeks to liberate and concentrate.

---

## Psychological Reading

The *humidum radicale* as a psychological symbol names the primordial vitality of the unconscious — the original life-force that predates consciousness and sustains it. It is what remains when everything else is stripped away: the "moisture" at the root of the tree of life, the [[Philosophical-Tree]]'s underground source.

The doctrine's medical dimension (the radical moisture drying up with age → death) maps onto the psychological concern with psychic vitality: when a person loses contact with the *humidum radicale* — the instinctual, root-level life-energy of the unconscious — there is a drying-out of the personality, a loss of imaginative capacity, a premature aging of the soul. The alchemical opus is, among other things, the attempt to replenish the radical moisture by re-establishing contact with its source.
