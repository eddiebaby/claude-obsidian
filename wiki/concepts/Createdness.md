# Createdness

Jung's psychological reading of the Ignatian "Principle and Foundation" (*Spiritual Exercises* §23): *El hombre es criado* / **Latin: *Creatus est homo*** — "the human person is created." [[Eckhard Frick|Frick]] ([[frick2025-spiritual-path-transformation|2025]]) makes it the paper's culminating concept: we are **anticipated by the unconscious**, created rather than self-originating, and this acceptance is the humility that creativity and transformation require.

## Jung's Interpretation

From the 1939–40 Exercises seminar (lecture 8): "if we understand 'Creatus est homo' correctly, then we tell ourselves that we are something that has come about, a product, that we are anticipated. We were there and did not know it. It was 'known,' only we do not know who knew it." The alternative — assuming "everything we are comes from our own consciousness" — is "a diabolical hubris" that makes us think "we are the gods of this world" ("Where there's a will there's a way"), which multiplies into a war of many self-appointed gods.

## Creativity, Not Will

The crux (Zarathustra Seminar, 1934–35): "You don't create, you are created; in creation you are created. Something makes you do it… Try to stop creation and see what happens." And: "Only people with an inflation can assume that they create." Creativity is **not the fruit of conscious will** but the acceptance of the uncontrollable unconscious. Without obeyance and humility, "creativity will be destroyed by inflation" ([[Inflation-Jungian]]). The one who splits off and thinks "I am the fellow" who creates has already inflated.

## The Same Move as Faith in O

Frick's explicit link: "This is Jung's way of expressing Bion's [[Faith in O|faith in O]]." Createdness is the receptive pole of transformation — ceding the ego's claim to originate, accepting the priority of O / the Self / the unconscious. It is also the disposition behind [[Spiritus contra spiritum]] (the recovering alcoholic's "power greater than ourselves") and the antidote the [[Discernment of Spirits|discernment]] framework prescribes against spiritual inflation.

## Connections

- [[Faith in O]] — the same acceptance of the unknowable's priority, in Bion's terms.
- [[Inflation-Jungian]] — the failure mode createdness guards against: the ego claiming to be its own creative source.
- [[Discernment of Spirits]] — createdness as the humble disposition that makes discernment possible.
- [[Active Imagination]] — "the art of letting things happen, action through non-action" (Jung 1957) is createdness as method.
