# Fourfold Brain

## Definition

[[Ole Vedfelt|Vedfelt's]] didactic model of the dreaming brain: a schematic that marks four functional zones — color-coded in his figures — that interact while we dream. Not a claim about clean anatomical boundaries (he stresses the real networks overlap and hide each other) but a working map for locating where each of the [[Ten Core Qualities of Dreams|core qualities]] "happens." In dreaming, outer sensing and executive motor control are almost fully uncoupled, leaving a **surplus of activity** for these inner systems.

## The Four Zones

| Zone | Substrate | Function in dreams |
|---|---|---|
| **Instinctual brain** (brown / "reptilian") | Brainstem; periaqueductal grey (PAG); reticular activation system | Basic instinctual action plans (exploration, feeding, aggression, sexuality); PAG's "background emotions" (calm/tense, elated/depressed); the **seeking system** originates here |
| **Emotional brain** (red / "old mammalian") | Limbic system — amygdala, hypothalamus, pituitary | Behavioural/psychological resolution of emotion; amygdala **fear system** (fast route, aids survival); emotion has far more power *toward* cognition than the reverse |
| **Imaginative brain** (blue) | Paralimbic + midline frontal — mPFC, vmPFC, insula, anterior cingulate; occipito-temporo-parietal junction | Creativity, higher feeling, imagination; symbol and metaphor; self-awareness and "theory of mind"; the **vmPFC is absolutely necessary for dreaming** — damage abolishes it |
| **Cognitive brain** (purple) | Dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (DLPFC) | Higher reasoning, logic, abstract thought — relatively *deactivated* in dreaming |

## The Load-Bearing Claim

The **imaginative brain sits at the center**, not the cognitive brain. Solms's finding that vmPFC damage abolishes dreaming (while brainstem-only mechanisms cannot generate dreams) is Vedfelt's neuro-anchor for treating dreams as products of a *meaning-making, feeling, imagining* system — and for valuing dreamwork (which engages mPFC/vmPFC) over pure rational analysis (DLPFC). The thalamus, oscillating at ~40 Hz gamma, is the relay synchronizing these systems.

## Why It Matters

- Gives each [[Ten Core Qualities of Dreams|core quality]] a rough neural address (e.g. symbolization → imaginative brain; the fear-corrective warning dream → amygdala; the reassuring resource dream → reward system in the basal forebrain).
- Grounds [[Cybernetic Dream Theory]] in real tissue: the "self-optimizing network" is not a metaphor floating free of the brain but a claim about interacting neuromodulatory systems.
- Explains why [[Supramodal Space|supramodal]] dreamwork (body + image + emotion + movement) can reach material that verbal, DLPFC-driven talk cannot: it engages the very zones dreaming runs on.

## Caveat

Vedfelt himself flags the model as a *didactic simplification* — the zones are drawn on one plane though they overlap in three dimensions, and the color scheme is a teaching device, not a brain atlas. Treat as orientation, not neuroanatomy.
