# Hilo AI Consulting Offer

Three fixed-price packages sized for Big Island small businesses, followed by the competitive landscape, funding that can offset costs, and a startup checklist. Who to sell to: [[Big-Island-AI-Prospect-List]]. How to sell: [[Hilo-AI-Consulting-Outreach-Playbook]]. Parent: [[business]].

## The ladder

| | 1. AI Quick Wins Audit | 2. Implementation Sprint | 3. AI Care Retainer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | **$495** flat | **$2,500** per workflow (2 workflows: $4,500) | **$450/mo** (Pro: $900/mo) |
| **Time to deliver** | 5 business days | 10 business days | Ongoing; 3-month minimum, then month-to-month |
| **Your hours** | ~7 (drops to ~5 once templated) | ~20–25 (drops to ~12–15 on a repeat vertical) | ~3/mo (Pro ~6/mo) |
| **Effective rate** | ~$70–100/hr | ~$100–200/hr | ~$150/hr |
| **Role** | Paid qualification + one visible win | The revenue engine | Recurring revenue + stickiness |

All prices are **plus Hawaiʻi GET**, shown as a separate line (see Legal/admin). Third-party tool costs go on the client's own accounts at cost, typically $20–150/mo. You carry no software COGS and the client owns everything.

### 1. AI Quick Wins Audit: $495

- **Scope:** a 90-minute on-site walkthrough of the workflows that eat the most staff time: inbound inquiries, bookings, reviews, quotes, listings, intake, reminders, and email. Review of their existing tools (FareHarbor, Google Business Profile, Gmail/Outlook, Square/Toast/Shopify, PMS such as Streamline/Escapia/AppFolio).
- **Deliverables:**
  1. Ranked list of 5–10 automation opportunities, each with estimated hours/month saved, tool cost, and risk.
  2. **One quick win installed during the audit.** Examples: a review-reply prompt pack in their voice, an FAQ auto-reply, an email template set.
  3. A one-page 90-day roadmap.
  4. A one-page staff AI-use policy (what data never goes into AI tools).
- **Credit:** 100% of the fee is credited to a Sprint signed within 30 days.
- **Optional founding-client rate:** $295 for the first 3 clients in exchange for a written testimonial and permission to use them as a named case study.
- **Margin logic:** this is a paid discovery call. It filters out tire-kickers and pays for your drive time. The installed quick win creates proof inside their business, and the credit pushes toward the Sprint. Target audit-to-Sprint conversion: 40–50%.

### 2. Implementation Sprint: $2,500 per workflow

- **Scope:** one workflow built end-to-end inside the client's existing tools, with a human approval step wherever output reaches a customer. Typical builds by vertical:
  - Tour operators: inquiry auto-answers + review-reply drafts + Japanese tour pages.
  - Property managers: maintenance-request intake → triage → routed work order, plus listing copy.
  - Vets/clinics: request → confirmation → reminder sequence (no PHI in v1).
  - Trades: estimate-form acknowledgement + quote follow-up.
  - Producers: product-copy and Japanese gift-page set + customer-email triage.
- **Deliverables:** working automation, a 1-hour staff training session, a written SOP (how it works, how to turn it off), a credentials/data map, and 30 days of fix-it support.
- **Two-workflow bundle:** $4,500. Bilingual English/Japanese output counts as part of a workflow, not a separate one.
- **Payment:** 50% to start, 50% at go-live.
- **Margin logic:** the first build in a vertical takes ~25 hrs (~$100/hr). The second tour operator or property manager reuses ~60% of it (~12–15 hrs, $165–210/hr). Productize by vertical and the rate climbs with every client. With zero COGS, the price is almost all labor.

### 3. AI Care Retainer: $450/mo (Pro $900/mo)

- **Essentials ($450):** monitoring and fixes for everything built, a monthly tune-up (prompts, FAQs, seasonal changes), up to 2 hrs of new small improvements, a one-page monthly report (hours saved, volume handled), and staff Q&A by text/email.
- **Pro ($900):** Essentials plus up to 6 hrs/mo. Includes done-for-you content such as weekly review-reply batches, monthly newsletter/listing refresh, and Japanese-language updates.
- **Margin logic:** ~3 hrs/mo for $450 is ~$150/hr. Ten Essentials clients is $4,500 MRR for ~30 hrs/month. This is the income floor that frees time for trading and building.

### Add-on: Staff "AI at Work" session: $350 flat

A 2-hour on-site session for up to 12 staff: safe use, the client's own policy, and hands-on practice with their real tasks. It's a low-friction first sale for nonprofits and offices. If you become an ETF-approved provider, employers can recover 50% (see Funding).

**Big Island adjustment:**
- **Income:** Hawaiʻi County median household income is ~$78.6k (ACS 2024) vs. ~$100.4k statewide. Price at or slightly below Honolulu.
- **Audit:** $495 matches the one local published audit price (SuperLogicAI, $489). In-person delivery and an installed quick win make it better value, not a reason to charge more yet.
- **Sprint:** $2,500 sits at the bottom of the US small-project range and ~⅓ of SuperLogicAI's build floor. Scope is one workflow, not a platform, which fits 5–30-employee businesses that buy on trust.
- **Retainer:** $450 is about half of SuperLogicAI's $899 and inside Bushe's range, low enough to say yes to without a board.
- **When to raise prices:** after 3 testimonials, or when the calendar is >70% booked. Move Sprint to $3,500 and Essentials to $600.

## Legal / admin checklist (Hawaiʻi)

- [ ] **Entity.** Start as a sole proprietor (fastest), or form an LLC through Hawaii Business Express/DCCA BREG: Articles of Organization $50 + $1 archive fee (+$25 expedite). LLC annual report $15 paper / $12.50 online, due by the end of the registration-anniversary quarter. Name reservation $10. Trade name (DBA) Form T-1: $50, renew every 5 years.
- [ ] **GET license.** Form BB-1 via Hawaii Tax Online, $20 one-time. Hawaiʻi County rate is **4.5%** (4% state + 0.5% county surcharge through 2030), charged on gross receipts including consulting. You may visibly pass it on up to **4.712%**: always invoice it as a separate line so it doesn't eat margin. File periodic G-45 returns and an annual G-49.
- [ ] **No general state business license** for sole-proprietor consulting, and consulting isn't a DCCA-regulated profession. Hawaiʻi County has had a "Business and Occupational License" application (Dept. of Finance, Hilo; form dated 2012). Call Finance to confirm it doesn't apply to home-based consulting.
- [ ] **EIN** (free, IRS) if forming an LLC or hiring. Open a separate business bank account and set up invoicing (Stripe/Square) with a GET line.
- [ ] **Insurance.** Tech E&O/professional liability runs ~$785–$900/yr at $1M/$1M for IT consultants (Insureon/TechInsurance figures). Add third-party cyber (bundled is cheaper), plus general liability for on-site work. E&O is claims-made, so set the retroactive date at first coverage and never let it lapse.
- [ ] **Hawaii Compliance Express** certificate: only needed for government/HTDC contracts.
- [ ] **Contract stack:** one Master Services Agreement plus a short SOW per job (outline below).

### MSA + SOW outline

1. **Parties and term.** MSA has an indefinite term; each SOW has its own dates.
2. **Scope and deliverables.** A numbered list per SOW, plus an explicit **exclusions** list. Change requests are quoted separately.
3. **Acceptance.** Client signs off within 5 business days of delivery or the deliverable is deemed accepted.
4. **Fees and payment.** Audit 100% upfront. Sprint 50/50. Retainer monthly in advance by card/ACH. GET shown separately. Late fee after 15 days.
5. **Client accounts and third-party tools.** Client owns all accounts and pays vendors directly. Consultant has delegated access only. Access is revoked at the end.
6. **Data handling.** Minimum necessary data. No PHI, SSNs, or payment data in any AI tool unless a BAA-covered configuration is documented in the SOW. Credentials live in the client's password manager. Client data is deleted or returned at the end.
7. **AI output.** Drafts require human review before reaching customers. No legal, medical, tax, or financial advice. Vendor model behavior is outside the consultant's control.
8. **IP.** Client owns deliverables built for them. Consultant keeps pre-existing templates, prompts, and know-how, and licenses them to the client.
9. **Confidentiality**, mutual.
10. **Limitation of liability.** Capped at fees paid under the SOW; no consequential damages.
11. **Termination.** Retainer: 30 days' notice. Sprint: pay for work done.
12. **Governing law:** Hawaiʻi. Signatures.

### Data-privacy cautions

- **HRS 487N (breach notification)** covers any business that owns, licenses, *or maintains* personal information of Hawaiʻi residents. If you hold client data and it's breached, you must notify the data owner immediately. Penalties reported up to $2,500 per violation. Also check HRS 487R (record destruction) and 487J (SSN protection). Design so you hold as little client data as possible.
- **HIPAA (dental, chiro, medical, PT):**
  - If you touch PHI you are a **business associate** and must sign a BAA and meet Security Rule safeguards.
  - Consumer ChatGPT/Claude plans are **not** BAA-covered. BAA routes exist for Anthropic's API/Enterprise (owner action required), OpenAI API (zero data retention)/Enterprise/Healthcare, and Azure OpenAI, Vertex AI, and Bedrock.
  - **Rule for the first 90 days:** medical clients get non-PHI work only (reviews, marketing, staff policy, reminders that run inside their existing HIPAA-compliant practice software).
- **Veterinary** practices are not HIPAA-covered entities. This is why Aloha Veterinary Center ranks above the dental and chiro prospects.
- **Law firms:** attorney confidentiality duties are the lawyer's. Use business-tier tools with no training on inputs, keep v1 to intake *structure* rather than privileged content, and let the attorney approve the setup.
- **Japanese/visitor data:** booking platforms hold guest PII. Automations should read from and write to the platform rather than copy guest lists into AI tools.
