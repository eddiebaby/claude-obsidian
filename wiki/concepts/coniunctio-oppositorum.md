# coniunctio oppositorum

> **Latin:** *coniunctio* ("union, joining, marriage") + *oppositorum* ("of opposites") = **"the union of opposites."**

---

## Definition

The specific alchemical name for the operation that joins two opposed principles into a third — the same event the vault's general [[Coniunctio]] page treats at length (Sol/Luna, Rex/Regina). This page exists because [[ajuonuma2023-alchemical-oedipus]] uses the exact Latin phrase *coniunctio oppositorum* to name a particular, morally-charged instance of the union: an **incestuous** one, and treats that specific charge (union that is simultaneously punishable) as distinct enough to warrant its own record, cross-linked from the general [[Coniunctio]] page.

## How This Source Uses It

In the *[[Visio Arislei|Visio Arislei]]*, Gabricus is dissolved into his sister Beya's womb during their intercourse — his death is called by Jung "punishment for the incestuous ***coniunctio oppositorum*** [union of opposites]" with his sister (Jung 1944, para. 436). Ajuonuma extends the term to the Oedipus myth: Oedipus's self-blinding is read as the "dreadful consequence of his own incest in the womb of his father-mother," whom Ajuonuma, in the paper's most speculative move, identifies with Beya (via the androgyny/consubstantiality argument — see [[filius-regius]] and the source page).

The specific weight of *oppositorum* here is not simply "opposites" in the abstract Sol/Luna sense but **opposites that should not, by ordinary moral law, unite** — sibling with sibling, son with mother. The *coniunctio* is still generative (it produces the *filius regius*/Oedipus-as-Self), but it is a *coniunctio* purchased at the price of a "crime against nature," which is also why both Gabricus's product and Oedipus himself are subsequently expelled as *[[Pharmakon|pharmakos]]*.

## Relation to coincidentia oppositorum

Distinct from [[coincidentia-oppositorum]] (Nicholas of Cusa's "coincidence of opposites," the structural definition of Mercurius and the Self as the point where contraries are simultaneously true). *Coniunctio oppositorum* names the **operation** — the act of joining; *coincidentia oppositorum* names the **state** that results, or that a paradoxical figure like Mercurius already occupies without needing an operation to produce it. In the Visio Arislei/Oedipus material, the *coniunctio oppositorum* (the incestuous union/killing) is the event that *produces* a figure (the *filius regius*/Oedipus-as-Self) whose nature can then be described as *coincidentia oppositorum*.
