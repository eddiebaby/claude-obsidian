# Dream Recall

## Definition

The practice and science of remembering dreams long enough to record them. Recall is a neurochemically-bounded race against forgetting: capture must happen within seconds of waking.

## Why Dreams Vanish

During REM, **norepinephrine** (essential for stabilizing new memories) drops to nearly zero while acetylcholine is high, and the hippocampus is not fully online at waking. Dreams sit in fragile short-term memory and are not transferred to long-term storage. Rule of thumb: **~50% of a dream's content is lost within 5 minutes of waking, ~90% within 10.**

## Techniques (evidence-backed)

- **Pre-sleep intention** — rehearse "I will remember my dreams" at sleep onset (the basis of MILD, Mnemonic Induction of Lucid Dreams).
- **Stillness on waking** — keep eyes closed, don't move; recall *before* engaging the forward-thinking waking mind. Replay the dream backward from the last scene.
- **Immediate capture** — voice-first is ideal (eyes closed, motionless); transcribe and structure later. Record **fragments and feelings** even when the narrative is gone — a single image often unlocks more.
- **Consistency** — regular journaling raises recall ~30–50% within 1–2 weeks; log "no recall" nights too (they still build the habit).
- **WBTB (Wake-Back-to-Bed)** — wake after ~4.5–6 h, stay up 15–60 min, return to sleep to exploit REM-rich late sleep. REM recall averages ~82% vs ~43% for NREM (Nielsen 2000).
- **Substances** — THC and alcohol suppress REM and reduce recall (both cause REM-rebound on withdrawal); caffeine disrupts continuity. Worth logging to see your own pattern.
- **B6 (preliminary)** — one sizeable double-blind trial (Aspy et al., 2018) found 240 mg before bed increased recalled dream *content* by ~64% vs placebo (no effect on vividness). Treat as preliminary, with a medical disclaimer.

## Why It Matters

No capture, no material. For Scott's [[dreams-index|journal]] and the [[jung-dream-app-design|app]], this is the MVP constraint: the capture surface must be reachable and usable within the golden first minutes, before movement.
