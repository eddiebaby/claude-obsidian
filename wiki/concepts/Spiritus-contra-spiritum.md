# Spiritus contra spiritum

**Latin: "spirit against spirit."** Jung's formula from his 1961 letter to **Bill Wilson**, co-founder of Alcoholics Anonymous — a discernment ([[Discernment of Spirits]]) between two meanings of *spiritus*: the destructive "spirit" of alcohol and the higher "Spirit" of transformative religious experience. Only spirit can counter spirit. Highlighted by [[Eckhard Frick|Frick]] ([[frick2025-spiritual-path-transformation|2025]]) as his best single example of spiritual transformation.

## The Formula

*Spiritus* in Latin carries both senses — distilled liquor (as in "spirits") *and* the Holy Spirit. Jung's insight to Wilson: the craving for alcohol is, at a low level, a spiritual thirst — "the equivalent, on a low level, of the spiritual thirst of our being for wholeness." The cure therefore cannot be merely medical or moral; it must be a higher *spiritus* set against the lower one. This letter is widely credited as an intellectual seed of the AA program's spiritual dimension — the "power greater than ourselves" and the "spiritual awakening" of the twelfth step.

## Frick's Reading

- AA spirituality is "the best studied of all Jungian 'therapies'" — despite a critical Cochrane review (Kelly et al. 2020), the 12-step movement is the most-researched Jungian-influenced self-help practice.
- It is also a **caution against accelerated spirituality** — the "hungry ghosts" of the psychedelic renaissance. Frick notes the irony that Bill Wilson himself later experimented with LSD and wrote Jung a "secret" letter about treating alcoholics with it; Jung died before he could reply.
- Frick includes three anonymized AA members' accounts of what "spirituality" and "a power greater than ourselves" mean to them **without a fixed creed** — spirituality in the health-care sense does not presuppose religious affiliation. (One: "God starts where I stop thinking." Another: letting go of a punishing childhood God so "a space can open up… something completely new can emerge.")

## Why It Matters Here

*Spiritus contra spiritum* is the concrete, constructive pole of [[Discernment of Spirits]]: not spiritual bypassing, neglect, or inflation, but a discernment that channels a destructive drive into a transformative one. It also exemplifies [[Faith in O]] and [[Createdness]] — the alcoholic's recovery turns on ceding the ego's claim to self-mastery ("we admitted we were powerless") and accepting a power greater than the ego, i.e. the unconscious's / the Self's priority.
