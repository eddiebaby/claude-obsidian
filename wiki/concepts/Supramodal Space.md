# Supramodal Space

## Definition

[[Ole Vedfelt|Vedfelt's]] term (coined 2001) for the multi-modal field of dreamwork in which **all experiential modalities are given equal attention** — visual imagery, bodily sensation, movement, emotion, and thought — rather than privileging verbal-visual interpretation. Working "supramodally" means tracking the flow of energy *across* modalities: an emotion evokes an image, which elicits an involuntary movement (e.g. a facial expression), felt as an inner bodily sensation, which connects to a thought or unfolds an autobiographical memory.

## The Method

Attending to this cross-modal flow "circumscribes the dream's entire experiential spectrum" and helps the therapist "navigate past defences that should not be provoked." It realizes **Core Quality 9** (dreams are experiences of wholeness) in practice: dreams already fuse all modalities, so the dreamwork should too. The neural correlate Vedfelt points to is the vmPFC as a **convergence-divergence zone** and "overriding regulator of a somatic marker system" (Damasio's "gut feeling / felt sense" behind intuitive decisions).

Worked example (Dorothy): she drew her recurrent "extra room" dream — a staircase to an attic with a magnificent view. The dreamwork circumambulated the image across *all* modalities; the visual corridor evoked a bodily feeling of powerlessness, she stayed with the feeling (rather than willing it away), and it transformed — "light streamed between the tiles… her feeling shifted to quiet joy." The rule she extracted: stay with the felt state until it transforms *by inner power, not by will*.

## Why It Matters

- It is the **clinical enactment** of [[Cybernetic Dream Theory]]: engaging multiple [[Fourfold Brain|brain zones]] at once is how you get the "requisite variety" to reorganize the network, which pure verbal analysis (DLPFC alone) cannot.
- Extends [[Active Imagination]] from a mainly visual-dialogic method to a full-body, multi-sensory one — a bridge between Jung's technique and somatic/experiential therapies.
- Design cue for the individuation app: dream capture and reflection should prompt across modalities (what did you *see, feel in the body, do, feel emotionally, think*?), not just narrative text — the richer the modal capture, the more of the dream's "wholeness" is available to work with.
