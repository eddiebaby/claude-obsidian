# Amplification

## Definition

Jung's core method for working a dream image: stay with the image and enrich it with associations, rather than drift away from it. Jung called the move *circumambulation* — "I work all around the dream picture and disregard every attempt that the dreamer makes to break away from it." It is the deliberate opposite of looking a meaning up in a symbol dictionary.

## How It Works

Three layers, strictly in order:

1. **Personal** — the dreamer's own memories and associations to the image. *"What comes to mind about X — and what* else *about X?"* Always first.
2. **Cultural** — what the image means in the dreamer's own culture.
3. **Archetypal** — mythic, religious, folkloric parallels. Used *only* when personal associations are exhausted yet the image still carries energy ("charge").

Contrast with **Freudian free association**, which chains away from the image ("what comes to mind, and then? and then?") until it drifts to a complex. Amplification repeatedly returns to the same image.

Jung's name for the first move is **"taking up the context"** (CW 8, *On the Nature of Dreams*, ¶542): "making sure that every shade of meaning which each salient feature of the dream has *for the dreamer* is determined by the associations of the dreamer himself." He proceeds "in the same way as I would in deciphering a difficult text." The examination of context is "a simple, almost mechanical piece of work which has only a preparatory significance" (¶543); the readable interpretation that follows is the exacting part, demanding *"intelligence du cœur."* The governing rule: start from "I have no idea what this dream means" (¶533), and avoid all "stereotyped interpretation of dream-motifs" (¶543).

## Why It Matters

This is the anti-dictionary safeguard. Jung: "No dream symbol can be separated from the individual who dreams it"; there is "no stereotyped explanation." A snake is fear for one dreamer, transformation for another. [[James Hillman]] sharpens the caution: "The moment you've defined the snake, interpreted it, you've lost the snake… The task of analysis is to keep the snake there." Any archetypal material is *amplificatory possibility*, explicitly subordinate to the dreamer's own associations — never a verdict.

## Examples

- In [[2026-06-25-montana-inheritance]], the snake-equivalent move: the *house* is amplified through Scott's own associations (can't finish it; Jason completes it) before any "house = Self" archetypal reading is offered.
- von Franz's encodable tip: write the dream on one half of the page, the spontaneous associations to each element opposite it.
