# Aqua Permanens (Divine Water)

The *aqua permanens* (also *aqua divina*, *aqua nostra*, *aqua vitae*, *humidum radicale*) is the central alchemical symbol and the pivot of Jung's commentary on the Visions of Zosimos (CW 13, pars. 89–117). It is at once a material substance the alchemists sought to extract, a cosmic principle, and a symbol of the *anima mundi* — the soul of the world imprisoned in matter and released through suffering. Its defining characteristic: it is a paradox. Water that is fire. Spirit that is liquid. A substance that kills and vivifies simultaneously.

Primary source: [[jung-cw13-alchemical-studies]] Essay II (pars. 89–117).

---

## What It Is

The *aqua permanens* cannot be reduced to a single definition. The alchemical texts approach it through accumulated equivalences:

| Latin term | Meaning | Relationship |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| *aqua permanens* | permanent/enduring water | the substance itself |
| *aqua divina* | divine water | its cosmological status |
| *humidum radicale* | radical moisture | the substrate of all matter |
| *anima media natura* | soul of middle nature | standing between spirit and matter |
| *anima aquina* | water-soul | the soul imprisoned in water |
| *anima mundi* | world-soul | released form of the same |
| *rotundum* | round element | its spherical/omega aspect |

These are not synonyms — they are facets. The *aqua permanens* is the one thing that has all these names because it is the *prima materia* in its transformative aspect.

> [!key-insight] The Central Paradox
> "The spirit which is a water, or the water which is a spirit, is essentially a paradox, a pair of opposites like water and fire. In the *aqua nostra* of the alchemists, the concepts of water, fire, and spirit coalesce." (par. 98)

---

## How It Is Obtained: Torment as Method

The *aqua permanens* is extracted from the *prima materia* through the torment of fire. This is why the alchemical process requires suffering. The Zosimos visions dramatize it as sacrificial ritual:

- Ion is dismembered, burned, his skin drawn off, his bones mingled with flesh
- "till I perceived by the transformation of the body that I had become spirit"
- The boiling and burning of the people in the bowl = the *separatio*

"The genuineness or incorruptibility of the stone is proved by the torment of fire and cannot be attained without it. This *leitmotiv* runs all through alchemy." (par. 94)

The *separatio* was often represented as the dismemberment of a human body. The *aqua permanens* dissolves bodies into four elements — this is the first stage of purification. Of it was said: it transformed the *nigredo* into the *albedo* through the miraculous "washing" (*ablutio*); it animated inert matter; it made the dead rise again; it therefore "possessed the virtue of the baptismal water in the ecclesiastical rite" (par. 89).

## The Osiris Connection (par. 97)

The divine water connects to Osiris at a deep structural level:

- The **Nile** = the dismembered body of Osiris (fourteen parts)
- **Set (Typhon)** covered the coffin of Osiris with **lead**
- Lead = "the sealed tomb of Osiris, containing all the limbs of the god"
- The water possessed the power of resuscitation — for it was Osiris, who rose from the dead
- In the "Dictionary of Goldmaking": "Osiris is the name for lead and sulphur, both of which are synonyms for the arcane substance"

This identifies the *aqua permanens* with Osiris himself: the god who was killed, dismembered, and resurrected. The alchemist's work recapitulates the Osiris myth. Water = the dispersed body of the god = the imprisoned soul = the arcane substance. Lead → water → spirit = tomb → dissolution → resurrection.

## The Rotundum: Water as World-Soul

The *aqua permanens* is also identified with the *rotundum* — the "round element" or omega element (par. 101):

Zosimos: "It consists of two parts. It belongs to the seventh zone, that of Kronos. In the language of the corporeal it is named Okeanos, the origin and seed of all the gods." Outwardly water; inwardly the arcanum.

Steeb's interpretation: "The brooding of the Holy Spirit upon the waters above the firmament brought forth a power which permeates all things in the most subtle way, warms them, and, in conjunction with the light, generates in the mineral kingdom the mercurial serpent, in the plant kingdom the blessed greenness, and in the animal kingdom the formative power; so that the supracelestial spirit of the waters... may fitly be called the soul of the world." When the celestial waters were animated by the spirit, they "immediately fell into a circular motion, from which arose the perfect spherical form of the *anima mundi*."

The rotundum is therefore "a bit of the world soul" — the *aqua permanens* in its most concentrated, most spherical form. This is probably the secret that Zosimos guarded (par. 102).

## Water = Spirit: The Alchemical Tradition

The identification of water with spirit is systematic across the tradition:

- *Rosarium philosophorum*: "Water is spirit"
- *Aurora consurgens*: "Send forth thy Spirit, that is water, and thou wilt renew the face of the earth"
- Arnaldus de Villanova: "They have called water spirit, and it is in truth spirit"
- Apollonius (*Turba*): "God will give it back its soul and its spirit, and the infirmity being removed, that thing will be stronger and better after its destruction, even as a man becomes stronger and younger after the resurrection"
- The steam arising from boiling water = the first visible impression of *metasomatosis* — the transformation of the corporeal into the incorporeal

This identification has its roots in the Hermetic creation narrative: "There was darkness in the deep and water without form; and there was a subtle breath, intelligent, which permeated the things in Chaos with divine power" (*Corpus Hermeticum*, par. 104). The spirit broods over the waters and impregnates them — which is why water *is* spirit.

## "Nature Applied to Nature Transforms Nature"

The great formulation of the alchemical law from Zosimos (III, i, 4):

> "The brazen man gives and the hydrolith receives; the metal gives and the plant receives; the stars give and the flowers receive; the heavens give and the earth receives... all things are woven together and all things are undone again; all things are mingled together and all things combine; and all things unite and all things separate; all things are moistened and all things are dried; and all things flourish and all things fade in the bowl of the altar. For each thing comes to pass with method and in fixed measure and by exact weighing of the four elements. The weaving together of all things and the undoing of all things and the whole fabric of things cannot come to pass without method... **For nature applied to nature transforms nature.** Such is the order of natural law throughout the whole cosmos, and thus all things hang together."

The *aqua permanens* is not a single substance but the principle of transformation itself — the *sympatheia* of all things in their mutual giving and receiving.

## Psychological Dimension

Jung reads the *aqua permanens* as the symbol of the unconscious in its most active, self-transforming aspect:

- The *humidum radicale* = the unconscious as the root-moisture of the personality
- The *anima aquina* = the anima *as* imprisoned unconscious content, awaiting liberation through the opus
- The ablutio (washing) = the dissolving action of the unconscious on hardened ego-attitudes
- The transformation of nigredo to albedo = the first lightening of dark, depression-saturated states through confrontation with unconscious material

The Isis narrative (pars. 99–101) shows this clearly: the angel Amnael comes with a vessel filled with "shining water" and reveals the secret. The angel represents "personified transmitters of unconscious contents" (par. 108). The alchemist receives the arcane substance the same way a dreamer receives a transformative dream: through a figure from the other side.

## The Deus Absconditus (par. 138)

Jung's final definition of what the *aqua permanens* ultimately means:

> "The divine water or its symbol, the uroboros, means nothing other than the *deus absconditus*, the god hidden in matter, the divine Nous that came down to Physis and was lost in her embrace. This mystery of the 'god become physical' underlies not only classical alchemy but also many other spiritual manifestations of Hellenistic syncretism."

The *aqua permanens* is not merely a symbol of the unconscious — it is the symbol of the **divine imprisoned in matter**. The divine Nous of the Hermetic creation myth descended into Physis (Nature) and became entangled in it. This entanglement is simultaneously a fall and a hidden presence: the god is lost, but also everywhere. The alchemists' labor was to *find* the lost god — to extract it from lead, from water, from the very substance of the world.

This is why the *aqua permanens* kills and vivifies, burns and purifies, dissolves and resurrects: it IS the divine process of loss-and-return, the death and rebirth of the god. The Osiris connection (see §"The Osiris Connection") is the same archetype in Egyptian dress: the dismembered god distributed through nature, to be reconstituted by the alchemical opus.

The *deus absconditus* formula connects the *aqua permanens* directly to the lapis: the stone, the water, and the hidden god are three faces of the same archetype — the Self imprisoned in matter, awaiting liberation through the *opus*. See [[Lapis-Philosophorum]].
