# ipso facto

Latin: "by the fact itself" / "by that very fact." A legal and philosophical term of art indicating that a consequence follows automatically and necessarily from a given state of affairs, without need for further proof, intervening action, or intent.

Context: [[JOAP-68-1-Charura-Bushell-Duoethnographic-Colonialism]] (*JAP* 68/1, 2023).

---

## Definition

*Ipso facto* asserts a structural or logical inevitability: X is the case simply *because* Y is the case, with no further mechanism required. It is distinguished from causal claims that require an intervening process — the relationship is definitional/constitutive rather than mechanistic.

---

## Use in the Source

Charura & Bushell write of the consulting room: "our contention is that, in so as far as the client is seen and experienced as an 'other', then, *ipso facto* the colonizing archetype is prone to constellate in the dynamic of 'other (colonized) – colonizer'."

The word is load-bearing for the paper's argument. The authors are not claiming that therapists who colonize their clients *intend* to do so, or that colonizing only happens through failures of technique. They are claiming something stronger and more uncomfortable: the mere structural fact of experiencing the client as an "other" is, *by that fact alone*, sufficient to constellate the [[Colonizer Archetype]] in the therapeutic dyad. This removes the possibility of exemption by good intention — the archetype's potential activation is built into the relational structure of "otherness" itself, not into any particular therapist's failing. It is the theoretical basis for the paper's insistence that analysts must actively watch for colonizing dynamics in *every* clinical encounter with a client experienced as different, not just in cases of overt bias.
