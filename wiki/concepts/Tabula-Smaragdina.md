# Tabula Smaragdina

**Tabula smaragdina** ("Emerald Tablet") is the most famous short text of Western alchemy — thirteen aphorisms attributed to Hermes Trismegistus, transmitted through Arabic sources (c. 7th–8th century) and later Latinized. Jung reads it primarily for its statement of the circular movement of [[Mercurius]] as *filius macrocosmi*: an ascent that returns to earth rather than remaining above.

Primary CW 13 source: [[jung-cw13-alchemical-studies]] Essay IV §8, par. 280.

---

## The Text and Its Origin

The *Tabula smaragdina* survives in several versions, the most influential being the Latin text via the Book of the Secret of Creation (8th–9th century). The alchemical tradition attributed it to Hermes Trismegistus and treated it as the foundational statement of the hermetic art. Its first words: "It is true, without a lie, certain and most true."

The most psychologically significant passage for Jung (par. 280):

> "He ascends from earth to heaven and **descends again to earth**, and receives the power of Above and Below. His power is complete when he has returned to earth."

---

## The Circular Return

The key phrase is the **return to earth**. This defines Mercurius's movement as fundamentally circular and descending — in direct contrast to the Christian Redeemer's movement, which is linear and ascending:

| Figure | Movement | Terminus |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Christ (*filius microcosmi*) | Descends from Above → redeems → returns Above | Heaven |
| Mercurius (*filius macrocosmi*) | Rises from Below → ascends to Heaven → **returns to earth** | Earth |

The *Tabula smaragdina*'s Hermes receives the "power of Above and Below" precisely *by returning to earth* — the circular movement is what completes and integrates both powers. He is a *circulus simplex* ("I am One and at the same time Many in myself").

Mercurius says: "My power is complete when I have returned to earth." This is the opposite of the Christian soteriology, where the Redeemer's power is complete in glory above.

---

## Psychological Reading

The circular return has a specific psychological meaning. The individuation process is not a linear ascent toward spiritual heights — it is a spiral that returns to the earth, the body, the concrete. The alchemical *opus* completes itself not in transcendence but in the grounding of transformed consciousness back in ordinary life.

"The salt" (par. 280): Mercurius's centre in man is earth = "salt" (Christ: "Ye are the salt of the earth"). The completed opus produces not a spiritually floating adept but someone whose higher capacities are *salted* — preserved, concrete, embodied in the earthly.

The *filius macrocosmi* (see [[Filius-Macrocosmi]]) is the counterpart to Christ as *filius microcosmi*: where Christ saves the soul by lifting it above, Mercurius-as-*filius macrocosmi* transforms matter by descending into it and returning with both powers united.

---

## The Hermes Lineage

The *Tabula smaragdina*'s "He ascends and descends" places Mercurius in the lineage of Hermetic *hodēgos* — guide, pointer of the way. Michael Maier (par. 278) identified Hermes explicitly as **ὁδηγός** who points toward paradise. The Erythraean Sibyl said of him: "He will make you a witness of the mysteries of God and the secrets of nature." The *Tabula smaragdina* is the condensed statement of what that guidance accomplishes: the integration of Above and Below, the return to earth completed.
