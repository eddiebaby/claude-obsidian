# Hilo AI Consulting Outreach Playbook

Goal: **first paying client within 30 days (by Thu Nov 12, 2026).** Who to approach is in [[Big-Island-AI-Prospect-List]], and what they buy is in [[Hilo-AI-Consulting-Offer]]. Parent: [[business]].

> **Standing rule:** no new research, tooling, or reading until **15 walk-ins are logged**. This page is complete enough to act on. Execution is the bottleneck, not information.

## Scripts

All plain-spoken. No "revolutionize," no "AI-powered," no jargon. Lead with what you noticed about *their* business.

### 30-second in-person pitch

> "Hi, I'm Scott. I live here in Hilo. I help local businesses use AI for the repetitive stuff: answering the same customer questions, replying to reviews, writing listings, following up on quotes. It doesn't replace anybody. It just gives your people a few hours back each week.
>
> I noticed [**one specific thing**: *you've got 3,000+ reviews and replying to them must take forever* / *your site says text or WhatsApp to book* / *the estimate form goes to an office that closes at 4*].
>
> I can show you in 20 minutes, using your own reviews or your own FAQ, what that'd look like. No charge, no pitch deck. If it's not useful, no worries. Would Thursday or Friday morning be better?"

Then stop talking. Leave the one-pager whatever the answer.

### Cold email: Variant A (observation-led)

**Subject:** Quick idea for [Business], from a Hilo neighbor

> Hi [first name, or "there"],
>
> I'm Scott, based in Hilo. I set up simple AI tools for local businesses so staff spend less time on repetitive messages.
>
> I was looking at [Business] and noticed [specific: *a lot of your reviews don't have replies yet* / *guests book by phone, text and WhatsApp*]. I put together a quick example using a few of your public reviews to show what drafting replies in your voice could look like. Takes a staff member about 2 minutes to approve a week's worth.
>
> Happy to show you in 20 minutes, at your place or on the phone. No cost, and if it's not a fit, no hard feelings.
>
> Mahalo,
> Scott
> [business phone] · [booking link]

### Cold email: Variant B (short, peer-to-peer)

**Subject:** 20 minutes, your own [reviews / FAQ / listings]

> Hi [name],
>
> Short one. I'm a Hilo-based programmer helping island businesses use AI for the boring parts: inquiries, reviews, listings, reminders. I'm doing free 20-minute demos this month built from each business's own public info, so it's specific to you, not a sales video.
>
> Would [day] or [day] work? If now's a bad time, just say so and I won't follow up.
>
> Scott · [business phone]

### Follow-ups

**Same day after a walk-in (text or email):**
> "Mahalo for the few minutes today. Here's the one-pager I mentioned [link/PDF]. The 20-minute demo offer stands. Want me to pencil in [day/time]?"

**Follow-up #1 (3–4 days after no reply):**
> "Hi [name], circling back once. I already drafted [3 review replies / your top 5 FAQ answers] from your public info. Happy to send them over, no strings, even if you never hire me. Want them?"

(Giving the drafts away is the move. It's 10 minutes of work and it proves the value.)

**Follow-up #2 (10 days, closing the loop):**
> "Last note from me. I'll assume the timing's not right. If [busy season / tax season / the holidays] makes this more useful later, my number's [ ]. Mahalo, and good luck with [something specific]."

### Objection handling (one line each)

- **"AI isn't for a business like ours."** "Probably not robots. Just the 10 questions you answer every day. Let me show you on yours."
- **"I don't want a robot talking to my customers."** "Agreed. Everything I set up drafts; your person approves. Nothing goes out unseen."
- **"We tried ChatGPT."** "Most people use it like a search box. The win is wiring it into your booking or email so it saves time without anyone opening it."
- **"Too expensive."** "The audit's $495 and you get one fix installed that week. If it doesn't save more than that in a month, don't do anything else."
- **"My nephew does computers."** "Great, I'll work with him. I'm the one who's done this for [tour operators / property managers]."

## Free 20-minute "AI quick wins" demo agenda

**Before the demo (30–45 min prep):** pull their public info, meaning 5 recent reviews, their FAQ/website text, and one listing or product page. Pre-build 3 things:
1. Review replies drafted in their voice.
2. FAQ/inquiry answers, plus one in Japanese if they sell to visitors.
3. One rewrite of a listing or product page, or for trades/property managers, an intake form that turns a messy message into a clean work order.

| Min | Segment | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Their week | "What eats your time? What do customers ask ten times a day? Where do leads go cold?" Write down their words |
| 3–12 | Live demo | Show the 3 pre-builds on their own content. Edit one live from their feedback |
| 12–15 | Where it would live | Name the actual tool it plugs into (FareHarbor, Gmail, Google reviews, Shopify, their PMS). Typical tool cost: $20–150/mo, paid on their account |
| 15–17 | Guardrails | Human approves everything customer-facing. No sensitive data in AI tools. They own all accounts. Easy off-switch |
| 17–19 | The offer | **AI Quick Wins Audit: $495, 5 days, one fix installed, credited 100% toward a Sprint.** (Founding rate $295 for the first 3, in exchange for a testimonial) |
| 19–20 | Next step | "Want to book the walkthrough? I have [two specific slots]." If no: "Who else should see this?" and leave the drafts with them |

## One-page leave-behind (outline)

Print 50 copies. Make it QR-linked to a booking page. Plain design, one page, no stock robots.

1. **Headline:** *A few hours back every week. Practical AI for Big Island businesses, set up by a Hilo neighbor.*
2. **What it handles** (3–4 vertical examples, one line each):
   - Tours/attractions: inquiries, review replies, Japanese-language pages.
   - Rentals/property: maintenance requests, listings, tenant/guest messages.
   - Clinics/vets/offices: appointment requests, reminders, intake.
   - Shops/farms: product descriptions, order emails, holiday campaigns.
3. **How it works:** Free 20-min demo → **$495** Quick Wins Audit (5 days; one fix installed) → Implementation Sprint **from $2,500** → optional care **from $450/mo**.
4. **Guardrails:** you approve everything · your accounts, your data · no long contracts · medical clients get HIPAA-appropriate setups only.
5. **Proof:** blank slot for the first testimonial (fill after client #1).
6. **Contact:** Scott, business phone, business email, QR to booking page.
7. **Footer:** Hilo-based · GET-licensed · insured (add once done).
