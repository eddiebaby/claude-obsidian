# Lower Triad

The chthonic or infernal counterpart to the upper Trinity. Jung's psychological law: whenever an upper triad is established, a corresponding lower triad is constellated in opposition. The lower triad is the structural shadow of the Christian Trinity — not a theological curiosity but a psychodynamic consequence.

Source: [[edinger-aion-lectures]] (Lectures 11-12, pp. 84-86); [[jung-cw9ii-aion]] (pars. 156-158); CW 9i, pars. 423, 426, 430f.

---

## The Structural Principle

> "Threeness denotes polarity, since one triad always presupposes another, just as high presupposes low, lightness darkness, good evil. In terms of energy, polarity means a potential, and wherever a potential exists there is the possibility of a current, a flow of events, for the tension of opposites strives for balance." (CW 9i, par. 426)

When Christian theology crystallized the upper Trinity (Father, Son, Holy Ghost) as the supreme good, it simultaneously drove the shadow into organized infernal opposition. This is not merely a religious problem — it is a psychological one with clinical consequences. The three differentiated functions in typology produce three corresponding unconscious components that constellate as an opposing force.

## Symbolic Manifestations

### Dante's Three-Faced Satan (*Inferno*, Canto 34)

At the lowest point of hell, embedded in ice, Satan appears with three faces:
- Center face: crimson hue
- Right face: yellowish white
- Left face: black as an Ethiopian ("As any Ethiopian's on the Nile")

Six wings like bats generate three separate blasts of icy wind. Six eyes weep simultaneously; three chins drip bloody foam.

> "When I perceived three faces on his head! / The one in front was of a crimson hue, / And to it were the other two attached / Above the very middle of each shoulder... / Beneath each face there sprang two great wings... / Like wings of bats, and he was flapping them / To make three separate blasts of icy wind... / With all six eyes he wept, and from three chins / The tears and bloody foam were trickling down."

This is the explicit symbolic image of the infernal trinity: three-faced as the Trinity is three-personed, but frozen, weeping, generating cold rather than warmth, embedded in ice rather than enthroned in light.

**Dante's three beasts** at the very start of *Inferno* (Canto 1) — leopardess, lion, she-wolf — are the lower triad encountered at the threshold before the descent even begins.

### Nostradamus and the Northern Kings

Jung's analysis of Nostradamus's prophecy (par. 156): the Antichrist "possessing two confederates, one in the East and one in the South. This power corresponds to the 'lower triad.'" Adam Scotus: "a frightful dragon's head in the north from which all evil comes. From its mouth and snout it emitted smoke of a triple nature." (par. 158)

The Antichrist is something infernal — Typhon, Set — with two confederates: a lower triad explicitly opposed to the upper Trinity of Christianity.

The north as the source of both evil *and* the highest divine revelation (Ezekiel's vision came from the north; Lucifer's fall from the north — Isa. 14:12ff; Yahweh's cooking pot of disaster from the north — Jer. 1:13ff). The same directional origin for the most terrible and the most numinous: both come from the unconscious, positive and negative aspects of the same Self.

## The Functional Mechanism: Three-Legged Horse

In "The Phenomenology of the Spirit in Fairytales" (CW 9i, par. 423), Jung analyzes a fairy tale involving a three-legged horse:

A witch originally owned a four-legged horse (= the wholeness of the unconscious). A magician rode it away (consciousness claiming the unconscious content). At the border of the witch's realm, twelve wolves attacked and tore off one hoof. The horse became three-legged.

**The three-leggedness occurs at the border** — precisely when the unconscious content is being claimed by consciousness.

### The Typological Basis (CW 9i, pars. 430f.)

Jung's analysis of the four psychological functions explains the mechanism precisely:

- One function (the *superior*) becomes relatively well differentiated, available to the will
- One or two *auxiliary* functions partially differentiate
- The fourth (*inferior*) function remains held by the unconscious — inaccessible to the will, coming and going on its own volition

When the two auxiliary functions differentiate, they split: one part accompanies the superior function into consciousness; the other part stays connected to the inferior function in the unconscious.

Result: three functions relatively available to consciousness, each with a corresponding unconscious component still rooted in the matrix. The fourth element — the inferior function — is held entirely by the dark mother.

This generates **two triads**: the upper triad of the three partially-differentiated functions, and the lower triad of their three unconscious counterparts. They oppose each other. The inferior function connects the two systems.

> "Just as the three conscious and differentiated parts of these functions are confronted by a fourth, undifferentiated function which acts as a painfully disturbing factor, so also the superior function seems to have its worst enemy in the unconscious." (CW 9i, par. 430f.)

The three-legged horse has exactly this structure: three legs function; the fourth (torn off by wolves, held by the Great Mother) is what links the horse to the unconscious realm it came from.

## The Christian Aeon and Its Shadow

The creation of the upper Trinity — the most elevated, purely positive God-image in Western religious history — drove the shadow into intensified, organized opposition. The two-fish structure of Pisces (Christ and Antichrist, the pair of hostile brothers) is the historical expression of this.

Jung identifies the nodal points where the lower triad erupts historically:
- **1000 AD**: The Cathars, Waldensians, Beghards, Brethren of the Free Spirit — inflationary sects claiming to be God by nature
- **1500 AD**: Science, materialism, the conquest of nature — the Anti-Christian trend
- **1789**: The enthronement of the Goddess Reason in Notre Dame — the explicit inversion

Each eruption is not the defeat of the upper Trinity but the compensation it generates by its one-sidedness.

## Clinical Significance

When the ego aligns exclusively with an upper triad — positive values, spiritual aspirations, goodness — the lower triad constellates as opposition. In dreams this appears as groups of three lowly, animal, or threatening figures. In life it appears as the return of the repressed with organized force.

The path forward is not strengthening the upper triad but recovering the fourth — the inferior function, the hoof held by the wolves. Moving from three to four transforms triad into quaternary and resolves the tension.

> [!key-insight] The triadic symptom points to the missing fourth
> When a patient presents with repeated patterns of three — three failed relationships, three aborted projects, three recurring conflicts — consider the lower triad archetype. Three always points to a missing fourth. The fourth is the inferior function, held in the unconscious by the *horror vacui* of the matrix. Its recovery transforms the compulsion into a quaternity. The question to ask: what is being held by the wolves?
