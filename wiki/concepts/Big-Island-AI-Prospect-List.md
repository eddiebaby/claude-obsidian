# Big Island AI Prospect List

58 real Hawaiʻi Island businesses, Hilo first, scored for how fast an AI engagement pays off. Use with [[Hilo-AI-Consulting-Offer]] (what to sell) and [[Hilo-AI-Consulting-Outreach-Playbook]] (how to approach). Parent: [[business]].

**Data rule:** business-level public info only — name, town, website, public size signals. No owner names, personal phones, personal emails, or home addresses. Keep it that way when updating.

**Verification:** every website below loaded on 2026-10-13 (HTTP 200 / page-title check, or a Cloudflare challenge on a live site). Size signals come from the business's own site or a cited listing. "Use case" is an inference from what the public site shows. Confirm it in conversation.

## Fit score (1–10)

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---|
| Walk-in distance from Hilo (relationship market; in-person converts) | high |
| Owner/manager reachable (not a corporate/off-island decision-maker) | high |
| Volume of repetitive comms visible (bookings, inquiries, reviews, tenants) | high |
| Budget signal (established, multi-product, multi-location) | medium |
| Compliance friction (HIPAA, attorney confidentiality, client PII) | negative |

9–10 = approach this month. 7–8 = approach in first 60 days. 5–6 = nurture / content. ≤4 = dropped.
